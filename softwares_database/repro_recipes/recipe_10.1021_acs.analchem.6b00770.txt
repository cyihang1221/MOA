paper_title: Hydrogen Rearrangement Rules: Computational MS/MS Fragmentation and Structure Elucidation Using MS-FINDER Software
source_paper: Tsugawa H et al. (2016), Analytical Chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.6b00770, PMID: 27419259
overall_reproducibility_score: 78/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Hydrogen rearrangement (HR) rule derivation and validation
    tool: MOPAC2012 (v2012 (PM7 parametrization))
    algorithm: Enthalpy-based ionic stability assessment
    parameters: parametrization=PM7, energy_components=['electronic_energy', 'nuclear_nuclear_repulsion', 'valence_electron_removal', 'heat_of_atomization'], empirical_settings=default element-specific parameters for heat of atomization
    input: ionic substructures in MOL format
    output: stability assessment (heat of formation), HR rule classification (resolved/semiresolved/formula-assigned)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: MS/MS fragment ion assignment using HR rules
    tool: MS-FINDER
    algorithm: Combinatorial bond cleavage with hydrogen rearrangement enumeration (±2 H per cleavage)
    parameters: max_bond_cleavages={'tree_like': 2, 'two_ring_dissociation': 4}, hydrogen_rearrangement_range=[-2, 2], cleavage_priority_order=['first_cleavage_HR_regular', 'first_cleavage_HR_irregular_±2H', 'second_cleavage_HR_regular', 'second_cleavage_precursor_assigned_higher_mz', 'minimum_mass_error'], mass_tolerance_da=0.01, deisotoping_criteria={'isotope_intensity_threshold': 1.5, 'mass_tolerance_da': 'user-defined'}
    input: experimental MS/MS spectrum (m/z, intensity), precursor m/z, adduct type
    output: annotated fragment ions with HR classification and elemental formulas
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Molecular formula generation and ranking
    tool: MS-FINDER
    algorithm: Valence-constrained combinatorial formula enumeration + multi-scoring ranking
    parameters: allowed_elements=['C', 'H', 'N', 'O', 'P', 'S', 'F', 'Cl', 'Br', 'I', 'Si'], valence_constraints={'N': 3, 'O': 2, 'P': 5, 'S': 6}, valence_equations=['OV or SV is even', 'SV >= 2 * (TA - 1)'], hydrogen_carbon_ratio_constraints=empirically derived from metabolome databases (SI Tables S3, S7), formula_scoring_components=['mass_error_score', 'isotopic_ratio_score', 'product_ion_assignment_rate', 'neutral_loss_match_rate', 'database_existence_score'], mass_error_score_function=Gaussian: exp[-0.5*(Δm/δ)^2], isotopic_ratio_score_function=mean of Gaussian scores for M+1 and M+2, neutral_loss_database_size=135, database_score_base=0.5, database_score_increment=0.5 * (num_databases_containing_formula / total_databases)
    input: precursor m/z, adduct type, MS/MS spectrum (for neutral loss & product ion assignment)
    output: ranked list of molecular formulas with total score F ∈ [0,5]
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Structural isomer retrieval and in silico fragmentation
    tool: MS-FINDER
    algorithm: InChIKey-based deduplication + BDE-weighted fragmentation scoring
    parameters: source_databases=14, InChIKey_truncation=14, stereochemistry_handling=merged by first 14 chars of InChIKey, structure_selection_heuristic=PubChem synonym count, in_silico_fragmentation_rules=HR rules + bond dissociation energy (BDE) dictionary, penalty_scores={'HR_unresolved_penalty_Ph': 0.2, 'fragment_linkage_penalty_Pt': 0.5}, BDE_penalty_function=1 - (Bf / Bm), structure_score_normalization=cumulative fragmentation score normalized to 4 + database presence score (0–1), final_ranking_function=T = a*F + b*S, with a=1, b=1
    input: top-ranked molecular formula(s), MS/MS spectrum
    output: ranked list of structural isomers with total score T ∈ [0,5]
    confidence: high

reproducibility_gaps:
  - MOPAC2012 is commercial software requiring license and manual execution — not automatable in open pipeline
  - Ionic substructures in MOL format are 'available on request' — not publicly deposited
  - Exact mass tolerance δ values are user-defined but not reported in methods (e.g., for mass error or isotopic windows)
  - Internal BDE dictionary and neutral loss database (135 entries) are not publicly shared
  - In-house metabolome database curation details (Table S4) are in Supporting Information but not archived or linked
  - No source code or algorithmic implementation details for HR rule engine or combinatorial cleavage enumeration
reproducibility_strengths:
  - MS-FINDER software freely available at http://prime.psc.riken.jp/
  - Validation performed on public resources: MassBank and GNPS databases
  - All HR rules explicitly enumerated and illustrated in Figure 1
  - Full mathematical specification of scoring functions (Gaussian mass error, isotopic ratio, BDE penalty, etc.)
  - Transparent valence constraints and elemental ratio heuristics provided
