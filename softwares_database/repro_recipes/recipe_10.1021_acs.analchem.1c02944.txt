paper_title: Fully Automatized Detection of Phosphocholine-Containing Lipids through an Isotopically Labeled Buffer Modification Workflow.

overall_reproducibility_score: 52/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Chromatographic buffer modification with isotopically labeled acetate
    tool: Custom LC method
    parameters: buffer_A_light=0.1% acetic acid in water, buffer_B_light=0.1% acetic acid in 95% acetonitrile/5% water, buffer_A_heavy=0.1% deuterated acetic acid (CD3COOH) in water, buffer_B_heavy=0.1% deuterated acetic acid in 95% acetonitrile/5% water, gradient_profile=not fully specified, column=C18 reversed-phase, flow_rate=not specified, temperature=not specified, injection_volume=not specified
    input: human plasma samples (pre-processed)
    output: LC-MS raw data with co-eluting [M + CH3COO]−, [M + CD3COO]−, and [M − CH3]− adducts
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Data acquisition on high-resolution mass spectrometer
    tool: Orbitrap-based MS
    parameters: mass_resolution=120,000 (at m/z 200), scan_range_mz=150–1200, polarity=negative mode, automatic_gain_control_target=1e6, maximum_injection_time_ms=100
    input: chromatographic eluate
    output: raw .raw files
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Automated lipid identification using isotopic adduct pattern recognition
    tool: Compound Discoverer (v3.3)
    algorithm: Isotopic pattern matching, adduct grouping, molecular formula assignment
    parameters: adducts_included=['[M + CH3COO]−', '[M + CD3COO]−', '[M − CH3]−'], mass_tolerance_ppm=5, retention_time_window_min=0.5, isotope_pattern_similarity_threshold=not specified, grouping_algorithm=component detection with retention time alignment, filtering_rules=presence of both light/heavy acetate adduct pairs + methyl loss
    input: raw .raw files
    output: annotated lipid feature table with confidence-ranked identifications
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: False-positive reduction and manual validation streamlining
    tool: Compound Discoverer + custom rules (v3.3)
    algorithm: Rule-based filtering using isotopic mass shift (Δm = 3.020 Da for CD3COO− vs CH3COO−) and co-elution criteria
    parameters: required_adduct_pairs=['[M + CH3COO]− and [M + CD3COO]−'], coelution_rt_tolerance_sec=3, minimum_signal_to_noise_ratio=not specified, exclusion_of_non-phosphocholine_lipids=True
    input: Compound Discoverer feature table
    output: curated phosphocholine-containing lipid list
    confidence: low
