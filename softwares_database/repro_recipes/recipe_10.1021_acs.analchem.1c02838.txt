========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Quantum Chemical Prediction of Electron Ionization Mass Spectra of Trimethylsilylated Metabolites.
source_paper: Wang S et al. (2022), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.1c02838, PMID: 35006668
overall_reproducibility_score: 72/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Compound selection and TMS derivatization
    tool: manual curation
    parameters: compound_classes=['organic acids', 'alcohols', 'amides', 'amines', 'thiols'], derivatization_agent=trimethylsilyl (TMS), n_compounds=816
    input: chemical structures (SMILES or IUPAC names)
    output: list of 816 TMS-derivatized compound structures
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Quantum chemical EI mass spectrum prediction
    tool: QCEIMS
    algorithm: quantum chemistry-based fragmentation simulation
    parameters: ionization_mode=electron ionization (EI), energy=70, level_of_theory=DFT/B3LYP/6-31G(d), conformer_generation=systematic search, fragmentation_pathway_sampling=trajectory-based
    input: TMS-derivatized compound structures
    output: in silico EI mass spectra (m/z, intensity)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Experimental spectrum retrieval
    tool: NIST17 Mass Spectral Library (vNIST17)
    parameters: instrument_type=GC-EI-MS, ionization_energy=70, spectral_resolution=unit mass resolution
    input: compound names or CAS numbers of TMS derivatives
    output: experimental EI mass spectra (m/z, intensity)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Spectral similarity assessment
    tool: weighted dot product scoring
    algorithm: weighted dot product (WDP)
    parameters: max_score=1000, mass_tolerance=0.1, intensity_normalization=to base peak, weighting=intensity-weighted
    input: paired in silico and experimental spectra
    output: similarity scores (0–1000)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Class-wise performance analysis
    tool: custom Python/R script
    algorithm: group-wise mean similarity calculation
    parameters: grouping_variable=compound class (aromatic vs. oxygen-containing), statistic=mean weighted dot score
    input: similarity scores + compound annotations
    output: class-averaged similarity scores
    confidence: low
  Step 6: Fragmentation trajectory analysis
    tool: QCEIMS internal trajectory inspection
    algorithm: reaction pathway analysis
    parameters: rearrangement_reactions_included=['McLafferty', 'ortho-quinoid', 'TMS-specific eliminations']
    input: QCEIMS-generated fragmentation trajectories
    output: missed fragmentation patterns (e.g., specific rearrangements)
    confidence: medium
