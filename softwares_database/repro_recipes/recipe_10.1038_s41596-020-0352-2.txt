paper_title: A quantitative thiol reactivity profiling platform to analyze redox and electrophile reactive cysteine proteomes.
source_paper: Fu L et al. (2020), Nature protocols, DOI: 10.1038/s41596-020-0352-2, PMID: 32690958
overall_reproducibility_score: 52/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Thiol labeling with IPM probe
    tool: IPM (2-iodo-N-(prop-2-yn-1-yl)acetamide)
    parameters: ipm_concentration_control_vs_treatment=100 mM, h2o2_concentration=1 mM, h2o2_incubation_time=900, h2o2_incubation_temperature_celsius=37, ipm_labeling_time=not specified, ipm_labeling_temperature_celsius=not specified
    input: cell or tissue lysates (e.g., HEK293T)
    output: IPM-labeled cysteinome (probe-modified proteins)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Proteolytic digestion and click chemistry tagging
    tool: Trypsin + CuAAC (copper-catalyzed azide–alkyne cycloaddition)
    parameters: azido_biotin_reagents=L and H isotopically labeled azido-UV-cleavable-biotin (Az-UV-biotin), labeling_ratio_l_to_h=1:1, scx_cleaning_option=with or without SCX
    input: IPM-labeled proteins
    output: biotinylated, isotope-coded tryptic peptides
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Streptavidin-based enrichment
    tool: Streptavidin beads
    input: biotinylated peptides
    output: enriched biotin-conjugated peptides bound to streptavidin resin
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Photo-cleavage and LC-MS/MS analysis
    tool: UV photolysis + LC-MS/MS
    parameters: uv_cleavage=photo-cleavable linker activated by UV, ms_measurement_time_per_sample_minutes=75
    input: streptavidin-bound biotinylated peptides
    output: LC-MS/MS raw files (.raw)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Peptide identification and quantification
    tool: pFind 3 (v3.x)
    algorithm: database search (human proteome)
    parameters: search_database=human proteome, enzyme=trypsin, missed_cleavages=2, fixed_modifications=['Carbamidomethyl (C)'], variable_modifications=['Oxidation (M)', 'IPM-modified C (Cysteine)'], mass_tolerance_ppm=not specified, fragment_tolerance_da=not specified
    input: LC-MS/MS .raw files
    output: peptide-spectrum matches (PSMs), quantified peptide ratios (H/L, R_10:1)
    confidence: low
  Step 6: Post-processing and reactivity classification
    tool: custom automatic post-processing algorithm
    algorithm: ratio-based thresholding
    parameters: redox_sensitivity_threshold_rh_l=high R_H/L indicates reduced free thiol after H₂O₂ → redox-sensitive, hyperreactivity_threshold_r10_1=2.0, r10_1_definition=ratio of 100 µM IPM vs 10 µM IPM labeling
    input: pFind 3 output (quantified peptides)
    output: list of redox-sensitive or hyperreactive cysteines
    confidence: low
