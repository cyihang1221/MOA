    output: posterior probability distribution over feature-metabolite matches
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Metabolite selection under matching uncertainty
    tool: BAUM
    algorithm: Bayesian variable selection with posterior inclusion probabilities
    parameters: selection_threshold=posterior inclusion probability > 0.5 (default), FDR control=via Bayesian false discovery rate
    input: posterior match probabilities + group labels + feature intensities
    output: list of selected metabolites with uncertainty-weighted effect sizes and posterior probabilities
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Bayesian functional enrichment analysis
    tool: BAUM
    algorithm: Knowledge graph–aware pathway scoring using hierarchical Bayes
    parameters: pathway_prior=derived from ontology/graph structure, aggregation_method=weighted by posterior match probabilities
    input: selected metabolites with posterior match probabilities + pathway database (e.g., KEGG, Reactome)
    output: ranked list of enriched pathways with Bayesian posterior probabilities and credibility intervals
    confidence: medium
