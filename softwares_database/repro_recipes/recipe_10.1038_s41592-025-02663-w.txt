paper_title: Unifying the analysis of bottom-up proteomics data with CHIMERYS.

workflow_steps: 9
  Step 1: In silico protein digestion and decoy generation
    tool: CHIMERYS internal
    algorithm: mimic entrapment generator logic
    parameters: decoy_strategy=shuffled, isobaric_handling=single-representative-per-isobaric-group
    input: FASTA protein database
    output: in silico peptide library with decoys
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Coarse first search for recalibration
    tool: CHIMERYS
    algorithm: fast fragment ion indexing (MSFragger-like)
    parameters: min_matched_ions=3, base_peak_required=True, top3_peak_required=True, isomer_handling=I/L collapsed to one representative
    input: raw MS2 spectra (.raw, .mzML, or vendor format)
    output: high-confidence PSMs for calibration
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Retention time and fragmentation model calibration
    tool: CHIMERYS
    algorithm: linear discriminant analysis (LDA)
    parameters: rt_window_quantile=100, rt_security_factor=2.5, nce_refinement=True, mz_tolerance_recals=True
    input: high-confidence PSMs from coarse search
    output: refined RT model, optimized NCE, recalibrated m/z tolerances
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Main spectrum-centric search with RT filtering
    tool: CHIMERYS
    algorithm: fragment ion indexing + intensity/peak-count scoring
    parameters: rt_tolerance_window=±(100% quantile of |ΔRT|) × 2.5, candidate_filtering=post-scoring based on RT and intensity criteria
    input: raw MS2 spectra + calibrated models
    output: ranked candidate peptides per spectrum
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Chimeric spectrum deconvolution via LASSO regression
    tool: CHIMERYS
    algorithm: non-negative L1-regularized linear regression (LASSO)
    parameters: objective_function=sum((I_exp - I_pred·β)^2) + λ||β||₁, λ_selection=corrected Akaike information criterion (AICc), constraint=β_p ≥ 0, mass_channel_definition=union of matched + unmatched fragment ions within tolerance
    input: predicted spectra matrix (P×M) + experimental spectrum intensities
    output: CHIMERYS coefficients β_p, shadow spectra
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Shadow-spectrum-based re-scoring
    tool: CHIMERYS
    algorithm: intensity-based and counting-based scoring on interference-corrected spectra
    parameters: quality_criteria=['min_shared_abundant_peaks'], shared_ion_handling=distributed via β optimization
    input: shadow spectra + CHIMERYS coefficients
    output: final scored PSMs (target/decoy)
    confidence: medium
  Step 7: PSM-level FDR estimation
    tool: mokapot (vv.0.10.0+ (post-merge with streaming/RAM optimizations))
    algorithm: machine learning–based FDR control (SVM + posterior error probability)
    parameters: training_fdr=0.01, training_subset_size=400000, iterations=10, top_psm_per_scan_retention=False
    input: target/decoy PSM list with scores
    output: q-values, SVM scores, posterior error probabilities
    confidence: high
  Step 8: I/L isomer ambiguity resolution
    tool: CHIMERYS internal
    algorithm: score propagation + flagging
    parameters: isomer_propagation=identical scores assigned to all I/L variants, flag=ambiguous
    input: FDR-filtered PSMs
    output: final PSM table with ambiguity flags
    confidence: high
  Step 9: MS2-based quantification
    tool: CHIMERYS
    algorithm: coefficient-weighted retention time apex estimation + peak width quantile integration borders
    parameters: apex_calculation=CHIMERYS coefficient-weighted mean of RT deltas, integration_borders=99% quantile of peak widths at base (≥3 PSMs at 1% FDR), inclusion_file_handling=fallback RT estimation using external inclusion list
    input: PSMs with coefficients and RTs
    output: precursor-level quantitative features (apex, borders, integrated intensity)
    confidence: medium
