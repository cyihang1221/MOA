sample_description: The study used peripheral blood specimens from 22 sepsis patients and 10 normal human volunteers, all collected from the Emergency Intensive Care Unit (EICU) of the Affiliated Hospital of Southwest Medical University between January 2019 and December 2019. Sepsis patients met the 2016 SCCM/ESICM Sepsis 3.0 diagnostic criteria, were aged 16–75 years, and provided informed consent (or their legal representative did); exclusion criteria included age <16 years, prior organ failure (cardiac, respiratory, hepatic, renal), immune or hematologic disorders, or unwillingness to participate. Normal controls were healthy volunteers meeting the same age and consent criteria. Each specimen was 5 mL of peripheral blood, stored at −80 °C in the hospital’s biospecimen bank. All procedures were approved by the Ethics Committee of the Affiliated Hospital of Southwest Medical University (Approval Number: KY2018029) and conducted per the Declaration of Helsinki.
