========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Improved Characterization of Soil Organic Matter by Integrating FT-ICR MS, Liquid Chromatography Tandem Mass Spectrometry, and Molecular Networking: A Case Study of Root Litter Decay under Drought Conditions.
source_paper: DiDonato N et al. (2024), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.4c00184, PMID: 38991201
overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Broad compositional profiling via direct infusion FT-ICR MS
    tool: FT-ICR MS
    algorithm: peak detection and molecular formula assignment
    parameters: mass_accuracy_ppm=0.1, signal_to_noise_threshold=4, minimum_intensity=10000.0
    input: soil extract in methanol/water (50:50 v/v)
    output: molecular formula table with elemental composition, m/z, intensity, and KMD/DBE
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Targeted compound identification via LC-MS/MS
    tool: LC-MS/MS
    algorithm: library matching (GNPS, mzCloud, MassBank)
    parameters: collision_energy=ramped, precursor_mass_tolerance_da=0.01, fragment_mass_tolerance_da=0.02, min_matched_peaks=3, cosine_score_threshold=0.7
    input: same soil extracts, chromatographically separated
    output: annotated metabolite list with compound names, confidence levels (Level 1–2)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Molecular networking for structural inference of unknowns
    tool: GNPS (Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking) (vv4.1)
    algorithm: cosine similarity-based clustering
    parameters: min_cosine_score=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, max_shift_mz=1.0, network_edge_type=hybrid
    input: LC-MS/MS fragment spectra (mgf format)
    output: molecular network graph with clusters, annotations, and putative structural relationships
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Cross-platform annotation integration
    tool: custom R/Python workflow
    algorithm: molecular formula filtering + network-guided annotation propagation
    parameters: formula_match_tolerance_ppm=1.0, network_propagation_depth=2, annotation_confidence_boost_threshold=0.75
    input: DI-FT-ICR MS molecular formulas + LC-MS/MS annotations + GNPS network clusters
    output: integrated annotation table with enhanced confidence (Level 2→1b), structural hypotheses for unknowns
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Chemical class and redox characterization
    tool: R (enviPat, RMassBank, or custom scripts)
    algorithm: elemental ratio calculation (H/C, O/C, N/C), nominal oxidation state of carbon (NOSC), double bond equivalents (DBE)
    parameters: db_e_formula=DBE = C − H/2 + N/2 + 1, nosc_formula=NOSC = 2 × O/C − H/C
    input: integrated molecular formula table
    output: chemical space summary (e.g., van Krevelen plot, NOSC distribution, class enrichment)
    confidence: medium
