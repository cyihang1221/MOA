========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: A spectroscopic test suggests that fragment ion structure annotations in MS/MS libraries are frequently incorrect.
source_paper: van Tetering L et al. (2024), Communications chemistry, DOI: 10.1038/s42004-024-01112-7, PMID: 38355930
overall_reproducibility_score: 42/100

workflow_steps: 8
  Step 1: Sample preparation and ionization
    tool: Electrospray ionization (ESI)
    parameters: solvent_composition=50:50 MeOH-H2O, formic_acid_percentage=0.005, flow_rate_ul_per_h=[120, 180], capillary_voltage_V=4500, end_plate_voltage_V=500, dry_gas_temperature_C=180, dry_gas_flow_L_per_min=4
    input: chemical standards in solution
    output: protonated precursor ions ([M+H]+)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: MS/MS and MSⁿ fragmentation
    tool: Bruker Amazon Speed ETD (3D quadrupole ion trap)
    algorithm: collision-induced dissociation (CID)
    parameters: fragmentation_method=CID, mass_isolation=yes, cid_amplitude_optimized=True
    input: protonated precursor ions
    output: MS² and MSⁿ fragment ions
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Accurate mass determination of fragments
    tool: Bruker SolariX XR 7 T FTICR-MS
    parameters: instrument_type=FTICR-MS, magnetic_field_T=7.0
    input: MSⁿ fragment ions
    output: accurate m/z → molecular formula
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Infrared ion spectroscopy (IRIS)
    tool: FELIX free-electron laser + ion trap
    algorithm: IR-induced dissociation yield measurement
    parameters: ir_range_cm_minus1=[560, 1950], macropulse_duration_us=[5, 10], macropulse_energy_mJ=[20, 160], repetition_rate_Hz=[5, 10], bandwidth_percent_of_center_frequency=0.004, step_size_cm_minus1=5, spectra_averaged_per_frequency=6, yield_formula=ln[sum(I_all_ions) / I_precursor_ion], laser_calibration_method=grating spectrometer, energy_correction=linear pulse energy correction, normalization_method=max-peak normalization
    input: mass-isolated MSⁿ fragment ions
    output: experimental IR vibrational spectrum
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Alternative structure enumeration
    tool: RDKit
    algorithm: protonation isomer & stereoisomer generation
    parameters: input_format=SMILES, protonation_isomers_generated=True, stereoisomers_handled=R/S assigned randomly for single stereocenter, enantiomer_exclusion=True
    input: database-annotated fragment structures (HMDB/METLIN/mzCloud)
    output: enumerated candidate ion structures
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Conformational sampling and filtering
    tool: RDKit + classical force field
    algorithm: distance geometry + clustering
    parameters: conformers_sampled=500, clustering_method=RMSD-based, max_structures_after_clustering=20, energy_cutoff_kJ_per_mol=40
    input: candidate ion structures
    output: filtered low-energy conformer ensemble
    confidence: high
  Step 7: Quantum chemical geometry optimization and frequency calculation
    tool: Gaussian16
    algorithm: DFT B3LYP/6-31++G(d,p)
    parameters: theory_level=B3LYP/6-31++G(d,p), initial_optimization_level=PM6, final_optimization_level=B3LYP/6-31++G(d,p), frequency_calculation=True, anharmonicity_correction_factor=0.975, H_stretching_correction_factor=0.955, line_shape=Gaussian, fwhm_cm_minus1=20, post_optimization_energy_refinement=MP2/6-31++G(d,p) single-point
    input: filtered conformer ensemble
    output: theoretical IR spectra
    confidence: high
  Step 8: Spectral matching and annotation validation
    tool: custom comparison (not specified)
    algorithm: experimental vs theoretical IR spectrum correlation
    parameters: matching_metric=visual and spectral similarity, validation_criterion=correctness of database-provided fragment structure annotation
    input: experimental IR spectrum + theoretical IR spectra
    output: validated (or invalidated) fragment ion structure annotation
    confidence: low

reproducibility_gaps:
  - Raw IRIS and MSⁿ spectral data not deposited in any public repository
  - No access to custom scripts for spectral comparison or structure enumeration workflow
  - FELIX free-electron laser and modified Bruker Amazon instrument are highly specialized and not widely accessible
  - Gaussian16 is commercial software requiring license; no input files or job scripts shared
  - No details on how 'spectral similarity' was quantified — visual assessment only implies low objectivity
  - Supplementary Tables S1–S5 and Figs. S62–S66 are referenced but not described in methods nor linked
reproducibility_strengths:
  - Full quantum chemistry protocol explicitly specified (theory level, basis set, scaling factors, line shape)
  - Chemical standards and preparation conditions fully disclosed
  - Instrument parameters (voltages, temperatures, flow rates, laser specs) reported in detail
  - RDKit usage clearly identified for cheminformatics steps
  - All three major MS/MS libraries (HMDB, METLIN, mzCloud) named as sources of erroneous annotations
