  Step 1: Construction of patient-specific genome-scale metabolic models (GEMs)
    tool: COBRA Toolbox

    input: RNA-seq data, gene mutation data, generic human GEM (e.g., Recon3D)
    output: patient-specific GEMs
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Integration of somatic mutation data into GEMs
    tool: custom Python/R scripts
    algorithm: gene knockout simulation based on mutation status (loss-of-function or gain-of-function)
    parameters: mutation_effect=LOF_or_GOF_annotation, knockout_rule=remove reactions associated with mutated enzyme-encoding genes
    input: somatic mutation calls (e.g., VCF), patient-specific GEMs
    output: mutation-perturbed GEMs
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Flux variability analysis (FVA) and differential flux analysis
    tool: COBRA Toolbox
    algorithm: FVA under mutant vs. wild-type constraints
    parameters: objective_function=biomass_production, solver=Gurobi or GLPK, flux_threshold=0.01
    input: mutation-perturbed GEMs, wild-type GEM
    output: differentially active metabolites and reactions
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Metabolite-gene-pathway set identification
    tool: custom R/Python scripts
    algorithm: statistical enrichment (hypergeometric test) + correlation-based filtering (Spearman r > |0.4|, FDR < 0.05)
    parameters: correlation_threshold=0.4, fdr_threshold=0.05, enrichment_pvalue_cutoff=0.01
    input: differential flux results, KEGG/Reactome pathway annotations, mutation-metabolite associations
    output: metabolite-gene-pathway sets
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Multi-omics validation
    tool: R (DESeq2, limma-voom, MetaboAnalyst)
    algorithm: differential expression (RNA-seq), differential abundance (metabolomics), concordance analysis
    parameters: padj_method=BH, log2fc_threshold=1.0, metabolite_pvalue_cutoff=0.05
    input: RNA-seq data, metabolomics data, mutation data from AML and RCC samples
    output: validated metabolite-gene pairs
    confidence: medium
