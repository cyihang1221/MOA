>Materials and Methods   Clinical Study.   The study was approved by the University of Surrey Ethics Committee and conducted according to the Declaration of Helsinki. Subjects had to meet defined inclusion/exclusion criteria to be deemed eligible for the study ( SI Appendix ,  Materials and Methods ). In-Laboratory Session.   The 4-d in-laboratory session was conducted at the Surrey Clinical Research Centre and consisted of an adaptation night followed by a 48-h sampling period beginning at 12:00 h, which comprised a 24-h period (day 1) incorporating an 8-h sleep opportunity (23:00–07:00 h; N1), followed by 24 h (beginning at 12:00 h, day 2) during which subjects remained continually awake (day 2/N2;  SI Appendix , Fig. S5 ). Blood samples were collected for 48 h at hourly and 2-hourly intervals from 12:00 h on day 1 for melatonin and LC/MS analysis, respectively ( SI Appendix ,  Materials and Methods ). Metabolomic Analysis.   Details of the untargeted and targeted metabolomics analysis, as well as all statistical analyses, are given in  SI Appendix ,  Materials and Methods .
