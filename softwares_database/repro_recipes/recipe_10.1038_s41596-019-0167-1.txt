paper_title: Systems-level analysis of isotopic labeling in untargeted metabolomic data by X<sup>13</sup>CMS.
source_paper: Llufrio EM et al. (2019), Nature protocols, DOI: 10.1038/s41596-019-0167-1, PMID: 31168088
overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Raw data conversion to mzML
    tool: MSConvert
    algorithm: ProteoWizard MSConvert
    input: vendor-specific raw files (e.g., .raw, .wiff)
    output: .mzML files
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Isotopic labeling detection and quantification
    tool: X<sup>13</sup>CMS
    algorithm: mass defect filtering, isotopic envelope matching, label incorporation scoring
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=5, retention_time_window_sec=30, min_labeling_fraction=0.05, isotope_label=['13C', '15N', '2H'], enriched_precursor=['U-13C-glucose', 'U-13C-glutamine', 'U-13C-lactate', 'U-13C-2HG']
    input: mzML files + metadata (labeling time, precursor, dose)
    output: labeled feature table with labeling fractions, m+0 to m+n intensities, pathway mapping candidates
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Label fate comparison across conditions
    tool: X<sup>13</sup>CMS
    algorithm: pairwise or multi-group isotopic enrichment ratio analysis
    parameters: statistical_test=two-sided t-test, p_value_threshold=0.05, fold_change_threshold=1.5
    input: labeled feature table from multiple conditions
    output: differentially labeled metabolites list with statistics
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Metabolic pathway mapping of labeled features
    tool: X<sup>13</sup>CMS
    algorithm: carbon atom tracing using known biochemical reactions + mass shift constraints
    parameters: pathway_database=KEGG, reaction_filtering=biochemically plausible carbon transitions only
    input: differentially labeled metabolites + precursor labeling pattern
    output: candidate metabolic fates, pathway activity scores
    confidence: medium
