========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Human Faecal <sup>1</sup>H NMR Metabolomics: Evaluation of Solvent and Sample Processing on Coverage and Reproducibility of Signature Metabolites.
source_paper: Cui M et al. (2020), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.0c00606, PMID: 32567838
overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Faecal sample pooling and preparation
    tool: manual_protocol
    parameters: n_pools=4, storage_temperature=-80°C, thawing_protocol=on ice
    input: fresh human faecal samples
    output: four pooled faecal samples
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Solvent-based extraction (10 protocols)
    tool: manual_extraction
    parameters: solvents=['water', 'methanol', 'DMSO'], concentrations=['100% water', '80% methanol/water', '50% DMSO/water', '100% DMSO'], sample_states=['fresh slurry', 'freeze-dried'], extraction_ratio=1:3 w/v, centrifugation={'speed_rpm': 13000, 'time_min': 10, 'temperature_c': 4}, supernatant_collection=after centrifugation
    input: pooled faecal samples
    output: NMR-ready supernatants
    confidence: high
  Step 3: NMR data acquisition
    tool: Bruker AVANCE III HD spectrometer
    parameters: field_strength_T=600, probe_type=cryoprobe, pulse_sequence=1D NOESYPR1D, temperature_c=298, acquisition_time_s=2.73, relaxation_delay_s=4, scans=128, spectral_width_ppm=20, data_points=64000, water_suppression=presaturation
    input: NMR-ready supernatants
    output: raw .fid NMR data
    confidence: high
  Step 4: NMR spectral preprocessing
    tool: TopSpin (v3.5pl7)
    algorithm: Fourier transformation, phase correction, baseline correction, chemical shift referencing (to TSP at 0 ppm)
    parameters: zero_filling=32k, line_broadening_hz=0.3, apodization=exponential multiplication
    input: raw .fid NMR data
    output: processed .1r spectra (intensity vs. ppm)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Signature mapping (SigMa) analysis
    tool: SigMa
    algorithm: unbiased peak detection and signature assignment using predefined metabolite chemical shift libraries
    parameters: ppm_range=[0.5, 9.5], bin_width_ppm=0.005, library_metabolites=81, library_source=in-house faecal metabolite library
    input: processed .1r spectra
    output: signature-mapped metabolite table (binary or semi-quantitative presence/absence per metabolite)
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Data evaluation: reproducibility, coverage, sensitivity
    tool: custom_R_scripts
    algorithm: CV calculation, metabolite count per protocol, PCA, ANOVA on variance components
    parameters: reproducibility_metric=within-pool CV (%), coverage_metric=number of detected metabolites, sensitivity_metric=detection of low-abundance SCFAs and bile acids, variance_partitioning=ANOVA with solvent type, sample state, and pool as factors
    input: SigMa output table
    output: comparative metrics table, variance decomposition report
    confidence: low

reproducibility_gaps:
  - Bruker TopSpin version 3.5pl7 requires commercial license
  - Exact pulse sequence parameters (e.g., gradient strength, mixing time in NOESYPR1D) not fully specified
  - No raw .fid files directly downloadable — only processed spectra and SigMa tables provided in MTBLS1522

  - Full experimental metadata and SOPs deposited in MetaboLights (MTBLS1522)
  - SigMa software open-source with DOI and MIT license
  - Comprehensive 81-metabolite chemical shift library publicly available
  - All 10 protocols explicitly described with solvents, concentrations, and processing steps
