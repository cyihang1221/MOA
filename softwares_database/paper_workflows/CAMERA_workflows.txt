Samples were collected at the end of the treatment period, processed using UHPLC-qTOF-MS, and subjected to nontargeted metabolomics analysis. QC samples were prepared by pooling equal aliquots from all biological samples to monitor analytical stability.
complete_workflow_description: Raw UHPLC-qTOF-MS data (.whiff.scan files) were first converted to MzXML format using ProteoWizard MSConvert. Peak detection and alignment were performed in XCMS with centWave parameters: m/z tolerance = 25 ppm, peak width = c(10, 60), prefilter = c(10, 100); peak grouping used bw = 5, mzwid = 0.025, minfrac = 0.5. CAMERA was then applied to annotate isotopic peaks and adducts. Features with less than 50% nonzero values in at least one group were filtered out. Metabolite identification was conducted using accurate mass (<10 ppm error), retention time, and MS/MS spectral matching against an in-house database of authentic standards and cross-referenced with KEGG and HMDB; identifications met Level 2 confidence (at least two orthogonal pieces of evidence, e.g., RT + MS/MS + accurate mass). Data were normalized to total peak intensity, Pareto-scaled, and analyzed via PCA and OPLS-DA in SIMCA-P (v16.1), with model robustness assessed by 7-fold cross-validation and response permutation testing. VIP > 1 metabolites from OPLS-DA were subjected to univariate Student’s t-test (p < 0.05). Finally, KEGG pathway enrichment analysis was performed using MetaboAnalyst (www.metaboanalyst.ca).
source_paper: Zhen et al. (2024), Drug design, development and therapy, DOI: 10.2147/DDDT.S423974, PMID: 38476205
