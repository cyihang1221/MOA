========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Ultra-fast and accurate electron ionization mass spectrum matching for compound identification with million-scale in-silico library.
source_paper: Yang Q et al. (2023), Nature communications, DOI: 10.1038/s41467-023-39279-7, PMID: 37349295
overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Chemical dataset filtering and deduplication
    tool: Custom Python/RDKit pipeline
    algorithm: Rule-based filtering + exact SMILES deduplication
    parameters: molecular_mass_cutoff_Da=1000, allowed_elements=['H', 'C', 'O', 'N', 'P', 'S', 'Cl', 'F', 'Br', 'I', 'Si'], ionic_compound_exclusion=True, logp_range=[-12, 24], cross_dataset_deduplication=True
    input: SMILES or SDF files (NIST 2017, ChEMBL 28, HMDB 5.0, extra test set)
    output: Filtered and deduplicated molecular datasets (f-NIST, f-ChEMBL, f-HMDB, extra test set)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: ECFP generation
    tool: RDKit (v2022.03.3)
    algorithm: Extended Connectivity Fingerprint (ECFP4)
    parameters: radius=2, nBits=2048, useFeatures=False, useChirality=False
    input: SMILES strings
    output: ECFP bit vectors
    confidence: high
  Step 3: In-silico EI-MS spectrum prediction
    tool: NEIMS
    algorithm: Deep neural network (pre-trained)
    input: ECFP bit vectors
    output: Predicted spectral intensity vectors (m/z-binned)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Word2vec spectral embedding
    tool: gensim
    algorithm: Continuous Bag of Words (CBOW) Word2vec
    parameters: window_size=1000, vector_size=500, negative=5, alpha=0.025, min_alpha=0.00025, epochs=60, sg=0, sample=1e-05
    input: Predicted spectral peak lists (m/z-intensity pairs)
    output: d-dimensional spectral embeddings (d=500)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: HNSW index construction
    tool: hnswlib or FAISS (implied by context)
    algorithm: Hierarchical Navigable Small World graph
    parameters: M=16, ef_construction=200, ef_search=100, num_threads=0
    input: Word2vec spectral embeddings
    output: HNSW index structure for approximate nearest neighbor search
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Spectrum matching via ANNS
    tool: HNSW-based search + L2 norm re-ranking
    algorithm: Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search with post-hoc Euclidean distance ranking
    parameters: ef_search=100, top_k=10, mass_filter_Da=5.0
    input: Query experimental spectrum (m/z-intensity), HNSW index
    output: Top-k candidate molecules with scores (recall@10, recall@1)
    confidence: high
