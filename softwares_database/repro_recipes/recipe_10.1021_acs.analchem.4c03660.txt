workflow_steps: 3
  Step 1: Alteration analysis (ALA) for trend discovery
    tool: Alteration Analysis (ALA)
    algorithm: unsupervised chemometric technique for detecting systematic changes across sample series
    parameters: number_of_samples=10, signal_to_noise_ratio_range=[15, 50, 65, 200], minimum_detectable_change_per_sample=[0.02, 0.01, 0.001], chromatographic_resolution_limit=0.01
    input: chromatographic data sets (e.g., GC-MS total ion chromatograms or extracted ion chromatograms)
    output: statistically significant chemical trends / alteration profiles
    confidence: low
  Step 2: Two-dimensional correlation analysis (2DCOR)
    tool: 2D Correlation Analysis (2DCOR)
    algorithm: nonquantitative, synchronous/asynchronous correlation mapping of intensity fluctuations across sample series
    input: ALA output (alteration profiles of statistically significant compounds)
    output: 2D correlation maps (synchronous and asynchronous)
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Application to pyrolysis GC-MS data
    tool: Custom implementation of ALA and 2DCOR
    algorithm: ALA followed by 2DCOR on pyGC-MS data across thermal gradient (350–700 °C)
    parameters: temperature_range_celsius=[350, 700], number_of_temperature_points=None, compound_discovery_count=523
    input: pyGC-MS raw or processed chromatographic data (TIC or EIC matrices)
    output: list of 523 statistically significant compounds; 2D correlation maps for selected compounds
    confidence: medium
