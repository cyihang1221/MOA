paper_title: Targeting unique biological signals on the fly to improve MS/MS coverage and identification efficiency in metabolomics.
source_paper: Cho K et al. (2021), Analytica chimica acta, DOI: 10.1016/j.aca.2021.338210, PMID: 33551064
overall_reproducibility_score: 30/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Real-time feature filtering during acquisition
    tool: Orbitrap ID-X instrument software
    algorithm: on-the-fly blank subtraction, adduct/isotope pattern recognition
    parameters: blank_subtraction_enabled=True, adduct_detection_rules=standard (e.g., [M+H]+, [M+Na]+, [M-H]-), isotope_pattern_matching=enabled
    input: raw LC/MS data stream (centroided or profile)
    output: reduced list of candidate features for MS/MS triggering
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Hidden ion-trap scan-based collision energy optimization
    tool: Orbitrap ID-X parallel detection mode
    algorithm: empirical CE selection via low-resolution ion-trap MS/MS scouting scans
    parameters: scouting_scan_type=ion-trap, scanning_mode=hidden/parallel, CE_search_range_eV=[10, 50], resolution_of_CE_selection=discrete (e.g., 5 eV increments)
    input: candidate feature list (m/z, RT, intensity)
    output: feature-specific optimal collision energy (CE) table
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Targeted high-resolution Orbitrap MS/MS acquisition
    tool: Orbitrap ID-X instrument software
    algorithm: data-dependent acquisition (DDA) with feature-specific CE
    parameters: acquisition_mode=targeted DDA, ms1_resolution=60000, ms2_resolution=15000, trigger_threshold_intensity=user-defined (not specified), max_number_of_ms2_scans_per_cycle=None
    input: candidate feature list + optimal CE table
    output: high-res MS/MS spectra (.raw)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Metabolite annotation and credentialing
    tool: Manual curation + credentialing analysis
    algorithm: independent post-acquisition manual annotation using standards, retention time, MS1 accuracy, and MS/MS spectral matching
    parameters: mass_accuracy_tolerance_ppm=5, rt_tolerance_sec=10, spectral_similarity_metric=dot product or similar (not specified)
    input: processed .raw files (converted to .mzML or similar), standard library spectra
    output: annotated metabolite list with confidence levels (e.g., Level 1–2)
    confidence: low
