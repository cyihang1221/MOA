  Step 2: Workflow execution and performance evaluation
    tool: NOREVA
    algorithm: multi-criteria objective assessment
    parameters: criteria_count=5, criteria_types=['class_separation', 'batch_effect_correction', 'missing_value_ratio', 'feature_stability', 'biological_interpretability']
    input: peak table + metadata (sample classes, time points, batches)
    output: performance scores per workflow per criterion
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Collective performance ranking
    tool: NOREVA
    algorithm: weighted aggregation or Pareto-optimal selection
    parameters: ranking_method=comprehensive multi-criteria ranking

    confidence: low
  Step 4: Time-course and multiclass workflow optimization
    tool: NOREVA
    algorithm: study-design-aware workflow selection
    parameters: analysis_type=['time_course', 'multiclass'], support_enabled=True
    input: peak table + structured metadata (time points, >2 classes)
    output: optimized workflow(s) tailored to experimental design
    confidence: medium
