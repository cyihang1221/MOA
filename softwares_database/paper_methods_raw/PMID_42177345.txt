
Methods
Ethical consideration
Prior to the start of the experiment, the procedures and details of
the experiment were evaluated by the Board of Ethical use of
Animals in Teaching and Research, Swedish University of
Agricultural Sciences, Uppsala, Sweden, and an ethical permit was
obtained from the Swedish Board of Agriculture, Uppsala, Sweden
ID number: 5.8.18-05598/2021. All procedures were conducted in
accordance with the ethical guidelines proposed by the Ethical
Committee of the ISAE (International Society of Applied Ethology18
and met the ARRIVE guidelines19.
