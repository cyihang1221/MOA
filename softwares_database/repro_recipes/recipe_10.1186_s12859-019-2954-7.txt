paper_title: Improving MetFrag with statistical learning of fragment annotations
source_paper: Ruttkies C et al. (2019), BMC Bioinformatics, DOI: 10.1186/s12859-019-2954-7, PMID: 31277571
overall_reproducibility_score: 78/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: MS/MS spectral preprocessing and centroiding
    tool: MetFrag (v2.4.5)
    algorithm: centroiding
    input: raw MS/MS spectra (centroided)
    output: centroided m/z fragment peak lists
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: In silico fragmentation and fragment-structure assignment
    tool: MetFrag (v2.4.5)
    algorithm: combinatorial in silico fragmentation
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=None, mass_tolerance_abs=None
    input: candidate molecular structures (from database) and precursor m/z
    output: fragment-structure assignments to m/z peaks (f_nck)
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Fragment fingerprint encoding
    tool: RDKit or Open Babel (implied)
    algorithm: MACCS fingerprint or Circular fingerprint
    parameters: fingerprint_type=['MACCSFingerPrint', 'CircularFingerprint', 'LingoFingerprint']
    input: fragment-structures (as graphs)
    output: binary molecular fingerprints
    confidence: high
  Step 4: m/z binning with mass accuracy-aware discretization
    tool: custom implementation in MetFrag2.4.5 (v2.4.5)
    algorithm: non-equidistant binning
    parameters: mzabs=None, mzppm=None, b0=minimum mass value
    input: centroided m/z fragment peaks
    output: binned m/z values (central bin values)
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Bayesian statistical scoring model training
    tool: custom Bayesian model in MetFrag2.4.5 (v2.4.5)
    algorithm: Bayesian probability estimation P(f̃_k | m_k)
    parameters: domain_reduction=empirical (observed only), independence_assumption=True
    input: training MS/MS spectra + reference candidates + assignments
    output: learned fragment-structure likelihoods per binned m/z
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Candidate scoring with statistical fragment annotation
    tool: MetFrag2.4.5 (v2.4.5)
    algorithm: product of fragment probabilities + neutral loss term
    parameters: scoring_formula=S^c_RawPeak = ∏_{k=1}^K P(f̃_k | m_k)
    input: test MS/MS spectra + candidate structures + learned model
    output: ranked candidate list (Top1, Top10 scores)
    confidence: high
