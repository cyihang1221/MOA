========== sample_description&software_configuration_description entry ==========
sample_description: Milk samples from three commercial labels (A, B, and C) were purchased from a local supermarket. Label A (Arla, 1.5% fat) was used exclusively for spiking with 19 pesticide model contaminants; labels B (Garant) and C (Ekologisk) served as reference samples, each containing three fat concentrations (0.5%, 1.5%, and 3.0%), resulting in six distinct reference sample types. From each of these six reference types, two replicates were prepared, yielding a total of 12 reference samples. Each sample aliquot weighed 10 g (±0.05 g) and was placed in 50-mL test tubes. For spiking, label A milk was fortified with 19 pesticides at four concentration levels (400, 100, 25, and 5 μg/kg), each in duplicate, producing eight spiked samples total. Spiking was performed by adding either 160, 40, 10, or 2 μL of a 25 μg/mL standard solution (in methanol) to 10 g of milk, followed by vigorous agitation. Extraction was carried out by adding 1 mL MilliQ-Integral purified water and 6 mL acetonitrile containing 1% (v/v) formic acid to 1 mL of the spiked or reference milk sample. The mixture was vortexed, then centrifuged at 3000×g, 10°C, for 10 min. Supernatants were filtered using Mini-UniPrep™ vials and stored at +5°C until UHPLC-TOF analysis.
software_configuration_description: TracMass 2 software was used for data processing, consisting of two major steps: feature detection ('peak picking') and retention time alignment. Optimized parameters for the 'tracking' step were: minLength = 9, minIntensity = 200, mzTolerance = 0.01 Da, mzAnchor = 400, and mzTransformation = 'sqrt'. For the 'detection' step, parameters were: zafSigma = 2, zaf2Sigma = 2, gaussSigma = 0.375, nSignaltoNoise = 15, and stdFiltWidth = 16. The generalized fuzzy Hough transform module—designed to resolve alignment ambiguities—was intentionally disabled because retention times in the raw data were highly stable, rendering it unnecessary. In this study, a 'feature' is defined as a monoisotopic peak from any ion (e.g., protonated molecule [M+H]⁺, adducts, fragments, or isotopes), and TracMass 2 output consisted of aligned feature lists where even isotopes of the same ion were not clustered together. These lists were exported to Excel for further sorting based on presence/absence criteria across spiked versus reference samples.
