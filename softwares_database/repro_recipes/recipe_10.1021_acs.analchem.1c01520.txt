========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Chemical Proportionality within Molecular Networks.
source_paper: Petras D et al. (2021), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.1c01520, PMID: 34533933
overall_reproducibility_score: 85/100

workflow_steps: 3
  Step 1: Molecular network generation
    tool: GNPS
    algorithm: cosine similarity
    parameters: min_cosine=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, fragment_tolerance=0.02
    input: centroided .mgf files (MS/MS spectra)
    output: molecular network graph (edges: cosine score > threshold)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: ChemProp scoring
    tool: ChemProp (GNPS plugin)
    algorithm: chemical proportionality calculation
    parameters: directionality=sequential, normalization=z-score per compound, smoothing=none
    input: molecular network + ordered sample metadata (e.g., time series, spatial gradient)
    output: directed edge weights (ChemProp scores), directional network visualization
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Biological prioritization and interpretation
    tool: GNPS web interface + manual curation
    algorithm: directional edge filtering and pathway-aware ranking
    parameters: min_ChemProp_score=0.5, max_edge_distance=2
    input: directed molecular network with ChemProp scores
    output: prioritized transformation hypotheses (e.g., oxidation, glycosylation, deconjugation)
    confidence: low
