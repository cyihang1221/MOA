paper_title: Sharing and community curation of mass spectrometry data with Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking
source_paper: Wang M et al. (2016), Nature Biotechnology, DOI: 10.1038/nbt.3597, PMID: 27504778
overall_reproducibility_score: 92/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: MS/MS data upload and storage
    tool: GNPS platform
    input: raw .mzML or .mgf files
    output: centroids, metadata, and spectral libraries
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Molecular networking via spectral similarity
    tool: Cytoscape + GNPS backend (based on Cosine score)
    algorithm: Cosine correlation
    parameters: min_cosine_score=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, fragment_mz_tolerance=0.5, precursor_mz_tolerance=0.5
    input: centroided MS/MS spectra (.mgf)
    output: molecular network graph (nodes = spectra, edges = cosine similarity)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Spectral library matching
    tool: GNPS spectral library search
    algorithm: dot-product similarity (modified cosine)
    parameters: min_similarity_score=0.3, min_matched_peaks=3, max_precursor_mass_diff=2.0, library_matching_mode=best_match_only
    input: query MS/MS spectra
    output: annotated spectra with compound IDs and confidence scores
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Crowdsourced curation and annotation
    tool: GNPS community annotation interface
    algorithm: manual and consensus-based annotation
    input: unannotated or low-confidence spectral matches
    output: curated spectral library entries with community-voted annotations
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Reanalysis of deposited data ('living data')
    tool: GNPS automated reprocessing pipeline
    algorithm: re-clustering and re-matching against updated libraries
    parameters: reanalysis_frequency=quarterly, trigger_conditions=['new library additions', 'user request']
    input: previously uploaded datasets
    output: updated molecular networks and annotations
    confidence: medium
