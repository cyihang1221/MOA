paper_title: Auto-deconvolution and molecular networking of gas chromatography-mass spectrometry data.
source_paper: Aksenov AA et al. (2021), Nature biotechnology, DOI: 10.1038/s41587-020-0700-3, PMID: 33169034
overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 3
  Step 1: Auto-deconvolution of GC-MS data
    tool: MSHub
    algorithm: unsupervised non-negative matrix factorization
    input: raw GC-MS .cdf or .mzXML files
    output: deconvoluted mass spectra (fragmentation patterns) and abundance matrix
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Molecular networking of deconvoluted GC-MS data
    tool: GNPS Molecular Networking platform
    algorithm: cosine similarity scoring with spectral alignment
    parameters: cosine_score_threshold=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, max_shift_mz=0.5
    input: deconvoluted spectra (peak lists with m/z, intensity, retention time)
    output: molecular network graph (nodes = spectra, edges = cosine similarity ≥ threshold)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Quantification of fragmentation pattern reproducibility across samples
    tool: MSHub
    algorithm: cross-sample correlation of deconvolved spectral components
    parameters: correlation_method=Pearson, reproducibility_threshold=0.8
    input: deconvoluted component spectra across sample set
    output: reproducibility scores per molecular feature
    confidence: low
