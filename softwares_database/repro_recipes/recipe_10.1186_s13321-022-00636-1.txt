paper_title: Machine learning for identification of silylated derivatives from mass spectra.
source_paper: Ljoncheva M et al. (2022), Journal of cheminformatics, DOI: 10.1186/s13321-022-00636-1, PMID: 36109826
overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Dataset curation and spectral preprocessing
    tool: Commercial MS library (NIST/EPA/NIH Mass Spectral Library)
    algorithm: Spectral filtering and normalization
    parameters: derivatization=trimethylsilylation, ion_mode=electron ionization (EI), mass_range=50–800 Da
    input: GC-MS EI spectra of trimethylsilyl (TMS) derivatives
    output: Curated spectral dataset with associated molecular structures (SMILES)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Model training with CSI:IOKR
    tool: CSI:IOKR (vv1.0 (as described in Brouard et al., 2016, J. Cheminf.))
    algorithm: Input Output Kernel Regression
    parameters: kernel=molecular fingerprint kernel + spectral kernel, fingerprint_type=ECFP4, spectral_kernel=marginalized graph kernel on peak lists, cross_validation_folds=5
    input: Paired (spectrum, SMILES) training data
    output: Trained CSI:IOKR model mapping spectra to molecular structures
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Prediction and candidate ranking
    tool: CSI:IOKR (vv1.0)
    algorithm: Top-K structure retrieval via kernel-based similarity
    parameters: top_k=[10, 20], score_metric=IOKR prediction score
    input: GC-MS EI spectra of unknown TMS-derivatized environmental contaminants
    output: Ranked list of candidate compounds (SMILES) per input spectrum
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Performance evaluation
    tool: Custom Python script
    algorithm: Rank-based accuracy assessment
    parameters: metrics=['top-10 accuracy', 'top-20 accuracy'], ground_truth=manually verified compound identities
    input: Predicted top-K rankings + ground-truth annotations
    output: Accuracy metrics (37% top-10, 50% top-20)
    confidence: low
