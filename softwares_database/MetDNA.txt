(2) Spectral matching: modified dot-product (DP) score (Eq. 1) with n=1, m=0; intensities rescaled so highest fragment = 1; forward and reverse DP scores computed; threshold ≥0.8 for retention; RT filtering applied at ±30% of theoretical RT. (5) Reaction-paired neighbor annotation: maximum reaction step = 3 (default); surrogate MS2 spectra transferred from seed to neighbor metabolites with m/z-filtering of fragment ions (removing fragments outside precursor m/z range of target); Score_iden (Eq. 7) uses weights W_m/z = 0.25, W_RT = 0.25, W_spec = 0.5; RT tolerance = ±30%; spectral match tolerance = 0.5 (dot-product threshold implied by scoring context). (6) Confidence grading: Grade 1 (standard-library match), Grade 2 (isotope peaks present), Grade 3 (reliable adducts: e.g., [M+H]+, [M+Na]+, [M+NH4]+ in positive mode; [M−H]−, [M+Cl]−, [M+CH3COO]− in negative mode), Grade 4 (all others). (7) Redundancy removal: recursive process prioritizing higher-grade annotations; peak redundancy = metabolite annotations / annotated peaks; metabolite redundancy = annotated peak groups / metabolite annotations; mean redundancy = (peak redundancy + metabolite redundancy)/2. (8) Pathway analysis: dysregulated peaks selected via t-test or Mann–Whitney–Wilcoxon test (FDR correction optional); hypergeometric test (p < 0.05) for KEGG pathway enrichment across 16 species; quantitative pathway expression computed as average Pareto-scaled peak intensities (most abundant peak per peak group; highest Score_iden peak per metabolite).
