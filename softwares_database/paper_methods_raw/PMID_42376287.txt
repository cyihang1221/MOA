
Methods
4.1. Environment
We collected the dataset in an indoor room measuring 10 m x 10 m
equipped with a motion capture system by Optitrack composed of
18 IR cameras, which tracks the 6D pose of rigid objects inside a 6
m x 6 m x 2 m volume, at up to 200 Hz with sub-millimeter
accuracy. To track objects, we placed reflective markers on the
robotic platform and on hats that people wear during recording.
The room has both artificial lights installed on the ceiling and
natural light from large windows located on one side and equipped
with curtains.
