========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: GCMS-ID: a webserver for identifying compounds from gas chromatography mass spectrometry experiments.
source_paper: Wakoli J et al. (2024), Nucleic acids research, DOI: 10.1093/nar/gkae425, PMID: 38783107
overall_reproducibility_score: 70/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Compound structure input
    tool: GCMS-ID webserver
    algorithm: user-submitted SMILES or InChI
    input: chemical structure (SMILES/InChI)
    output: predicted EI-MS spectrum and/or retention index
    confidence: high
  Step 2: EI-MS spectral prediction
    tool: CFM-EI
    algorithm: competitive fragmentation modeling for electron impact
    parameters: energy_eV=70
    input: chemical structure
    output: predicted EI-MS spectrum (m/z, intensity)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Retention index prediction
    tool: RIpred
    algorithm: machine learning model trained on experimental Kovats RI data
    parameters: column_type=non-polar, temperature_program=standard
    input: chemical structure
    output: predicted Kovats retention index
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Experimental spectrum/RI matching
    tool: GCMS-ID webserver
    algorithm: weighted cosine similarity (EI-MS) + RI deviation penalty
    parameters: ri_tolerance=100, ms_similarity_threshold=0.7
    input: experimental EI-MS spectrum and/or measured RI
    output: ranked list of candidate compounds with scores
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Compound annotation
    tool: GCMS-ID webserver
    algorithm: top-N candidate ranking with confidence scoring
    parameters: max_candidates=10, score_weight_ri=0.3, score_weight_ms=0.7
    input: matched candidates from step 4
    output: annotated compound list with confidence scores
    confidence: high
