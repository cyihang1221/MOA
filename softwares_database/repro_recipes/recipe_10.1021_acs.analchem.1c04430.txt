paper_title: Combining Isotopologue Workflows and Simultaneous Multidimensional Separations to Detect, Identify, and Validate Metabolites in Untargeted Analyses.
source_paper: Dodds JN et al. (2022), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.1c04430, PMID: 35089687
overall_reproducibility_score: 30/100

workflow_steps: 3
  Step 1: Isotopic labeling of biological system
    tool: IROA / MIRACLE / credentialing
    algorithm: isotopic ratio outlier analysis, mass isotopomer ratio analysis of U-13C labeled extracts
    parameters: labeling=U-13C, organism=Escherichia coli
    input: live bacterial culture
    output: isotopically labeled metabolite extract
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Four-dimensional separation and detection
    tool: LC-IMS-CID-MS
    algorithm: ion mobility spectrometry coupled with liquid chromatography, collision-induced dissociation, and tandem mass spectrometry
    parameters: dimensions=['LC', 'IMS', 'MS', 'MS/MS'], drift_time_alignment=codrift of isotopologues
    input: isotopically labeled metabolite extract
    output: multidimensional raw data (.raw or vendor format)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Metabolite annotation using orthogonal molecular descriptors
    tool: custom annotation pipeline
    algorithm: integrated scoring using accurate mass, carbon number, MS/MS spectra, and collision cross section (CCS)
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=None, CCS_tolerance_percent=None, fragment_match_threshold=None
    input: processed LC-IMS-CID-MS data (features with m/z, RT, drift time, CCS, MS/MS spectra)
    output: putatively annotated metabolite list (n = 30)
    confidence: low
