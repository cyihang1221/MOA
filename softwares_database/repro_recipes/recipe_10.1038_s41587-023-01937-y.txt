  Step 4: MSI data preprocessing and ion image generation
    tool: SCiLS Lab Pro and SCiLS Lab API (v2023b (Pro), 6.0 (API))
    algorithm: reference peak list-based m/z calibration
    parameters: mz_calibration_strategy=per-derivative reference peak list, correlation_method=Pearson’s correlation of mean spectra
    input: raw MSI spectra (.d)
    output: calibrated ion images and m/z-aligned feature tables
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Metabolite identification
    tool: Bruker flexImaging and ftmsControl (vv 5.0 and v 2.3.0)
    algorithm: accurate mass matching (<1 ppm) + MS/MS spectral matching
    parameters: mass_accuracy_ppm=1, reference_databases=['PubChem', 'LIPID MAPS'], validation_strategy=colocalization + orthogonal derivatization for DOPAL
    input: MSI spectra + MS/MS spectra
    output: annotated metabolite list with spatial localization
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Spatial transcriptomics library preparation and sequencing

    parameters: read1_length_bp=28, read2_length_bp=150, sequencing_platform=Illumina NextSeq2000
    input: tissue sections on Visium slides
    output: FASTQ files
    confidence: high
