
Methods
Study design
This study evaluates a deep learning-based offside detection system
using supervised learning and held-out test validation on the
YOLOv8 architecture. Annotated professional match footage
provided training and testing data, with validation protocols
following established computer vision benchmarking standards
adapted for sports officiating contexts.
Dataset collection and characteristics
The dataset consisted of 400 high-resolution images and video clips
from professional football matches, captured under various playing
conditions. Broadcast footage was used to ensure coverage across
different stadiums, lighting environments, and camera angles
typically found in competitive settings. Only footage with clear
visibility of players, the ball, and field markings was included, with
a minimum resolution of 1080p to provide sufficient detail for
accurate detection and classification. Eligible samples contained
well-defined offside situations, including unobstructed views of key
players and ball position during active play sequences such as
passes and positional shifts15. Footage was excluded if it exhibited
