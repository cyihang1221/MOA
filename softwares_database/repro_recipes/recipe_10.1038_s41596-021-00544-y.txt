    output: fluorescently labeled GSL glycans
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Lectin microarray preparation

    input: purified lectins
    output: functionalized lectin microarray slide
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: GSL-glycan analysis by lectin microarray
    tool: microarray scanner (e.g., Axon GenePix)
    algorithm: fluorescence intensity quantification
    parameters: excitation_wavelength_nm=488, emission_wavelength_nm=520, scan_resolution_um=5
    input: fluorescently labeled GSL glycans
    output: lectin-binding intensity matrix (raw fluorescence units)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Complementary MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry analysis
    tool: MALDI-TOF MS
    algorithm: linear and reflector mode acquisition
    parameters: matrix=2,5-dihydroxybenzoic acid (DHB), laser_frequency_hz=200, detector_voltage_v=1800, mass_range_mz=[500, 3000], shot_number=100
    input: GSL glycans (released or intact)
    output: MALDI-TOF mass spectra (m/z vs intensity)
    confidence: high
  Step 7: MALDI-TOF/TOF-MS fragmentation and structural annotation
    tool: MALDI-TOF/TOF MS
    algorithm: collision-induced dissociation (CID)
    parameters: collision_energy_ev=1, precursor_ion_selection_window_da=1.0, fragment_mass_range_mz=[50, 2000]
    input: selected precursor ions from MALDI-TOF
    output: MS/MS spectra, annotated glycan structures
    confidence: high
  Step 8: Data integration and glycan linkage inference
    tool: manual curation + pattern matching
    algorithm: lectin binding specificity mapping + MS annotation cross-validation
    parameters: lectin_specificity_database=CFG Glycan Binding Database, linkage_inference_rules=empirical lectin reactivity profiles
    input: lectin microarray intensities + MALDI-TOF/TOF annotations
    output: glycan structure and linkage assignments
    confidence: low
