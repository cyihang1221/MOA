reproducibility_gaps:
  - Raw mass spectrometry data not deposited in public repository (e.g., MetaboLights, MassIVE)
  - Shimadzu .lcd/.qgd raw files require proprietary software (LabSolutions) for conversion — no open converter specified
  - OAD RADICAL SOURCE I is proprietary hardware with undocumented gas mixing/pressure control firmware
  - No access provided to the in-house theoretical OAD fragment library implemented in MS-DIAL 5
  - Supplementary Data 4 referenced but not included in text or linked; critical parameters (adducts per subclass, internal standards) remain inaccessible
  - ZenoTOF 7600 EAD method details (e.g., electron energy calibration, pulse timing) insufficient for replication
  - No description of statistical analysis pipeline (e.g., differential abundance testing, isomer ratio estimation)
reproducibility_strengths:
  - Detailed LC gradient profiles (with time/resolution), column specs, and mobile phase compositions provided
  - Explicit MS instrument parameters (voltages, temperatures, gas flows, pressures) reported for both Shimadzu and SCIEX platforms
  - Use of community-standard lipid nomenclature (Lipidomics Standards Initiative)
  - Reference to established extraction (Bligh & Dyer) and internal standard (EquiSPLASH/UltimateSPLASH) protocols
