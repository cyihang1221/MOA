paper_title: MSFragger-DDA+ enhances peptide identification sensitivity with full isolation window search.
source_paper: Yu F et al. (2025), Nature communications, DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-58728-z, PMID: 40199897
overall_reproducibility_score: 72/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Database search with full isolation window matching
    tool: MSFragger-DDA+ (v4.1)
    algorithm: Hyperscore-based spectrum-peptide matching
    parameters: max_missed_cleavages=1, variable_modifications=['Acetyl (Protein N-term)', 'Oxidation (M)'], fixed_modifications=['Carbamidomethyl (C)'], mass_tolerance_ppm=None, isolation_window_search=True
    input: tandem mass spectra (.mzML or vendor raw)
    output: PSMs (peptide-spectrum matches) before rescoring
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Precursor XIC extraction and quality filtering
    tool: MSFragger-DDA+ (v4.1)
    algorithm: Isotopic XIC extraction + Kullback-Leibler divergence comparison to theoretical isotopic distribution
    parameters: xic_extraction_method=fragment indexing accelerated, kl_divergence_threshold=None
    input: PSMs from step 1
    output: Filtered PSMs with high-quality XICs
    confidence: low
  Step 3: PSM rescoring and FDR control
    tool: FragPipe (v22.0)
    algorithm: Greedy rescoring (Yu et al.), MSBooster deep learning, Percolator re-ranking, Philosopher FDR filtering
    parameters: fdr_threshold=0.01, psm_fdr_level=peptide, protein_fdr_level=protein, workflow=Default, decoy_generation=reversed
    input: Filtered PSMs from step 2
    output: FDR-filtered peptide and protein identifications (peptide.tsv, combined_protein.tsv)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Label-free quantification with/without MBR
    tool: IonQuant
    algorithm: MaxLFQ intensity estimation
    parameters: mb_enabled=[True, False], mb_top_runs=100, max_missed_cleavages=2
    input: FDR-filtered PSMs and feature tables
    output: Protein-level intensity matrix (MaxLFQ Intensity)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Phosphosite localization and filtering
    tool: PTMProphet + FragPipe 'LFQ-phospho' workflow (v22.0)
    algorithm: Localization probability scoring
    parameters: localization_probability_threshold=0.75, fdr_level=global PSM and protein, fdr_threshold=0.01
    input: Phosphorylation-enriched PSMs
    output: Localized phosphosites and phosphosequences
    confidence: high
  Step 6: False discovery rate evaluation using entrapment database
    tool: Custom entrapment database generator + MSFragger-DDA+ (v4.1)
    algorithm: Random shuffling of target proteins (C-terminus fixed)
    parameters: entrapment_protein_shuffling=C-terminal fixed, fdr_evaluation_metric=false discovery proportion (FDP)
    input: Raw spectra + entrapment FASTA database
    output: FDP estimates per tool
    confidence: medium
