workflow_steps: 8
  Step 1: Sample preparation including solid-phase extraction (SPE) and LC fraction collection
    tool: Solid-phase extraction (SPE)
    input: biological samples (urine, plasma)
    output: fractionated samples for NMR/MS analysis
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: One-dimensional (1D) NMR data acquisition
    tool: NMR spectrometer
    input: prepared biological samples
    output: 1D NMR spectra
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Statistical Total Correlation Spectroscopy (STOCSY) analysis
    tool: STOCSY
    algorithm: correlation-based signal clustering
    parameters: correlation_threshold=0.7
    input: 1D NMR spectra
    output: correlated signal networks (putative metabolite groups)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Subset Optimization by Reference Matching (STORM) analysis
    tool: STORM
    algorithm: reference-matching subset optimization
    parameters: reference_signal=user-defined, subset_size=optimized
    input: 1D NMR spectra + reference signal
    output: enhanced signal identification for target metabolite
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Resolution-Enhanced STORM (RED-STORM) analysis
    tool: RED-STORM
    algorithm: resolution-enhanced reference matching
    parameters: smoothing_window=adaptive, resolution_factor=user-tuned
    input: 1D NMR spectra + reference signal
    output: higher-resolution correlated signals
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Two-dimensional (2D) NMR experiments (e.g., COSY, TOCSY, HSQC, HMBC)
    tool: NMR spectrometer
    input: fractionated or enriched samples
    output: 2D NMR spectra (structural connectivity data)
    confidence: low
  Step 7: LC-NMR-MS hyphenated analysis
    tool: LC-NMR-MS platform
    input: complex biological extracts
    output: chromatographic fractions with simultaneous NMR and MS detection
    confidence: low
  Step 8: Metabolite identification via database matching and spiking with authentic standards
    tool: NMR/MS databases (e.g., HMDB, BMRB, GNPS), manual interpretation
    algorithm: spectral matching + chemical validation
    parameters: matching_threshold=qualitative, spike_concentration=matched to endogenous level
    input: 2D NMR spectra, LC-NMR-MS data, reference standards
    output: confirmed metabolite identities
    confidence: high
