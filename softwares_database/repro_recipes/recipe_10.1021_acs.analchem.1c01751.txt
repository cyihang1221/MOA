    output: regioisomer-resolved PIPx feature table

  Step 3: Comprehensive PIPx profiling including acyl chain, phosphorylation degree, and regioisomer identification
    tool: High-resolution mass spectrometry
    parameters: analytes=['PIP', 'PIP2', 'PIP3', 'PI', 'PI4P', 'PI3P', 'PI5P'], regioisomers=["3'-", "4'-", "5'-phosphorylated forms where applicable"]
    input: feature table with m/z, RT, intensity, MS/MS spectra
    output: full PIPx profile (acyl chains × phosphorylation × regioisomer)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Biological validation via pharmacological perturbation
    tool: Wortmannin treatment
    parameters: perturbagen=wortmannin, target=PI3K inhibitor, samples=['NIST SRM1950 plasma', 'Pichia pastoris lipid extract', 'HeLa cell extract']
    input: biological extracts
    output: differential PIPx profiles (treated vs. control)
