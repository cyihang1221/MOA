========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: A metabologenomics strategy for rapid discovery of polyketides derived from modular polyketide synthases.
source_paper: Liu RZ et al. (2025), Chemical science, DOI: 10.1039/d4sc04174g, PMID: 39568943
overall_reproducibility_score: 52/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Bioinformatic identification of modular type I polyketide synthase (PKS) gene clusters
    tool: antiSMASH
    algorithm: HMM-based detection with PKS signature domains
    input: genomic DNA sequence (FASTA)
    output: annotated biosynthetic gene cluster (BGC) coordinates and domain architecture
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Structural prediction of putative polyketide backbones from PKS domain organization
    tool: PRISM
    algorithm: rule-based assembly of acyl units and post-assembly modifications
    input: PKS domain architecture (e.g., AT, KR, DH, ER, ACP, TE)
    output: predicted molecular formula and mass defect range for core scaffold
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Mass defect filtering (MDF) of LC-MS features in negative ion mode
    tool: Custom R/Python script (NegMDF workflow)
    algorithm: mass defect filtering centered on predicted polyketide mass defects
    parameters: ion_mode=negative, mass_defect_tolerance_da=0.005, mz_range_lower_bound=100.0, mz_range_upper_bound=2000.0
    input: LC-MS feature table (m/z, RT, intensity) from .mzML files
    output: MDF-filtered feature list enriched for polyketide-like ions
    confidence: low
  Step 4: Integration of genomic predictions with MDF-filtered MS features
    tool: Custom R/Python script (NegMDF workflow)
    algorithm: pairwise matching of predicted exact mass (±5 ppm) and retention time consistency across biological replicates
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=5, rt_window_min=0.2, min_replicate_support=2
    input: Predicted molecular formulas + MDF-filtered features
    output: Candidate gene-metabolite links (GMLs)
    confidence: low
  Step 5: MS/MS spectral annotation and structural validation
    tool: GNPS
    algorithm: molecular networking with cosine similarity ≥ 0.7
    parameters: cosine_score_threshold=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, min_consistent_peaks=3
    input: MS/MS spectra (.mgf)
    output: annotated molecular families and putative compound classes
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Targeted isolation and NMR validation
    tool: NMR spectroscopy
    algorithm: 1D/2D NMR (¹H, ¹³C, COSY, HSQC, HMBC)
    parameters: field_strength_T=600, solvent=CDCl₃
    input: purified metabolite fractions
    output: fully elucidated chemical structures
    confidence: high
