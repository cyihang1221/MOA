========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Metabolic study of aristolochic acid I-exposed mice liver by atmospheric pressure matrix-assisted laser desorption/ionization mass spectrometry imaging and machine learning.
source_paper: Guo W et al. (2022), Talanta, DOI: 10.1016/j.talanta.2022.123261, PMID: 35101835
overall_reproducibility_score: 42/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Tissue section preparation and AP-MALDI MSI data acquisition
    tool: AP-MALDI mass spectrometry imaging
    input: fresh-frozen liver tissue sections
    output: raw spatial MS imaging datasets (.imzML or vendor format)
    confidence: low
  Step 2: Spatial preprocessing and peak extraction
    tool: SCiLS Lab
    parameters: mass_resolution=10,000, mz_range=50–1000 Da, binning_width_ppm=0.01
    input: raw AP-MALDI MSI data
    output: peak intensity matrix (m/z × pixel)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Multivariate statistical discrimination
    tool: OPLS-DA
    algorithm: orthogonal partial least squares-discriminant analysis
    parameters: n_components=1, n_ortho_components=1, cross_validation=7-fold
    input: peak intensity matrix (11,726 pixels × m/z features)
    output: OPLS-DA scores and loading plots; group separation metrics
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Feature selection and classification modeling
    tool: LASSO
    algorithm: least absolute shrinkage and selection operator
    parameters: alpha=1.0, max_iter=1000, random_state=42
    input: peak intensity matrix (11,726 pixels × m/z features), class labels (control vs AAI)
    output: 16 discriminatory m/z features; binary classifier with 99.81% accuracy
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Metabolite annotation and pathway enrichment
    tool: MetPA
    algorithm: hypergeometric test + impact analysis
    parameters: database=HMDB, p_value_cutoff=0.05, impact_threshold=0.1
    input: list of 16 m/z values (with putative annotations)
    output: enriched metabolic pathways (e.g., taurine/hypotaurine, glycerophospholipid metabolism)
    confidence: high
