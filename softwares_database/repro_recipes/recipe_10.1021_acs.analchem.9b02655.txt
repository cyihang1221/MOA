workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Multiplexed MS/MS spectrum deconvolution
    tool: DecoMetDIA
    algorithm: Linear combination of multiple model peaks to reconstruct coeluted fragment ion chromatograms
    input: SWATH-MS raw data (e.g., .wiff, .d, or vendor-specific formats)
    output: Deconvolved, metabolite-specific MS2 spectra
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Metabolite identification using deconvolved MS2 spectra
    tool: MetDNA and Sirius
    algorithm: Spectral library matching (MetDNA) and molecular formula annotation via fragmentation tree analysis (Sirius)
    input: Deconvolved MS2 spectra from DecoMetDIA
    output: Annotated metabolites (with confidence scores, structural candidates)
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Performance validation against DDA-MS2
    tool: Experimental DDA-MS2 spectra
    algorithm: Spectral similarity comparison (e.g., cosine score or spectral angle)
    input: DecoMetDIA-deconvolved MS2 spectra and matched experimental DDA-MS2 spectra
    output: Spectral accuracy metrics (e.g., % match, similarity score distribution)
    confidence: low
  Step 4: Benchmarking against alternative deconvolution tools
    tool: MS-DIAL
    algorithm: Comparative deconvolution performance assessment on small polar metabolites
    input: Same SWATH-MS raw data processed by DecoMetDIA and MS-DIAL
    output: Comparative metrics (e.g., number of annotated spectra, coverage, accuracy)
    confidence: low
