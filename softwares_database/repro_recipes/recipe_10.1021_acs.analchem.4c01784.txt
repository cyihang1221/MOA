workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Retention index conversion
    tool: custom implementation

    parameters: sigma_tolerance=1.5
    input: experimental retention times, n-alkane standards
    output: retention indices (RI)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Molecular descriptor calculation
    tool: RDKit or similar cheminformatics toolkit
    algorithm: calculation of 5666 molecular descriptors
    parameters: max_molecular_weight_da=500, elemental_constraints=['C', 'O', 'H']
    input: SMILES or structural representations
    output: descriptor matrix (n_compounds × 5666)
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Descriptor selection and model training
    tool: Multiple Linear Regression (MLR)
    algorithm: feature selection + MLR
    parameters: num_descriptors_selected=16, training_set_size=100, test_set_size=16, stationary_phases=['C18', 'F5']
    input: descriptor matrix, retention indices
    output: two predictive models: MLR-C18 and MLR-F5
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Retention-based structural filtration
    tool: custom intersection algorithm
    algorithm: 2-stationary-phase intersection within ±1.5σ prediction error
    parameters: sigma_tolerance=1.5, error_metric=RMSE
    input: predicted RIs from MLR-C18 and MLR-F5, elemental composition candidates
    output: filtered list of plausible structures
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Application to real-world sample
    tool: custom pipeline integrating HRMS and RI prediction
    algorithm: elemental composition assignment → candidate structure generation → RI prediction → intersection filtering
    input: LC-HRMS data from drinking water sample
    output: prioritized structural identifications
    confidence: medium
