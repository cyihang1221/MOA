========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Increased diversity of peptidic natural products revealed by modification-tolerant database search of mass spectra.
source_paper: Gurevich A et al. (2018), Nature microbiology, DOI: 10.1038/s41564-017-0094-2, PMID: 29358742
overall_reproducibility_score: 87/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Molecular networking
    tool: GNPS
    algorithm: cosine score similarity
    parameters: min_cosine_score=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, max_shift_mz=1.0, max_shift_rt=60
    input: centroided .mzML files
    output: molecular network (Cytoscape-compatible edge list)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Database search with modification tolerance
    tool: VarQuest
    algorithm: mass shift-aware spectral matching against PNPdatabase
    parameters: max_mass_shift_da=200.0, max_modifications_per_peptide=5, fragment_tolerance_da=0.02, precursor_tolerance_da=0.01
    input: MS/MS spectra (from molecular network nodes or raw files)
    output: annotated PNP variants with modifications
    confidence: high
  Step 3: PNP database curation and extension
    tool: PNPdatabase
    algorithm: manual curation + automated expansion from literature and BGC predictions
    parameters: source_databases=['AntiSMASH', 'Norine', 'MANA'], inclusion_threshold=experimentally verified or high-confidence BGC-supported
    input: literature, genomic data, existing PNP databases
    output: extended PNPdatabase (FASTA + spectral library)
    confidence: low
  Step 4: Variant prioritization and filtering
    tool: VarQuest
    algorithm: score-based ranking (combined spectral match score and modification plausibility)
    parameters: min_varquest_score=0.6, min_spectral_similarity=0.4, exclude_nonbiosynthetic_modifications=True
    input: VarQuest search results
    output: high-confidence PNP variant identifications
    confidence: medium
