========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Proteome-wide structural changes measured with limited proteolysis-mass spectrometry: an advanced protocol for high-throughput applications.
source_paper: Malinovska L et al. (2023), Nature protocols, DOI: 10.1038/s41596-022-00771-x, PMID: 36526727
overall_reproducibility_score: 87/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Limited proteolysis under native conditions
    tool: Proteinase K (PK)
    parameters: enzyme_to_substrate_ratio=0.005–0.02 (w/w), incubation_time_minutes=[0.5, 1, 5, 10, 30, 60], temperature_celsius=25, buffer_composition=50 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 150 mM NaCl, 5 mM CaCl2, DOC_removal_method=filtration
    input: native protein lysates (mammalian, yeast, E. coli, human cell lines)
    output: partially digested proteolytic fragments (LiP peptides)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Denaturation and complete digestion
    tool: Trypsin
    parameters: denaturation_method=6 M guanidine HCl or 8 M urea, reduction_alkylation=10 mM DTT, 20 mM iodoacetamide, trypsin_to_protein_ratio=1:50 (w/w), digestion_time_hours=16, temperature_celsius=37
    input: PK-treated samples
    output: fully tryptic + half-tryptic (HT) peptides
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Mass spectrometry acquisition
    tool: Data-independent acquisition (DIA) mass spectrometry
    algorithm: DIA (e.g., SWATH, HDMS^E)
    parameters: instrument_platform=TripleTOF 6600+ or timsTOF Pro, mass_tolerance_ppm=10, fragment_ion_tolerance_da=0.02, collision_energy_gradient=optimized for peptide elution window, cycle_time_seconds=2.5, isolation_window_da=25
    input: peptide mixtures (LC-DIA-MS)
    output: DIA raw files (.wiff, .d, .raw)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Peptide identification and quantification
    tool: Spectronaut or DIA-NN
    algorithm: Library-based DIA quantification
    parameters: spectral_library_source=sample-specific or project-specific library, retention_time_alignment=enabled, interference_correction=enabled, peak_integration=top 3 fragment ions
    input: DIA raw files + spectral library
    output: peptide-level intensity matrix (log2-transformed or non-transformed)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Statistical analysis of structural changes
    tool: MSstatsLiP (v1.4.0)
    algorithm: Linear mixed-effects modeling with empirical Bayes moderation
    parameters: fold_change_threshold_log2=1.0, q_value_threshold=0.01, contrasts=treatment vs control, abundance_correction=enabled (spike-in based), trypticity_filtering=separate analysis of fully tryptic vs HT peptides
    input: peptide intensity matrix + metadata (condition, replicate, trypticity)
    output: list of structurally altered peptides, differentially abundant proteins, structural barcodes
    confidence: high

reproducibility_gaps:
  - Exact DIA acquisition method (e.g., SWATH vs HDMS^E) not specified per instrument
  - Spectral library generation parameters (e.g., FDR threshold, search engine settings) not detailed
  - No explicit retention time alignment algorithm (e.g., local regression, PCA-based) stated in MSstatsLiP config
  - Commercial DIA software (Spectronaut/DIA-NN) requires license for full reproducibility

  - MSstatsLiP is open-source (MIT licensed) with DOI and GitHub repository
  - Comprehensive Extended Data figures with statistical source data (XLSX) provided
  - Detailed parameter ranges for PK digestion (E:S ratio, time, temperature, DOC removal)
