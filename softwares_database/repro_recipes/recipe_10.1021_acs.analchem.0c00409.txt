paper_title: Enhanced in-Source Fragmentation Annotation Enables Novel Data Independent Acquisition and Autonomous METLIN Molecular Identification.
source_paper: Xue J et al. (2020), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.0c00409, PMID: 32242660
overall_reproducibility_score: 72/100

workflow_steps: 7
  Step 1: Enhanced in-source fragmentation (eISA) acquisition
    tool: Agilent 6550 QTOF-MS
    algorithm: instrumental parameter tuning for ISF
    parameters: fragmentor_voltage=200, skimmer_voltage=65, capillary_voltage=4000, drying_gas_temperature=325, nebulizer_pressure=35, drying_gas_flow=10
    input: raw .d files (Agilent vendor format)
    output: enhanced ISF spectra (.mzML after conversion)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Data conversion and preprocessing
    tool: ProteoWizard msconvert
    algorithm: centroiding, peak picking
    parameters: 32-bit=True, filter=['peakPicking', 'msLevel 1-2']
    input: raw .d files
    output: centroided .mzML files
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Molecular identification via METLIN matching
    tool: METLIN web platform
    algorithm: spectral similarity scoring (dot product, m/z tolerance, intensity weighting)
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=10, min_match_peaks=3, intensity_weighting=yes, score_threshold=0.7
    input: centroided .mzML files (ISF spectra)
    output: putative metabolite identifications with confidence scores
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Validation against DDA and DIA MS/MS
    tool: Agilent MassHunter Qualitative Analysis
    algorithm: manual spectral comparison and dot product calculation
    parameters: reference_library=METLIN MS/MS library, similarity_metric=dot product, mz_tolerance_ppm=10
    input: eISA spectra, DDA spectra, DIA spectra
    output: spectral similarity scores and fragment match statistics
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Quantitative comparison of precursor ion intensities
    tool: Agilent MassHunter Quantitative Analysis
    algorithm: peak area integration
    parameters: integration_method=baseline-to-baseline, smoothing=Savitzky-Golay (2nd order, 15 points)
    input: chromatographic peak data from .mzML
    output: precursor ion intensity ratios (eISA vs DDA, eISA vs DIA)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Application to macrophage metabolic extract
    tool: METLIN + manual curation
    algorithm: library search + retention time alignment + biological plausibility filtering
    parameters: rt_window_min=0.2, mass_tolerance_ppm=10, min_score=0.65, filter_by_class=True
    input: untargeted LC/MS eISA data from biological sample
    output: annotated metabolite list with confidence levels
    confidence: medium
  Step 7: Extension to low-resolution single quadrupole LC/MS
    tool: Agilent 6150 QQQ or equivalent single quad
    algorithm: enhanced ISF parameter transfer + METLIN matching with relaxed criteria
    parameters: fragmentor_voltage=180, mass_tolerance_da=0.3, min_score=0.5
    input: low-res .d files
    output: low-confidence putative identifications
    confidence: low
