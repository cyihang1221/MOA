========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Three-Dimensional Mass Spectrometry Imaging Reveals Distributions of Lipids and the Drug Metabolite Associated with the Enhanced Growth of Colon Cancer Cell Spheroids Treated with Triclosan.
source_paper: Xie P et al. (2022), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.2c00768, PMID: 36170179
overall_reproducibility_score: 58/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: 3D mass spectrometry imaging data acquisition
    tool: MALDI-TOF/TOF
    parameters: laser_wavelength=355, laser_frequency=200, spot_size=20 μm, mass_range=100–1200 Da, ionization_mode=positive
    input: frozen colon cancer cell spheroid (CCS) sections (serial 10-μm cryosections)
    output: 3D MSI raw data (.imzML + .ibd)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: 3D image reconstruction and spatial registration
    tool: SCiLS Lab
    algorithm: rigid registration
    parameters: interpolation_method=bilinear, z_spacing=10 μm, alignment_reference=central section
    input: series of 2D MSI .imzML files
    output: 3D volumetric ion image stack
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Ion feature detection and spatial segmentation
    tool: SCiLS Lab
    algorithm: peak picking with local maxima detection
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=10, snr_threshold=6, min_peak_width=2, intensity_threshold_percentile=95
    input: 3D volumetric ion image stack
    output: 3D ion feature table (m/z, intensity, x/y/z coordinates)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Region-of-interest (ROI) segmentation based on histology and morphology
    tool: SCiLS Lab + manual annotation
    algorithm: manual delineation of outer proliferative, middle transitional, and inner necrotic microregions
    input: H&E-stained adjacent sections + 3D MSI data
    output: spatially annotated 3D ROIs
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Differential lipid species analysis per microregion
    tool: SCiLS Lab + custom R scripts
    algorithm: fold change calculation + two-tailed t-test
    parameters: fold_change_threshold=1.15, p_value_threshold=0.05, multiple_testing_correction=Benjamini-Hochberg
    input: 3D ion feature table + spatial ROI annotations
    output: list of differentially abundant lipid species per region with FD and p-value
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Lipid identification
    tool: LipidBlast + HMDB + in-house spectral library
    algorithm: MS1 m/z matching + retention time (not applicable) + MS/MS spectral similarity
    parameters: mass_accuracy_ppm=10, spectral_similarity_threshold=0.7, confidence_level=Level 2 (putative structure)
    input: m/z features + MS/MS spectra (if acquired)
    output: annotated lipid species (e.g., PE(34:1), PI(36:2), PC(32:0), SM(34:1))
    confidence: low
