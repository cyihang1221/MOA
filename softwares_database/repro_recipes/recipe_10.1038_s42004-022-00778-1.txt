    input: OAD-MS/MS spectra (text format) with molecular species IDs

    confidence: high
  Step 5: Human verification of annotations
    tool: manual curation
    algorithm: expert review
    input: MS-RIDD output (text)
    output: curated C=C position-resolved lipid identifications
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: LOD and LOA evaluation
    tool: MS-DIAL + custom logic
    algorithm: signal-to-noise thresholding (S/N ≥ 3) for LOD; diagnostic ion presence for LOA
    parameters: s_n_cutoff=3, loa_definition=minimum concentration where essential C=C diagnostic ions are detected
    input: dilution series of standards + MS-DIAL peak table + MS-RIDD output
    output: LOD and LOA values per lipid
    confidence: high
  Step 7: Hierarchical clustering and visualization
    tool: SciPy (v1.5.3)
    algorithm: hierarchical clustering (default linkage)

    output: clustered heatmap, pairwise plots, bar charts
