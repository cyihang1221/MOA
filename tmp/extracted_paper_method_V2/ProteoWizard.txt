========== sample_description&software_configuration_description entry ==========
sample_description: The samples used in the research were grab samples collected from the effluent of a wastewater treatment plant located in Warburg, Germany (Stadtwerke Warburg GmbH). These non-target screening samples were selected due to their high organic load, which induces pronounced matrix-associated effects in high-resolution mass spectrometry (HRMS), such as signal overlay—making them ideal for robustness testing of the newly developed centroiding algorithm. Sample collection and preparation followed the detailed protocols published by Hohrenk et al. (2020) [24].
software_configuration_description: ProteoWizard's msConvert tool was employed solely for format conversion—not for centroiding—to transform raw instrumental data files (Thermo *.raw format from the Q Exactive Orbitrap) into open, platform-independent profile mass spectra in *.mzXML format. No additional msConvert parameters or settings (e.g., centroiding algorithms, smoothing, peak picking, or filtering options) were applied during this step; the conversion preserved the original profile (continuous) data intact. The purpose was strictly to enable seamless import into Python (v3.9), R (v4.0.4), and Julia (v1.6.3) programming environments for subsequent custom centroiding processing.
