workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Mass defect filtering (MDF1)
    tool: in-house MATLAB script
    algorithm: mass defect filter
    parameters: mass_window_da=50, mass_defect_threshold_da=0.001
    input: centroided .mzML or .raw files
    output: candidate metabolite ion list (MDF1 output)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Stable isotope tracing (SIT) filtering
    tool: in-house MATLAB script
    algorithm: isotopic pattern matching with [13C]-labeled pioglitazone reference
    parameters: isotope_shift_da=1.00335, min_isotope_ratio=0.3, max_false_discovery_rate=0.02
    input: MDF1 candidate ion list + isotopic pair data from [13C6]-PIO incubation samples
    output: SIT-filtered metabolite candidates
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Metabolite annotation and structural verification
    tool: Compound Discoverer 3.3 (v3.3)
    algorithm: MS1 mass accuracy + MS/MS spectral matching + retention time alignment
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=5, rt_tolerance_min=0.3, min_cosine_score=0.7
    input: SIT-filtered candidates + MS/MS spectra
    output: annotated metabolites with structural assignments
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Novelty assessment and structural classification
    tool: manual curation + ChemDraw + literature comparison
    algorithm: structural motif analysis (e.g., thiazolidinedione ring-opening)
    parameters: reference_database=HMDB, DrugBank, METLIN, literature on PIO metabolism
    input: annotated metabolites
    output: novel vs known metabolite classification
    confidence: low
