workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Proteoform mass graph (PMG) construction
    tool: TopMGRefine
    algorithm: graph-based proteoform representation
    input: protein sequence database
    output: proteoform mass graph (PMG)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Spectrum mass graph (SMG) construction
    tool: TopMGRefine
    algorithm: peak connectivity graph from top-down MS/MS spectrum
    parameters: error_range_per_peak=predefined
    input: top-down tandem mass spectrum
    output: spectrum mass graph (SMG)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Exact-mass-constrained alignment of PMG and SMG
    tool: TopMGRefine
    algorithm: maximum node-pair matching under global mass consistency constraint
    parameters: mass_consistency_constraint=exact match between sub-path masses in PMG and corrected peak intervals in SMG
    input: PMG and SMG
    output: aligned node-peak pairs with mass corrections
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Diagonal alignment for scalability
    tool: TopMGRefine
    algorithm: heuristic diagonal traversal of alignment space
    input: PMG and SMG (large instances)
    output: scalable approximate alignment
    confidence: medium
