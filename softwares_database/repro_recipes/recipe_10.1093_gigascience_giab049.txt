paper_title: M2aia-Interactive, fast, and memory-efficient analysis of 2D and 3D multi-modal mass spectrometry imaging data.
source_paper: Cordes J et al. (2021), GigaScience, DOI: 10.1093/gigascience/giab049, PMID: 34282451
overall_reproducibility_score: 92/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Data loading and memory-efficient access
    tool: M2aia (v1.0)
    algorithm: chunked imzML parsing with on-demand memory mapping
    parameters: chunk_size=65536, use_memory_mapping=True
    input: imzML files (2D/3D, single- or multi-modal)
    output: in-memory MSI data objects with spatial and spectral indexing
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Interactive visualization and exploration
    tool: M2aia (v1.0)
    algorithm: GPU-accelerated rendering of ion images and spectra
    parameters: rendering_mode=ray_casting, max_rendered_slices=128
    input: loaded imzML data objects
    output: interactive 2D/3D ion images, spectral views, and region-of-interest (ROI) selections
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Image segmentation and ROI definition
    tool: M2aia (v1.0)
    algorithm: region-growing and threshold-based segmentation integrated with MITK's segmentation framework
    parameters: segmentation_method=threshold, min_intensity_percentile=5, max_intensity_percentile=95
    input: ion images or fused multi-modal images
    output: segmented masks and annotated ROIs
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Deformable 3D image reconstruction
    tool: M2aia (v1.0)
    algorithm: B-spline deformable registration (via MITK)
    parameters: grid_spacing_mm=1.0, max_iterations=200, metric=mean_squares
    input: stack of 2D imzML sections (e.g., serial tissue slices)
    output: 3D reconstructed volume in common coordinate space
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Multi-modal image registration
    tool: M2aia (v1.0)
    algorithm: rigid + affine registration followed by B-spline refinement (MITK-based)
    parameters: registration_type=multi_resolution, pyramid_levels=3, interpolator=linear
    input: co-registered imzML datasets (e.g., lipid + peptide MSI)
    output: spatially aligned multi-modal dataset with shared coordinate system
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Fused visualization with individual mass axes
    tool: M2aia (v1.0)
    algorithm: mass-axis-aware overlay rendering
    parameters: overlay_mode=additive, mass_tolerance_ppm=10
    input: multi-modal imzML datasets with distinct m/z axes
    output: co-visualized ion images in shared spatial coordinates, preserving original mass calibration
    confidence: high
