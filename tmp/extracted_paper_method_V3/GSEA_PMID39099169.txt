>Materials and methods   Animals   C57BL/6J mice were obtained from Weitong Lihua Laboratory Animal Technology CO., Ltd.  optn −/−  mice were gifted by Prof. Ronggui Hu (University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Shanghai 200,031, China). The animals were supplied with a standard laboratory diet and water ad libitum at a temperature of 22 ± 1°C with a 12 h light-dark cycle and 65 ± 5% humidity. All animal experiments were approved by the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC) of Zhejiang University (IACUC-s22–008). DILI mouse models   APAP-induced DILI mouse model was established as follows: After fasting overnight, the male C57BL/6J mice (8–10 weeks old) were treated with 150–750 mg/kg APAP (Aladdin, A105808) by intraperitoneal injection to get the DILI mice model. To overexpression hepatic OPTN or OPTN E478G   in vivo , WT or  optn −/−  mice were injected tail intravenously with lentiviruses carrying pCDH-OPTN or pCDH-OPTN E478G  or the pCDH-vector at 0.5 × 10 8  transducing units/mouse for 5 days, then treated with APAP and sacrificed at indicated time-point. Serum GPT/ALT and GOT1/AST levels were measured by a fully automatic blood biochemical analyzer (Roche, Cobas c311, Switzerland). The mice were treated with 750 mg/kg APAP to record the survival rate of mice.   TSN-induced DILI mouse model was established as follows: WT or  optn −/−  male mice (8–10 weeks old) were treated with 10–15 mg/kg TSN (TAUTO BIOTECH, E-0634) by intraperitoneal injection for 24 h before all mice were sacrificed.   INH-induced DILI mouse model was established as follows: WT or  optn −/−  male mice (8–10 weeks old) were intragastric (i.g.) administrated with 150–200 mg/kg INH (Aladdin, I104692) or vehicle (normal saline) for 6 days before all mice were sacrificed.   To investigate the protective effect of pyridoxine on DILI, 20 mg/kg pyridoxine (TargetMol, T0973) was injected intraperitoneally with hepatotoxic drugs simultaneously. Hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) staining   Liver tissues from the mice were fixed in 10% formalin for at least 24 h followed by paraffin embedding. Sections (3 μm) of paraffin-embedded liver tissues were stained with hematoxylin and eosin. Necrotic areas indicated by H&E staining were quantified by ImageJ software. Immunostaining analysis   Liver tissues from the mice were fixed in paraformaldehyde for 2 h and dehydrated in 25% sucrose solution overnight at 4°C. Liver cryosections (8 μm) were incubated with a blocking buffer (PBS [ZETA life, BM0005] with 5% normal goat serum [Solarbio, SL039] and 0.3% Triton X-100 [Biosharp, BS084]) for 1 h. Cells were fixed in paraformaldehyde for 20 min and incubated with a blocking buffer (PBS with 5% normal goat serum and 0.1% Triton X-100) for 30 min. The immunostaining primary antibodies were as follows: rabbit anti-ADGRE1/F4/80 (eBioscience, 14-4801-85), rabbit anti-OPTN (Abcam, ab23666), mouse anti-OPTN (Santa Cruz Biotechnology, sc -166,576), mouse anti-TOMM20 (Santa Cruz Biotechnology, sc -17,764), Rabbit anti-DYKDDDDK Tag (Cell Signaling Technology 14,793), mouse anti-WIPI2 (Abcam, ab105459). The secondary antibodies were as follows: anti-rabbit/mouse secondary antibody (Beijing Zhongshan Biotechnology, PV-6001/PV-6002), Alexa Fluor™ 488/568 anti-rabbit IgG secondary antibody (Invitrogen, A-11008/A-10042), Alexa Fluor™ 488/568 anti-mouse IgG secondary antibody (Invitrogen, A-21202/A-10037), Alexa Fluor™ Plus 647 anti-rabbit IgG secondary antibody (Invitrogen, A-31573). The nuclei were counterstained with DAPI (Dojindo, D212) for tissues and Hoechst (Solarbio 33,343) for cells. Images were captured with Leica SP8 confocal. Cell culture and chemicals   The human hepatocyte cell line L02 was purchased from Jennio Biological Technology (JNO-048). 293T and 293 FT cells were purchased from the Shanghai Institute of Biochemistry and Cell Biology (SCSP-502, SCSP-5212), L02 cells were cultured in RPMI-1640 (Gibco 21,870,076) containing 10% fetal bovine serum (Gibco 16,140,071) with penicillin (100 U/mL) and streptomycin (0.1 mg/mL). 293T and 293 FT cells were cultured in DMEM (Gibco 12,800) containing 10% fetal bovine serum with penicillin (100 U/mL) and streptomycin (0.1 mg/mL). Cells were all maintained in a humidified atmosphere containing 5% CO 2  at 37°C. APAP (Aladdin, A105808), chloroquine (Sigma-Aldrich, C6628), cycloheximide (MCE, HY-12320), and DBEQ (TargetMol, T1969) were dissolved in DMSO as a stock solution for 1000× before cell treatment. RNA interference   Double-stranded oligonucleotides (siRNA) synthesized by Shanghai GenePharma (Shanghai, China) were designed against the human  OPTN . The sequences for the siRNA against OPTN are as follows: sense 5’-CAUGAAGCUAAAUAAUCAATT −3 and anti-sense 5’-UUGAUUAUUUAGCUUCAUGGC-3. The sequences for the siRNA against PINK1 is as follows: sense 5’- CGGACGCTGTTCCTCGTTATG −3. The sequences for the siRNA against PRKN is as follows: sense 5’- CGTGAACATAACTGAGGGCAT −3. Meanwhile, an irrelevant oligonucleotide served as a scramble. The cells were transfected with siRNA using jet medium and siRNA Transfection Reagent (Polyplus, 409–10). Plasmid transfection   Transient plasmid transfection was performed using jet medium and jetPRIME (Polyplus, 114–15), according to the manufacturer’s instructions. 1 μg of indicated plasmid was transfected per well of 6-well plates.   HA-tagged-OPTN and OPTN plasmids were gifted from Pro. Ronggui Hu (University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Shanghai, China). pLenti-GFP-mCherry-LC3B plasmid was personalized customization and obtained from Applied Biological Materials Inc. GFP-LC3 plasmid was gifted from Pro. Daishun Ling (Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, China). MitoQC plasmid was gifted from Pro. Xiangnan Zhang (Zhejiang University, Hangzhou, China). His-tagged-GCDH, Flag-tagged-GCDH, HA-tagged-OPTN [ 1 -]209], HA-tagged-OPTN[210–410], HA-tagged-OPTN[411–577], HA-tagged-OPTN[Δ210–410] and Flag-tagged-VCP plasmids were purchased from miaolingbio. Mutant OPTN E478G  was cloned by site-directed mutagenesis PCR using the following primers: sense 5’- ACTGTTCTGATTTTCATGCTGGAAGAGCAGCGAGAGAGAAAAT-3’ and anti-sense 5’-ATTTTCTCTCTCGCTGCTCTTCCAGCATGAAAATCAGAACAGT-3’. Lentivirus production   Lentivirus was produced by transfecting 293 FT cells with pCMV-R8.91, pMD2-VSVG, and pCDH-OPTN/pCDH-OPTN E478G  plasmids or pCDH plasmids by jetPRIME (Polyplus, 114–15). Virus-containing medium was harvested 48 h after transfection and concentrated by ultracentrifugation (82000 g. for 90 min) at 4°C and stored at −80°C. Western blotting analysis   Cells or liver tissues were washed with PBS and lysed in lysis buffer (0.3% NP-40 [Biosharp, BS205], 0.3% Triton X-100 [Biosharp, BS084], 0.25% leupeptin [Beyotime Biotechnology, G2012], 0.1% PMSF [Beyotime Biotechnology, ST506] and 0.1% NaVO 3  [Kayon, Q0400]). Protein (20–30 µg) from each sample were separated by 10% SDS-PAGE and transferred to PVDF membranes. After blocking with 5% skim milk for 1 h at room temperature, the membranes were stained with primary antibodies overnight at 4°C and followed by secondary horseradish peroxidase-conjugated antibodies for 1 h at room temperature. The membranes were further developed with GE AI100 or azure biosystem after incubated with ECL (PerkinElmer, ORT2655). The following antibodies were used: OPTN (Abcam, ab23666), OPTN (Santa Cruz Biotechnology, sc -166,576), HA-tag (Cell Signaling Technology, 3724), LC3A/B (Cell Signaling Technology 12,741), His-tag (HuaBio, R1207–2), TOMM20 (Santa Cruz Biotechnology, sc -17,764), DYKDDDDK Tag (Cell Signaling Technology 14,793), GCDH (Abcam, ab182152), VCP (PTM bio, 5823), BECN1/Beclin-1 (Santa Cruz Biotechnology, sc -48,341), GAPDH (Diagbio, db106). Quantitative reverse transcription-polymerase chain reaction (qRT-PCR)   Total RNA was isolated from liver tissues or L02 cells after grinding in TRIzol reagent (Takara, 9109) following the manufacturer’s instruction. SYBR Green Supermix (Bio-Rad, 172–5124) was used to perform qRT-PCR on QuantStudio 6 Flex Real-Time PCR System (Applied Biosystems, Carlsbad, California, USA) in 20 μL reaction mixtures. The relative mRNA levels were quantified by the 2 −ΔΔCt  method and all data were normalized to GAPDH (the internal control). PCR primer sequences are available upon request. Electron microscopy   For electron microscopy studies, fresh liver tissues were fixed in 2.5% glutaraldehyde (Sangon Biotech, A600875) in 0.1 mol/L phosphate buffer (pH 7.4) overnight, followed by 1% OsO 4  (AIKE REAGENT 115,355). After staining with uranyl acetate and lead citrate, the tissues were cut into thin sections for observation under Spirit 120kV electron microscope (FEI, America). Mitochondrial membrane potential (MMP) detection   JC-1 (mitochondrial membrane potential probe; Sigma-Aldrich, T4069) staining assays were performed to detect the cell MMP. JC-1 existed either as a cytoplasmic JC-1 monomer or in mitochondrial J-aggregates, depending on the MMP. In healthy cells with high MMP, JC-1 spontaneously forms J-aggregates that emit red fluorescence in the mitochondria. However, in unhealthy cells, the MMP declines, and JC-1 is released from the mitochondria and exists as a monomer with green fluorescence in the cytoplasm. Thus, the MMP was indicated by the ratio of red to green fluorescence intensity. After treatment with APAP for the indicated times and concentrations, cells were collected by trypsinization and subsequently stained with 5 μM JC-1 for 30 min at 37°C in the dark. The FACSCalibur cytometer (BD, USA) was used to measure the stained samples, which adjusted a 520-nm filter for the detection of the green fluorescence and 590 nm for the red fluorescence. Co-immunoprecipitation (co-IP)   Cells form one 10-cm 2  dish were lysed in IP buffer (20 mM Tris base, pH 7.4, 2 mM MgCl 2 , 200 mM NaCl, 0.25% leupeptin [Beyotime Biotechnology, G2012], and 0.1% PMSF [Beyotime Biotechnology, ST506]) with 0.5% NP-40 and the lysates were collected by centrifugation in 16,000 g, 4°C for 30 min. Input samples were collected and lysates were then incubated with indicated beads. For co-IP of endogenous protein, lysates were incubated with Protein A/G Magnetic beads (Bimake,  B23202 ) pre-incubated with BECN1 or normal mouse IgG (Santa Cruz Biotechnology, sc-2025). For co-IP of FLAG-tagged VCP or HA-tagged OPTN, lysates were incubated with 20 μl of magnetic anti-HA beads (Bimake,  B26202 ) or 20 μL of Gel anti-FLAG beads (HuiOu Biotech, HOAO32FL02). After incubation with beads for 12 h at 4°C, the beads were washed 8 times and then eluted with sample buffer. Proteomics analysis   L02 cells were transfected with OPTN-HA or Vector and treated with 5 mM APAP for 6 h and then immunoprecipitation with HA beads. The lysates of HA beads after immunoprecipitation were lysed by lysis buffer (8 M urea [Sigma-Aldrich, V900119-500 G], 1% protease inhibitor cocktail [Merck Millipore 539,134-10 ML]) and then subjected to LC-MS/MS analysis. LC-MS/MS analysis and data processing services were undertaken by PTM Biotech. MitoSOX staining   The L02 cells were stained with MitoSOX red mitochondrial superoxide indicator (Yeasen Biotech, 40778ES50) according to the product instruction. Briefly, the MitoSOX was dissolved in DMSO to reach a working solution of 5 mM. Next, the cells were incubated with MitoSOX for 10 min at 37°C in the dark. After washing with PBS three times, the cells were counterstained with Hoechst (Solarbio 33,343) for 10 min in a 37°C incubator. At last, stained cells were rinsed three times with PBS and then covered in warm PBS for imaging (Leica TCS-SP8, Germany). Autophagic flux assay   Cells cultured in glass-bottom cell culture dishes (Nest 801,001) were transfected with mCherry-GFP-LC3 for 24 h. After treatment with 5 mM APAP for 6 h, cells were covered in warm PBS for imaging (Leica TCS-SP8, Germany). Before being fused with the lysosome, the autophagosome can be detected as GFP (green) and mCherry (red) puncta (showed as a yellow signal after the merge). As the GFP signal is quenched in the acidic pH of autolysosomes, so once the mCherry-GFP-LC3 turns into autolysosomes, only the red signal can be detected. Similarly, under normal conditions, mitochondria fluoresce red and green when transfected with mitoQC, a tandem GFP-mCherry-tagged mitochondria-targeting probe. Upon mitophagy, mitochondria are delivered to lysosomes where the low pH quenches the GFP signal but not mCherry. The result is that a portion of mitochondria form punctate structures and fluoresce red only, with the degree of mitophagy calculated by their increase. GST affinity-isolation assay   Recombinant protein of GST, GST-OPTN and VCP-His were expressed in  E. coli  BL21 (DE3) strain (TransGen Biotech, CD601–02), induced with 0.1 mM isopropyl-1-thio-β-d-galactopyranoside (Invitrogen 15,529,019) for 18 h at 16°C and purified following the protocol from Beyotime Biotechnology (GST-tag Protein Purification Kit, P2262; His-Tag Protein Purification Kit, P2245S). GST (150 µg) or GST-OPTN proteins were incubated with GST Mag-Beads by rocking at room temperature for 30 minutes, and the beads were then washed three times with 1 × PBS. 150 μg of VCP were added to the beads and incubated by rocking at 4°C overnight. The beads were washed eight times with 1 × PBS, and then dissolved into 1.5× SDS loading buffer and boiled 10 min at 95°C. Microscale thermophoresis (MST) binding assay   The MST binding assay using NanoTemper monolith was described previously [ 68 ]. GST, GST-OPTN and VCP-6×His was purified and dialyzed. When purified VCP-6×His was used as target, protein was labeled with RED-Tris-NTA Protein Labeling Kit (MO-L018, NanoTemper Technologies). Seventy-five nM labeled VCP and 4.25 µM to 0.1297 nM GST or GST-OPTN was mixed in PBS-T (PBS with 0.05% Tween 20). The sample was loaded into the premium capillaries (NanoTemper Technologies, MO-K025), and the MST measurements were performed at 25°C, 100% excitation nano-red and medium MST-power. Kd values were calculated using the mass action equation available in MO.Affinity Analysis software. Protein expression-based compound screening assay   L02 cells were incubated with 81 compounds (Table S1) at a concentration of 10 μM for 12 h, and the cells were lysed for western blotting assay of the protein level of OPTN. LysoTracker staining   LysoTracker Red DND-99 (Invitrogen/Thermo Fisher Scientific, L7528) was used following the manufacturer’s instructions to assay lysosomal number and diameter. In briefly, remove the medium of L02 cells from the dish and add the prewarmed (37°C) probe-containing medium and then incubate the cells for 30 min under growth conditions. Then replace the solution with fresh medium for confocal imaging (Leica TCS-SP8, Germany). Evaluations of livers sections from DILI patients   Clinical and pathological evaluations of patients with DILI are from The First People’s Hospital of Huzhou, Zhejiang. This study was approved by the Ethics Committee of The First People’s Hospital of Huzhou (No. 2022KYLL068). RNA sequencing and data analysis   Total RNA was isolated and purified from WT and  optn −/−  livers following the manufacturer’s procedure. The RNA amount and purity were quantified using NanoDrop ND-1000 (NanoDrop, Wilmington, DE, USA) and then submitted for RNA sequencing (RNA-seq). The average insert size for the final cDNA library was 300 ± 50 bp and 2 × 150bp paired-end sequencing (PE150) on an Illumina Novaseq™ 6000 (LC-Bio Technology Co., Ltd., Hangzhou, China) were performed following the vendor’s recommended protocol.   StringTie was used to perform expression levels for mRNAs by calculating FPKM. The differentially expressed mRNAs were selected with fold change > 1.5 or fold change < 0.67 and with parametric F-test comparing nested linear models ( p  < 0.05) by R package edgeR ( https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/edgeR.html ). The scatter diagram, volcano plot, Venn diagram, and heatmap showing differential expression genes were plotted according to  https://www.omicstudio.cn/tool . Gene ontology analysis of gene expression changes was performed using GSEA ( http://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb/search.jsp ). The Dot plot of the GSEA summary was performed by R (v4.0.4) ( https://www.r-project.org/ ). The GO summary plots showing upregulated and downregulated pathways were plotted according to  https://toppgene.cchmc.org/enrichment.jsp . Gene set variation analysis analysis was performed by “gene set variation analysis (v1.48.1)” (R package). Quantification and statistical analysis   All quantifications were done using GraphPad Prism 8.0.1 (San Diego, California,  www.graphpad.com ). All data are shown as means ± SD. Statistical significance was determined using two-tailed Student’s t-tests or one-way ANOVA with Tukey’s multiple comparisons tests or two-way ANOVA with Sidak’s multiple comparisons tests or Log-rank   (Mantel-Cox) test as indicated. Significance was set as * p  < 0.05, ** p  < 0.01, and *** p  < 0.001.All studies were performed with at least three independent experiments.
