paper_title: SIRIUS 4: a rapid tool for turning tandem mass spectra into metabolite structure information

overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Molecular formula identification from MS1 isotope patterns
    tool: SIRIUS 4 (v4.0)
    algorithm: Isotope pattern scoring (based on theoretical isotope distributions)
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=10, charge_state=1, adducts=['[M+H]+', '[M+Na]+', '[M-H]-']
    input: centroided .mzML or .mgf files containing MS1 spectra
    output: ranked list of molecular formula candidates
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Fragmentation tree computation from MS/MS spectra
    tool: SIRIUS 4 (v4.0)
    algorithm: Fragmentation tree algorithm (FT-MS)
    parameters: min_peak_intensity_fraction=0.01, max_number_of_nodes=500, fragmentation_model=small_molecule
    input: centroided .mzML or .mgf files containing MS/MS spectra
    output: fragmentation trees per compound
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Molecular structure identification via fingerprint prediction and database search
    tool: CSI:FingerID (v1.1)
    algorithm: Support Vector Machine + ECFP4 fingerprint prediction + database search (PubChem)
    parameters: fingerprint_size=2048, ecfp_radius=2, svm_kernel=rbf, top_k_results=10
    input: fragmentation trees + molecular formula candidates
    output: ranked list of candidate structures with scores and predicted fingerprints
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Confidence assessment and filtering of identifications
    tool: SIRIUS 4 (v4.0)
    algorithm: Combined score (isotope pattern score × fragmentation tree score × CSI:FingerID score)
    parameters: score_threshold=None, min_combined_score_percentile=90
    input: CSI:FingerID top-k results + SIRIUS scores
    output: confidently annotated metabolite structures (with confidence estimates)
    confidence: low
