  Step 4: Molecular networking construction (MN-1, restrictive)
    tool: GNPS (Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking)
    algorithm: cosine similarity
    parameters: minimum_cosine_score=0.7, minimum_matched_peaks=6, maximum_shift=500, precursor_mass_tolerance=0.02, fragment_mass_tolerance=0.02
    input: UHPLC-MS/MS peak lists (.mgf)
    output: molecular network (clustered MS/MS spectra)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Cluster annotation using reference standards and database matching
    tool: GNPS library search + in-house chemical standards

    output: annotated metabolite clusters (e.g., glycerolipids, phospholipids, novel terpene-glycerolipids)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Iterative molecular networking with decreasing restrictiveness
    tool: GNPS
    algorithm: cosine similarity with relaxed parameters
    parameters: minimum_cosine_score=0.3, minimum_matched_peaks=4, precursor_mass_tolerance=0.5, fragment_mass_tolerance=0.5
    input: same UHPLC-MS/MS data as step 4
    output: expanded molecular networks with increased annotation coverage
    confidence: low
