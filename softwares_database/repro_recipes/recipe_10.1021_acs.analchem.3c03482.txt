========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Spatialized Metabolomic Annotation Combining MALDI Imaging and Molecular Networks.
source_paper: Levasseur M et al. (2024), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.3c03482, PMID: 38134413
overall_reproducibility_score: 58/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: MALDI-FT-ICR mass spectrometry imaging data acquisition
    tool: MALDI-FT-ICR MS
    parameters: instrument_model=solariX 7T FT-ICR MS, laser_wavelength=355, laser_frequency=1000, spatial_resolution=20 μm, mass_range=100–1000 m/z, mass_resolution=600,000 at m/z 400, acquisition_mode=positive ion mode
    input: fresh-frozen plant tissue sections
    output: raw imaging .d files (Bruker format)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: On-tissue MALDI-MS/MS data acquisition
    tool: MALDI-FT-ICR MS/MS
    parameters: fragmentation_method=collision-induced dissociation (CID), isolation_width=2 Da, collision_energy=20–30 eV, acquisition_mode=positive ion mode, target_ions=selected from imaging MS1 data
    input: same tissue sections used for imaging
    output: raw MS/MS spectra (.d files)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Data conversion and peak picking
    tool: CompassXtract
    algorithm: centroiding
    parameters: snr_threshold=3, min_peak_width=2, mass_tolerance_ppm=1
    input: raw .d files (imaging and MS/MS)
    output: centroided peak lists (.csv or .mgf)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Molecular network generation
    tool: GNPS (Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking) (vv42)
    algorithm: cosine similarity
    parameters: minimum_cosine_score=0.7, minimum_matched_peaks=6, precursor_mass_tolerance=0.02, fragment_mass_tolerance=0.02, max_cluster_size=500
    input: MS/MS spectra in .mgf format
    output: molecular network graph (Cytoscape-compatible .gml)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Spatial localization of molecular network nodes
    tool: SCiLS Lab
    algorithm: ion intensity mapping
    parameters: normalization_method=total ion current (TIC) normalization, smoothing=Gaussian filter (σ = 1 pixel)
    input: imaging peak list + molecular network node m/z values
    output: spatialized ion images per network cluster
    confidence: low
  Step 6: Metabolite annotation propagation
    tool: in-house workflow
    algorithm: network-guided annotation transfer
    parameters: annotation_confidence_threshold=high-confidence MS/MS matches (≥80% spectral match), propagation_depth=1
    input: molecular network + annotated nodes + spatial images
    output: spatially resolved metabolite annotations (m/z → putative structure + localization)
    confidence: low
