    algorithm: mass-to-gene and gene-to-mass querying
    input: mouse QTLs + human genome cross-reference
    output: candidate gene–lipid associations (e.g., B4galnt1–gangliosides, ABHD family–acyl-chain-specific phospholipids)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Validation via independent founder strain experiments
    tool: not specified

    input: lipid intensities from founder strain plasma/liver

    confidence: medium
