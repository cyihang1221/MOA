    input: high-resolution mass spectrum (m/z and intensity)
    output: list of candidate molecular formulae
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Parent subformula graph (PSG) construction
    tool: custom implementation
    algorithm: subformula graph generation from candidate parent formulae and fragment peaks
    parameters: min_fragment_intensity_ratio=0.01, mass_tolerance_ppm=10, allowed_elemental_losses=['H', 'CH3', 'H2O', 'CO', 'CH2O', 'C2H4', 'C2H2O']
    input: candidate molecular formulae + MS/MS or EI-MS peak list
    output: parent subformula graph (PSG) for each candidate
    confidence: low
  Step 3: PSG-based scoring
    tool: custom implementation
    algorithm: edge-count metric and normalized edge-density metric
    parameters: edge_score_weight=1.0, density_score_weight=1.0, penalty_for_isotopic_peak_mismatch=True
    input: constructed PSGs
    output: ranked list of candidate parent formulae
    confidence: low
  Step 4: Visualization via two-dimensional fragmentation plot (2DFP)
    tool: custom Python implementation
    algorithm: 2D projection of fragment formulae by elemental composition (C/H vs O/N/S)
    parameters: x_axis_elements=['C', 'H'], y_axis_elements=['O', 'N', 'S'], min_fragment_intensity_ratio=0.01
    input: PSG with annotated fragment formulae
    output: 2DFP figure
    confidence: low
