workflow_steps: 8
  Step 1: Sample preparation and extraction
    tool: MicroLab STAR® system (Hamilton Company)
    algorithm: Protein precipitation with methanol
    parameters: shaking_duration_sec=120, shaking_device=Glen Mills GenoGrinder 2000, centrifugation_g=18000, centrifugation_time_min=15, centrifugation_temp_c=4, drying_method=TurboVap® (Zymark), storage_condition=overnight under nitrogen
    input: whole breast milk and plasma samples stored at −80°C
    output: fractionated extracts for four LC-MS/MS methods
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Chromatographic separation and MS acquisition
    tool: Waters ACQUITY UPLC + Thermo Scientific Q-Exactive Orbitrap
    algorithm: UPLC-MS/MS with data-dependent MS² scanning and dynamic exclusion
    parameters: mass_resolution=35000, scan_range_mz=[70, 1000], ionization_mode=['positive', 'negative'], chromatography_methods=['PosEarly (acidic RP, C18, PFPA/formic acid)', 'PosLate (acidic RP, C18, higher organic)', 'Neg (basic RP, C18, ammonium bicarbonate pH 8)', 'HILIC (negative mode, amide column, ammonium formate pH 10.8)'], gradient_details=not fully specified; includes water/methanol/acetonitrile with additives, column_dimensions=['2.1 × 100 mm, 1.7 µm (BEH C18)', '2.1 × 150 mm, 1.7 µm (BEH Amide)']
    input: reconstituted sample extracts
    output: raw .raw files (Thermo) or vendor format
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Compound identification
    tool: Metabolon proprietary identification pipeline
    algorithm: Library matching using retention index, accurate mass (±10 ppm), and MS/MS spectral similarity
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=10, retention_index_window=narrow, ms_ms_scoring=forward and reverse scores against authentic standards
    input: raw MS and MS/MS spectra
    output: biochemically identified metabolite features with confidence levels
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Peak quantification and normalization
    tool: Metabolon internal software
    algorithm: Area-under-curve quantification; run-day block median normalization
    parameters: normalization_strategy=median alignment per run-day block, reference_value=1.0, normalization_target=each compound independently
    input: feature-intensity matrix (raw peak areas)
    output: normalized metabolite abundance matrix
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Quality control assessment
    tool: Metabolon internal QC pipeline
    algorithm: Median RSD calculation for internal standards and client matrix (CMTRX) replicates
    parameters: qc_sample_frequency=4 technical replicates per 36 study samples, cmtrx_composition=pooled aliquot of all study samples, process_blank_type=extracted water, instrument_qc_standards=spiked cocktail non-interfering with endogenous compounds
    input: QC samples (CMTRX, blanks, spiked standards)
    output: RSD metrics for instrument and process variability
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Statistical analysis and multivariate modeling
    tool: R (vegan package for PERMANOVA, lme4 for linear mixed effects)
    algorithm: Principal coordinates analysis (PCoA); permutational MANOVA (PERMANOVA); linear mixed-effects models
    parameters: distance_metric=Euclidean, model_formula=metabolite ~ HIVExposure * Visit + (1 | PID), random_effect=PID (participant ID), permutations=not specified, significance_threshold=0.05
    input: normalized metabolite abundance matrix + metadata (HIV status, visit time, PID)
    output: statistical associations, PCoA plots, model coefficients
    confidence: medium
  Step 7: Targeted quantification of tryptophan and kynurenine
    tool: Agilent 1290 / SCIEX QTrap 5500 LC-MS/MS
    algorithm: Isotope dilution LC-MS/MS with internal standards and weighted linear regression calibration
    parameters: internal_standards=['Tryptophan-d5', 'Kynurenine-d6'], calibration_range_trp_ug_ml=[0.2, 80.0], calibration_range_kyn_ug_ml=[0.025, 10.0], regression_weighting=weighted linear least squares, qc_frequency=6 QC samples per batch (2 low/2 med/2 high), column_type=BEH Amide UHPLC, ionization_mode=positive ESI
    input: plasma and whole breast milk extracts
    output: absolute concentrations (µg/mL) of tryptophan and kynurenine
    confidence: high
  Step 8: Independent ddhC identification
    tool: Agilent 6546 LC/Q-TOF MS
    algorithm: Accurate mass + MS/MS spectral matching to reference standard
    parameters: ion_mode=HILIC negative mode, mass_accuracy_requirement=molecular formula C9H11N3O4 confirmed via [M+H]+, collision_energy_ramp_v=[5, 40], confirmation_method=co-elution and identical MS/MS spectrum vs. ddhC standard
    input: breast milk extracts (protein-precipitated, dried, reconstituted)
    output: confirmed identity of ddhC
    confidence: high

reproducibility_gaps:
  - Metabolon's proprietary data processing, peak detection, and identification software is closed-source and not described in sufficient detail for independent implementation
  - No access details provided for the 'publicly available database' hosting raw/processed data
  - Gradient profiles, exact mobile phase compositions, and instrument method files not reported
  - R code for statistical modeling (PCoA, PERMANOVA, LMMs) not shared or archived
  - No deposition of raw .raw files in public repositories (e.g., MetaboLights, GNPS, MassIVE)
  - Haiti cohort data integration relied on ZEBS-derived CMTRX — no description of batch correction or harmonization algorithm
reproducibility_strengths:
  - Large-scale, deeply phenotyped longitudinal cohort (N=326 women, 1599 milk samples)
  - Comprehensive QC design including CMTRX, process blanks, and spiked standards
  - Use of isotopically labeled internal standards and rigorous calibration for targeted KT panel
  - Independent orthogonal validation of ddhC using reference standard and co-elution
