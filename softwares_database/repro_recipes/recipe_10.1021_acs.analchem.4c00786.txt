    tool: Rapid QC-MS
    algorithm: median intensity of internal standard peaks across runs
    parameters: internal_standard_names=['C13-glucose', 'd4-benzoic acid']
    input: feature-intensity table (from internal standards)
    output: sensitivity trend plot + alert threshold violation flag
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: In-run retention time stability assessment
    tool: Rapid QC-MS
    algorithm: local alignment of RT peaks using dynamic time warping (DTW) or linear regression fit residuals
    parameters: rt_alignment_reference=first injection, max_rt_drift_minutes=0.5
    input: chromatographic peak RTs (per run)
    output: RT drift heatmap + per-run stability score
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Mass accuracy drift monitoring
    tool: Rapid QC-MS
    algorithm: median absolute mass error (ppm) of lock mass or internal standard ions
    parameters: lock_mass_mz=445.120025, mass_error_window_size=10
    input: centroided MS1 spectra
    output: mass accuracy trend line + control chart (±3σ)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Cross-run batch-level QC aggregation and alerting
    tool: Rapid QC-MS
    algorithm: rolling z-score outlier detection + configurable Slack/Email webhook triggers
    parameters: z_score_threshold=3, alert_cooldown_minutes=30
    input: time-series QC metrics (per run)
    output: alert log + summary dashboard
    confidence: high
