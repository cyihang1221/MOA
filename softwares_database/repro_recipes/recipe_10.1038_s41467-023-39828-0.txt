========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Detecting diagnostic features in MS/MS spectra of post-translationally modified peptides.
source_paper: Geiszler DJ et al. (2023), Nature communications, DOI: 10.1038/s41467-023-39828-0, PMID: 37438360
overall_reproducibility_score: 87/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Mass shift binning and PSM grouping
    tool: PTM-Shepherd
    algorithm: peakpicking with histogram smoothing and prominence-based peak detection
    parameters: bin_width_da=0.0002, smoothing_window=normal distribution over ±2 adjacent bins, prominence_threshold=0.3, signal_to_noise_window_da=0.004, background_window_da=0.01, top_peaks_reported=500, psm_redundancy_handling=retain highest E-value PSM per peptide ion within bin
    input: open-search PSM results (e.g., from MSFragger or Comet)
    output: mass shift bins with representative PSMs
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Spectral feature extraction
    tool: PTM-Shepherd
    algorithm: ion filtering + remainder mass calculation
    parameters: ion_exclusion=['a-ions', 'b-ions', 'y-ions', 'shifted a/b/y-ions'], diagnostic_ion_mass_x_da=150, peptide_remainder_truncation_da=-250, fragment_remainder_calculation=['b-ions', 'y-ions'], msms_tolerance_ppm=None
    input: MS/MS spectra of representative PSMs
    output: three feature vectors/matrices: U (diagnostic ions), V (peptide remainder masses), W (fragment remainder mass matrix)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Recurring feature identification via histogramming
    tool: PTM-Shepherd
    algorithm: rolling mean smoothing with mass-dependent tolerance, local peak detection, integrated area thresholding
    parameters: histogram_bin_width_mda=0.2, smoothing_method=mass-dependent rolling mean, mass_tolerance_formula=tol_ppm * (mass_i + mass_x) / 1000000, integrated_area_threshold_percent=0.1, redundancy_resolution=remove less intense peak if peaks unresolved within MS/MS tolerance
    input: U, V, W feature vectors/matrices
    output: candidate diagnostic features (ions, peptide remainders, fragment remainders)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Statistical significance testing against unmodified background
    tool: Hipparchus statistics library (Java) (vv1.8)
    algorithm: two-sided Mann-Whitney U test with tie and continuity correction
    parameters: background_group=zero-mass-shift (unmodified) PSMs, test_group=mass-shift-bin PSMs, max_psms_per_group=1000, seed_for_reproducibility=internally provided, evalue_threshold=0.05, fold_change_threshold=3.0, auc_inclusion=True, multiple_testing_correction=E-value = p-value × number_of_tests_in_feature_class
    input: candidate features + intensity vectors per PSM (for both groups)
    output: statistically significant, enriched diagnostic features
    confidence: high
