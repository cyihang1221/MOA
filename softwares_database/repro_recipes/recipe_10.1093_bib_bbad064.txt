paper_title: FAMetA: a mass isotopologue-based tool for the comprehensive analysis of fatty acid metabolism.
source_paper: Alcoriza-Balaguer MI et al. (2023), Briefings in bioinformatics, DOI: 10.1093/bib/bbad064, PMID: 36857618
overall_reproducibility_score: 72/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Input of mass isotopologue data
    tool: FAMetA
    algorithm: isotopologue pattern decomposition
    input: FA-specific mass isotopologue profiles (e.g., M+0 to M+n for palmitate, stearate, oleate, etc.)
    output: quantified fractional contributions of metabolic pathways
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Metabolic pathway modeling
    tool: FAMetA
    algorithm: linear constraint-based isotopologue balancing
    parameters: isotope_labeling=13C-glucose or 13C-acetate, FA_chain_lengths_considered=['C16', 'C18', 'C20'], desaturation_positions=['Δ9', 'Δ6', 'Δ5'], elongation_steps=['C16→C18', 'C18→C20']
    input: isotopologue abundances per fatty acid species
    output: pathway flux estimates (import, DNL, elongation, desaturation)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Metabolic reconstruction and novel FA identification
    tool: FAMetA
    algorithm: biosynthetic route inference via isotopologue signature matching
    parameters: reference_pathways=['FASN-dependent DNL', 'SCD1-mediated Δ9-desaturation', 'FADS2-mediated Δ6-desaturation'], novel_FA_detection_threshold=≥2 standard deviations from control isotopologue pattern deviation
    input: pathway flux estimates + inhibitor treatment metadata (e.g., FASN/SCD1/FADS2 inhibition)
    output: inferred novel fatty acids and their putative synthesis routes
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Visualization and interpretation
    tool: FAMetA
    algorithm: interactive pathway mapping and isotopologue profile plotting
    input: pathway flux estimates and isotopologue data
    output: interactive plots: stacked bar charts (pathway contributions), isotopologue distribution heatmaps, biosynthetic route diagrams
    confidence: high
