  Step 8: Two-group statistical pathway testing
    tool: ECCO

    parameters: gamma_p=0.05, comparisons=['age', 'diet', 'sex', 'and all relevant two-way combinations']
    input: feature table + metadata
    output: statistically significant pathways per comparison
    confidence: medium
