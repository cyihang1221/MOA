Apoplastic fluid was harvested via vacuum infiltration followed by centrifugation under defined conditions (e.g., 2000 g for 10 min for L. perenne; 5000 g for 5 min at 4°C for T. aestivum), with subsequent debris removal by high-speed centrifugation (17,000 g for 10 min), and stored at −20°C or freeze-dried. Specifically, BioCyc was integrated into the marvis-pathway module to perform metabolite set enrichment analysis based on accurate mass information. The configuration included querying metabolite features against the BioCyc database (http://biocyc.org; Caspi et al., 2012) to assign putative identities, with no custom parameter modifications reported; the tool automatically leveraged BioCyc’s curated metabolic pathways and compound entries to support structural annotation of 203 filtered metabolite features (after FDR < 0.003 filtering and adduct correction) derived from LC-HR-MS datasets. sample_description: The study used human erythrocytes infected with the Plasmodium falciparum 3D7 strain (IRBC) and non-infected red blood cells (NRBC) as biological samples. Four separate biological replicate cultures (0.5 mL each) were prepared for each of three pH-adjusted solvent extraction conditions (pH 2, 7, and 9), resulting in 48 total samples equally distributed across four experimental conditions: (1) NRBC, SLO untreated; (2) NRBC, SLO treated; (3) IRBC, SLO untreated; and (4) IRBC, SLO treated. Asynchronous P. falciparum cultures were maintained in RPMI 1640 medium supplemented with 25 mM HEPES, 20 mM sodium bicarbonate, 370 µM hypoxanthine, and 0.5% AlbuMAX at 37°C under 90% N2, 5% O2, and 5% CO2 until parasitemia reached ~10%. Human blood samples were collected under protocols approved by the University of Hawaii’s Human Use Research Committee and in accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki, with written informed consent obtained from all subjects.
software_configuration_description: BioCyc was used for pathway analysis via the Pathways add-on in Pathway Architect (within Mass Profiler Professional v12.5). The P. falciparum 3D7 strain pathway data in .owl format was downloaded from BioCyc and uploaded into Pathway Architect. Annotated metabolite IDs from the experimental results were mapped to BioCyc database identifiers using open-source BridgeDB software. The Pathway Analysis feature queried the uploaded pathways to generate matching metabolic pathways, which were graphically rendered by the Pathway Architect engine. HeatStrips were generated from a HeatMap table summarizing identified metabolites in NRBC and IRBC conditions. The analysis produced a summary table listing the total number of metabolites per pathway and the number of matching annotated metabolites, followed by ranking pathways based on the number of possible associations to facilitate biological interpretation.

sample_description: Urine samples were collected from 11 irradiated C57BL/6 male mice (8–10 weeks old) 24 hours after whole-body exposure to a single 8 Gy dose of gamma radiation, and from 10 unexposed age- and strain-matched control mice.
