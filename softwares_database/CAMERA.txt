software_configuration_description: CAMERA was used as a Bioconductor package (version not explicitly stated, but the paper was published in 2012 and references Bioconductor 2.9–2.10 era tools) to perform compound spectrum extraction, isotope and adduct peak annotation, and accurate mass proposal from LC/MS data. It was applied to both positive and negative ion mode datasets, and a novel cross-ion-mode annotation strategy was introduced to improve annotation confidence by combining spectral evidence across modes. The package leverages XCMS-generated peak lists (though XCMS usage is implied rather than fully detailed in the provided text) and applies statistical scoring, retention time alignment, and mass difference filtering for adduct/isotope matching. Specific parameters such as m/z tolerance, retention time window, or significance thresholds are not detailed in the provided Methods or Data Analysis sections.
