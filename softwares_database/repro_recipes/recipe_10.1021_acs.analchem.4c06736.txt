  Step 3: LC-SWATH-MS/MS data acquisition
    tool: Liquid chromatography coupled to SWATH-DIA
    algorithm: sequential window acquisition of all theoretical mass spectra
    parameters: acquisition_mode=data-independent acquisition (DIA), fragmentation_method=electron-activated dissociation (EAD)
    input: DMED-derivatized FAHFAs
    output: SWATH-MS/MS raw files (.wiff or .d, vendor-specific)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: FAHFA annotation and structural elucidation
    tool: MS-DIAL (vnewly updated version (v4.90 or later implied))
    algorithm: library matching + EAD fragment interpretation
    parameters: ion_mode=positive, derivative_tag=DMED, database=custom/updated FAHFA spectral library
    input: SWATH-MS/MS raw files
    output: annotated FAHFA features (molecular species, OH-position-resolved, DB+OH-resolved)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Isomeric quantification
    tool: MRMPROBS
    algorithm: SWATH-MS/MS chromatogram integration using targeted transition lists
    parameters: quantification_mode=chromatogram-based, transition_selection=FAHFA isomer-specific fragment ions
    input: SWATH-MS/MS raw files
    output: isomer-level quantitative matrix (e.g., FAHFA 4:0/2O(FA 26:0), FAHFA 16:0/10O(FA 16:0))
    confidence: medium
