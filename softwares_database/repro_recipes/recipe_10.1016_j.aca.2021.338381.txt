========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Untargeted metabolomics of prostate cancer zwitterionic and positively charged compounds in urine.
source_paper: Cerrato A et al. (2021), Analytica chimica acta, DOI: 10.1016/j.aca.2021.338381, PMID: 33863412
overall_reproducibility_score: 30/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Sample enrichment
    tool: Graphitized carbon black solid-phase extraction
    parameters: enrichment_factor=40
    input: urine samples
    output: enriched polar and positively charged metabolite extracts
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Chromatographic separation
    tool: HILIC
    input: enriched metabolite extracts
    output: HILIC-separated fractions
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Mass spectrometry analysis
    tool: UHPLC-HRMS
    input: HILIC-separated fractions
    output: raw high-resolution mass spectra
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Region of interest (ROI) selection
    tool: ROIMCR
    algorithm: Multivariate Curve-Resolution Alternating Least Squares (MCR-ALS)
    input: raw high-resolution mass spectra
    output: resolved MS regions of interest (ROIs)
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Differential metabolite analysis
    tool: ROIMCR
    algorithm: MCR-ALS
    input: resolved ROIs
    output: tentatively identified differentially expressed metabolites
    confidence: low
  Step 6: Metabolite annotation
    tool: Tentative identification
    parameters: compound_classes=['amino acids', 'carnitine derivatives']
    input: differentially expressed features from MCR-ALS
    output: tentatively identified metabolites
    confidence: medium
