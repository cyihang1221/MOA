    input: feature table
    output: ranked list of features by control-normalized F-ratio
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Top-feature extraction and concentration ratio calculation
    tool: custom script
    algorithm: mean intensity ratio (patients/controls)
    parameters: n_top_features=30

    confidence: medium

    tool: standard statistical software (unspecified)
