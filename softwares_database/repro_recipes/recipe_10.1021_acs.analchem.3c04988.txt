paper_title: SCALiR: A Web Application for Automating Absolute Quantification of Mass Spectrometry-Based Metabolomics Data.
source_paper: Ponce LF et al. (2024), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.3c04988, PMID: 38642077
overall_reproducibility_score: 72/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Standard curve fitting
    tool: SCALiR
    algorithm: linear regression (y = mx + b)
    parameters: r_squared_threshold=0.9, outlier_removal=iterative residual-based
    input: peak area or height from LC-MS data for standard mixtures at known concentrations
    output: calibration equations (slope, intercept, R²) and linear dynamic range per metabolite
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Absolute quantification
    tool: SCALiR
    algorithm: concentration calculation using fitted linear equation
    parameters: use_internal_standard=False, concentration_unit=μM
    input: LC-MS signal intensities (peak area/height) of samples and standards
    output: absolute metabolite concentrations (e.g., μM)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Quantification limit evaluation
    tool: SCALiR
    algorithm: deterministic LOD/LOQ estimation based on calibration curve statistics
    parameters: lod_definition=signal-to-noise ratio ≥ 3, loq_definition=signal-to-noise ratio ≥ 10 or RSD ≤ 20%
    input: calibration curve parameters and replicate standard measurements
    output: limit of detection (LOD) and limit of quantification (LOQ) per metabolite
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Internal standard correction
    tool: SCALiR
    algorithm: ratio-based normalization using internal standard peak area
    parameters: is_correction_mode=area_ratio, is_normalization=True
    input: sample and internal standard peak areas
    output: matrix-effect-corrected absolute concentrations
    confidence: high
