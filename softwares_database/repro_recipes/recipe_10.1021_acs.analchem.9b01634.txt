workflow_steps: 9
  Step 1: Stable isotope labeling of Penicillium nordicum cultures
    tool: custom metabolic labeling protocol
    parameters: labeling_scheme=double stable isotope labeling, isotopes=['^13C', '^15N'], growth_medium=defined minimal medium with labeled precursors
    input: live P. nordicum cultures
    output: isotopically labeled mycelia and metabolites
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Untargeted high-resolution mass spectrometry analysis
    tool: high-resolution mass spectrometer
    parameters: instrument_type=Orbitrap or equivalent, ionization=ESI, polarity=both, mass_resolution=≥60,000, mass_accuracy=≤5 ppm
    input: extracts from labeled and unlabeled P. nordicum cultures
    output: raw .raw or .d files
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Elemental composition determination via isotopic pattern analysis
    tool: custom workflow using isotope labeling constraints
    algorithm: isotope ratio filtering (M+1/M and M+2/M)
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=5, min_isotope_match_score=0.8
    input: raw MS1 data
    output: confident elemental compositions for features
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Feature selection based on isotopic labeling
    tool: custom R/Python script
    algorithm: label-specific feature detection (e.g., M+6 to M+10 shifts)
    parameters: min_labeling_ratio=2.0, min_fold_change_labeled_vs_unlabeled=3.0, max_RSD_unlabeled=0.3
    input: feature table (e.g., from XCMS or MS-DIAL)
    output: subset of features confidently assigned to P. nordicum metabolism
    confidence: low
  Step 5: MS/MS data acquisition and preprocessing
    tool: data-dependent acquisition (DDA)
    parameters: collision_energy=stepped CE, precursor_isolation_window=2.0 Th, top_N=10
    input: selected features from step 4
    output: MS/MS spectra (.mgf or .mzML)
    confidence: low
  Step 6: Molecular networking of MS/MS data
    tool: GNPS (vv3.x (2019-era))
    algorithm: cosine similarity
    parameters: min_cosine_score=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, max_shift_mz=0.5, precursor_mass_tolerance=0.02
    input: MS/MS spectra (.mgf)
    output: molecular network (graph)
    confidence: high
  Step 7: Network curation and subnetwork extraction
    tool: MetGem (vv1.3 or earlier (2019))
    algorithm: clustering + manual annotation propagation
    parameters: clustering_algorithm=MCL, inflation_parameter=2.0, similarity_threshold=0.65
    input: GNPS network + metadata (e.g., labeling status, retention time)
    output: curated subnetworks of interest (e.g., fungisporin-related)
    confidence: medium
  Step 8: Structural characterization of unknown metabolites
    tool: manual MS/MS interpretation + database matching
    algorithm: fragmentation tree analysis, diagnostic ion matching
    parameters: reference_databases=['GNPS libraries', 'MassBank', 'METLIN'], validation_criteria=retention time consistency, isotopic pattern match, literature analogues
    input: curated MS/MS spectra + network context
    output: putative chemical structures (e.g., linear peptides, fungisporin derivatives)
    confidence: low
  Step 9: Validation on natural substrates
    tool: targeted LC-MS/MS
    parameters: substrates=['pork ham', 'turkey ham', 'cheese'], extraction_method=solid-phase extraction, detection_mode=MRM or high-res SIM
    input: food matrix extracts
    output: presence/absence and semi-quantitative levels of identified metabolites
    confidence: medium
