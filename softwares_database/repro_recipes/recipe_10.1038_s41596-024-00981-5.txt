  Step 2: Flow injection analysis of nitrate
    tool: Thermo Scientific Orbitrap mass spectrometer with flow injection system
    algorithm: flow injection
    parameters: scan_range_mz=[60, 80], resolution=15000, AGC_target=1000000, ion_transfer_time_ms=0.75, standards=['USGS35', 'USGS32']
    input: nitrate standards and samples in aqueous solution
    output: raw .RAW files
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Isotopocule extraction and ratio calculation
    tool: custom Orbitrap IRMS data-extraction and -processing software
    algorithm: isotopologue-specific ion current integration
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=2, integration_window_ms=100, baseline_correction=linear, one_point_calibration=True
    input: raw .RAW files
    output: isotopocule ratios (e.g., 13C/M0, 18O/M0), δ-values
    confidence: low
  Step 4: Shot-noise precision assessment
    tool: custom R/Python scripts (implied)
    algorithm: relative standard error vs. theoretical shot-noise limit
    parameters: replicates=5, integration_time_ms=1000
    input: extracted ion currents (EICs)
    output: shot-noise plots, relative standard error (RSE)
    confidence: medium
