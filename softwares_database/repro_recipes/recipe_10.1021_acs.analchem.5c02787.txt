========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: MALDI-TIMS-MS&lt;sup&gt;2&lt;/sup&gt; Imaging and Annotation of Natural Products in Fungal-Bacterial Coculture.
source_paper: Shepherd RA et al. (2025), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.5c02787, PMID: 40864852
overall_reproducibility_score: 42/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: MALDI sample preparation and imaging acquisition
    tool: timsTOF fleX
    parameters: ionization_mode=positive, laser_frequency=1000, laser_energy_percent=70, spatial_resolution=20 µm, mass_range_mz=[100, 1500], tim_s_duty_cycle=high sensitivity
    input: fungal-bacterial coculture tissue section
    output: raw .d files (Bruker format)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Untargeted precursor selection for iprm-PASEF
    tool: SCiLS Lab
    algorithm: intensity-based peak picking with mobility-resolved feature detection
    parameters: min_intensity=1000, mobility_drift_time_window_ms=2.0, mz_tolerance_ppm=10, retention_time_tolerance_sec=None
    input: raw .d files
    output: precursor list (.csv or .txt) for iprm-PASEF method generation
    confidence: low
  Step 3: iprm-PASEF acquisition
    tool: timsTOF fleX with Bruker Compass
    algorithm: prm-PASEF with TIMS mobility separation
    parameters: isolation_window_mz=1.2, collision_energy_ev=25, number_of_pasef_scans_per_cycle=4, tim_s_accumulation_time_ms=100, mobility_resolving_power=≥100
    input: precursor list from SCiLS Lab
    output: MS² spectra (.d files with MS¹ + MS² layers)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: MS¹ and MS² data processing and metabolite annotation
    tool: SCiLS Lab + in-house spectral matching pipeline
    algorithm: library matching against GNPS, mzCloud, and in-house fungal/bacterial NP database
    parameters: ms1_mass_tolerance_ppm=5, ms2_cosine_similarity_threshold=0.7, min_matched_fragment_count=3, library_search_mode=hybrid (MS¹ + MS²)
    input: MS¹ + MS² .d files
    output: spatially resolved annotated metabolite images (e.g., coproporphyrin III)
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Spatial visualization and interpretation
    tool: SCiLS Lab
    algorithm: ion intensity heatmap rendering with spatial registration
    parameters: normalization_method=total ion current (TIC) normalization per pixel, smoothing=Gaussian kernel σ=1.5 pixels
    input: annotated feature table with x/y coordinates
    output: metabolite distribution heatmaps
    confidence: high
