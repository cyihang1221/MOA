
Experimental resolution was evaluated using fluorescent
microspheres embedded in agarose gel. Microspheres with
diameters of 0.5 µm and 1.0 µm were used for the micro-scale and
meso-scale modes, respectively. For each configuration, at least 10
microspheres were analyzed and the average full width at half
maximum (FWHM) intensity profiles were calculated. In the micro-
scale mode, the measured lateral resolutions were 0.64 ± 0.03 µm
(x) and 0.73 ± 0.05 µm (y), and the axial resolution was 1.31 ± 
0.21 µm (z), including convolution with the bead diameter
(Fig. 2(b)). In the meso-scale mode, the measured lateral resolutions
