    tool: custom implementation

    parameters: similarity_threshold=0.7

    output: anchor-suspect pairs with similarity scores
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Biotransformation rule application
    tool: BAM (Biotransformation-based Annotation Method)
    algorithm: rule-based chemical transformation propagation (e.g., oxidation, methylation, glycosylation)
    parameters: max_transformations=2, rule_database=BioTransformer-derived or Reactome-based
    input: anchor molecule structure (SMILES/InChI), suspect MS/MS spectrum
    output: ranked list of candidate molecular structures for suspect
    confidence: low
  Step 4: Structure ranking and validation
    tool: custom scoring
    algorithm: integrated scoring combining spectral match, biotransformation plausibility, and network context
    parameters: top_k_candidates=10
    input: candidate structures + spectral data + network neighborhood
    output: annotated suspect metabolites with confidence ranks
    confidence: low
