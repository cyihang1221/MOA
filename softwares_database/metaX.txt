sample_description: Plasma samples from 210 patients diagnosed with preclinical atherosclerosis (PCA) and 50 healthy controls, all aged 40–75 years, recruited from Affiliated Zhongshan Hospital of Dalian University between July 2023 and February 2024. PCA diagnosis was based on carotid intima-media thickness (IMT) ≥0.9 mm or presence of atherosclerotic plaques >1.2 mm thick, assessed via Hitachi LOGIQ ultrasound. Blood was collected in K3EDTA tubes, centrifuged at 4°C and 3000 rpm for 10 min, plasma aliquoted (0.2 mL), flash-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at −80°C. For metabolomics, 100 μL plasma was extracted with 400 μL pre-chilled 80% methanol, incubated on ice, centrifuged twice at 15,000 × g and 4°C for 20 min each, and diluted to 53% methanol prior to UHPLC-MS/MS analysis.
software_configuration_description: metaX was used for principal component analysis (PCA) and partial least squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA). Differential metabolites were identified using criteria: variable importance in projection (VIP) > 1, P < 0.05 (from univariate t-test), and fold change ≥ 2 or ≤ 0.5. The paper does not specify metaX version number, parameter settings (e.g., scaling method, missing value imputation strategy, or cross-validation details), nor describe its integration with upstream (Compound Discoverer) or downstream (KEGG enrichment) tools beyond stating it was used for multivariate statistical modeling and visualization.
