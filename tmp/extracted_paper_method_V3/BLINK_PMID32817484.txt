Briefly, ALP from bovine intestinal mucosa was purchased from Sigma-Aldrich (Cat # P7923), further purified in house, and fluorescently labeled using amine-NHS chemistry. ABEL trap-based single-molecule diffusometry was implemented as previously described ( 26 ) and high-speed SPT was carried out according to the recent protocol ( 36 ).
