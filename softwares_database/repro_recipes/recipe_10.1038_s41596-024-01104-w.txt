========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: An accessible workflow for high-sensitivity proteomics using parallel accumulation-serial fragmentation (PASEF).
source_paper: Skowronek P et al. (2025), Nature protocols, DOI: 10.1038/s41596-024-01104-w, PMID: 39825144
overall_reproducibility_score: 78/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Sample preparation
    tool: Evosep One
    parameters: gradient_length=21 min, sample_loading_volume=1.5 μL, flow_rate=1000 nL/min
    input: biological sample (e.g., human cancer cell lysate)
    output: chromatographically separated peptides
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Mass spectrometry acquisition using PASEF
    tool: timsTOF (Bruker)
    algorithm: PASEF
    parameters: ion_mobility_resolution=up to 200, accumulation_time_ms=10, fragmentation_cycle_time_ms=100, isolation_window_width_mz=24
    input: chromatographically separated peptides
    output: raw .d files (Bruker format)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: DIA method design and optimization
    tool: py_diAID
    algorithm: Bayesian optimization
    parameters: scan_area_A1=400, scan_area_A2=1200, scan_area_B1=0.6, scan_area_B2=1.8, num_iterations=20, acquisition_scheme=dia-PASEF or synchro-PASEF
    input: representative single-run raw data (.d)
    output: optimized DIA method file (.xml or .txt)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Data acquisition with optimized dia-PASEF/synchro-PASEF
    tool: timsTOF + TimsControl
    algorithm: synchro-PASEF
    parameters: synchro_window_ms=10, mobility_step_V=10, mz_step_Da=0.4
    input: optimized DIA method file
    output: raw .d files (Bruker format)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Data processing and quantification
    tool: Spectronaut or AlphaDIA
    algorithm: library-free DIA analysis
    parameters: fdr_threshold=0.01, min_peptides_per_protein=1, min_unique_peptides=1, quantification_method=peak area
    input: raw .d files
    output: protein/peptide/phosphosite quantification table
    confidence: high
