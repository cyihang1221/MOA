========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Defining the Ceramide Composition of Bovine and Human Milk Gangliosides by Direct Infusion ESI-CID Tandem Mass Spectrometry of Native and Permethylated Molecular Species.
source_paper: Liyanage OT et al. (2023), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.3c00737, PMID: 37877731
overall_reproducibility_score: 30/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Sample preparation and derivatization
    tool: chemical derivatization
    parameters: derivatization_method=permethylation, reaction_conditions=sodium hydride in DMF, methyl iodide
    input: native ganglioside extracts from bovine and human milk
    output: native and permethylated ganglioside samples
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Direct infusion electrospray ionization mass spectrometry
    tool: ESI-MS
    parameters: ionization_mode=negative mode, source_temperature=250, capillary_voltage=-3.5
    input: native and permethylated ganglioside samples
    output: full-scan MS spectra (m/z 500–2500)
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Low-energy CID tandem MS for glycan characterization
    tool: CID-MS/MS
    parameters: collision_energy=low, target_mz=precursor ions of gangliosides, fragmentation_pattern_focus=glycosidic cleavages
    input: selected precursor ions from full-scan MS
    output: glycan sequence assignments
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Stepped higher-energy CID tandem MS for ceramide structural characterization
    tool: stepped-CID MS/MS
    parameters: collision_energy_steps=[20, 30, 40, 50], fragmentation_pattern_focus=ceramide-specific fragments (LCB and FA ions), key_ions_monitored=['m/z 264.2 (d18:1 LCB fragment)', 'm/z 278.2 (d18:2 LCB fragment)', 'FA carboxylate anions']
    input: same precursors as step 3
    output: LCB and FA composition assignments, including isomeric differentiation (e.g., d18:1 vs t18:1, FA chain length/unsaturation)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Structural interpretation and assignment of intact molecular species
    tool: manual spectral interpretation
    algorithm: fragment ion mapping and diagnostic ion matching
    parameters: diagnostic_ions_used=['Ceramide fragment ions', 'sialyl-glycan cross-ring fragments', 'permethylation-induced mass shifts'], isomer_discrimination_criteria=relative intensities of LCB-specific fragments and FA anions
    input: low- and high-energy CID spectra (native and permethylated)
    output: annotated ganglioside molecular species with defined LCB and FA structures
    confidence: low
