========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Visualization of metabolites and microbes at high spatial resolution using MALDI mass spectrometry imaging and in situ fluorescence labeling.
source_paper: Bourceau P et al. (2023), Nature protocols, DOI: 10.1038/s41596-023-00864-1, PMID: 37674095
overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 8
  Step 1: Tissue preparation and cryosectioning
    tool: Cryostat
    parameters: section_thickness=10, section_thickness_unit=µm, cryoprotectant=CMC, trimming_protocol=Supplementary Methods 1
    input: fresh/frozen tissue sample
    output: mounted tissue section on conductive glass slide
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: On-tissue fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH)
    tool: Custom FISH probes
    parameters: hybridization_temperature=46, hybridization_time_min=240, probe_concentration=100 nM, washing_steps=3, blocking_agent=BSA + salmon sperm DNA
    input: tissue section on slide
    output: FISH-labeled tissue section with microbial cell localization
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Fluorescence microscopy imaging
    tool: Widefield or confocal microscope
    parameters: objective_magnification=20, pixel_size_µm=0.32, emission_filters=['DAPI', 'FITC', 'Cy3', 'Cy5'], z-stack=True
    input: FISH-labeled tissue section
    output: multichannel fluorescence image stack (TIFF)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Image registration and correlative data integration
    tool: custom Python/ImageJ/Fiji scripts
    algorithm: affine registration using fiducial markers (e.g., tissue edges, scratches)
    parameters: registration_accuracy_target=≤ 2 µm, reference_channel=DAPI, interpolation_method=bilinear
    input: fluorescence image stack + MALDI-MSI raw data (imzML)
    output: co-registered spatial dataset (aligned fluorescence + ion images)
    confidence: low
  Step 5: MALDI matrix application
    tool: SunCollect sprayer (or similar robotic sprayer)
    parameters: matrix_solution=9-chloroanthracene (9-CA) in chloroform:ethanol (1:1), spray_cycles=8, layer_uniformity_target=homogeneous sub-micron crystallization, drying_time_min=10
    input: FISH-imaged tissue section
    output: matrix-coated tissue section ready for MSI
    confidence: high
  Step 6: High-resolution MALDI-MSI data acquisition
    tool: Bruker timsTOF fleX or ultrafleX MALDI-TOF/TOF
    algorithm: automatic laser rastering
    parameters: spatial_resolution=10, spatial_resolution_unit=µm, laser_frequency_Hz=1000, laser_intensity_percent=70, shots_per_pixel=50, mass_range_mz=[100, 1200], ionization_mode=positive, detector_voltage_kV=2.2
    input: matrix-coated tissue section
    output: imzML file (raw MSI data)
    confidence: high
  Step 7: MSI data processing and metabolite imaging
    tool: SCiLS Lab or Bruker FlexImaging
    algorithm: peak picking (SNR > 6), baseline correction, normalization (TIC), spatial smoothing (Gaussian kernel σ=1 pixel)
    parameters: snr_threshold=6, normalization_method=total ion current (TIC), smoothing_sigma_pixels=1, mass_tolerance_ppm=10
    input: imzML file
    output: ion intensity images (per m/z), feature table (m/z, x, y, intensity)
    confidence: medium
  Step 8: Metabolite–microbe co-localization analysis
    tool: Custom R/Python scripts
    algorithm: Pearson correlation of ion image intensities with fluorescence channel intensities per pixel; spatial overlap quantification (Jaccard index)
    parameters: correlation_threshold_r=0.5, jaccard_threshold=0.3, min_fluorescence_intensity_percentile=90, region_of_interest_definition=FISH-positive clusters
    input: co-registered ion images + fluorescence channel images
    output: co-localized metabolite lists, spatial correlation maps
    confidence: low
