  Step 3: Computational variation assessment
    tool: Custom R scripts
    algorithm: concentration-dependent CV estimation per feature
    parameters: cv_threshold=0.2, concentration_levels=[1, 0.5, 0.25, 0.125]
    input: PH and PA intensity matrices across concentration series
    output: computational variation profile per feature
    confidence: low
  Step 4: QC-based measurement selection
    tool: Custom R workflow
    algorithm: automated PH/PA selection per feature based on QC sample CV consistency
    parameters: qc_sample_count=6, cv_consistency_window=same concentration level, preference_rule=select PH if CV_PH < CV_PA in QC replicates
    input: PH matrix, PA matrix, QC sample intensities
    output: hybrid intensity matrix (PH or PA selected per feature)
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Statistical comparison before/after correction
    tool: R (stats package)
    algorithm: two-sided t-test with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction
    parameters: alpha=0.05, fdr_method=BH, test_type=paired t-test (pre- vs post-chemotherapy)
    input: uncorrected and corrected hybrid intensity matrices
    output: differentially abundant metabolite list (FDR-adjusted p-values)
