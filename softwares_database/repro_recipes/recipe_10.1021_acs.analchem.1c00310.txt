========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: MANTI: Automated Annotation of Protein N-Termini for Rapid Interpretation of N-Terminome Data Sets.
source_paper: Demir F et al. (2021), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.1c00310, PMID: 33729755
overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: N-terminal peptide identification
    tool: MaxQuant
    algorithm: Andromeda search engine
    input: raw .raw or .d folder files (Thermo)
    output: peptide-spectrum matches (PSMs), protein groups, evidence.txt table
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: N-termini validation and annotation
    tool: MANTI
    algorithm: multistep decision process integrating UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot, signal peptide predictions (SignalP), transmembrane domain predictions (TMHMM), subcellular localization, protein isoforms, and cleavage site context
    parameters: min_peptide_length=7, max_peptide_length=30, allow_internal_met_start=True, use_signalp=True, use_tmhmm=True
    input: MaxQuant evidence.txt output
    output: annotated N-terminal peptides with origin classification (e.g., mature N-terminus, neo-N-terminus, signal peptide cleavage, etc.)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Quantitative differential analysis
    tool: R scripts (included with MANTI)
    algorithm: limma-based linear modeling or t-test
    parameters: p_value_threshold=0.05, log2_fold_change_threshold=1.0, adjust_method=Benjamini-Hochberg
    input: MANTI-annotated peptide abundance table (LFQ intensities)
    output: differentially abundant N-terminal peptides, classified by origin
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Functional interpretation and visualization
    tool: R scripts (included with MANTI)
    algorithm: custom summarization and plotting
    parameters: plot_types=['barplot', 'heatmap', 'volcano', 'PCA']
    input: differential N-termini results + annotation metadata
    output: summary plots and tables (e.g., origin distribution, enriched protease families)
    confidence: low
