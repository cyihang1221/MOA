paper_title: Statistically sound identification of compounds by low-resolution GC-MS: Identification of tear agents in tear gas sprays.

overall_reproducibility_score: 40/100

workflow_steps: 3
  Step 1: Monte Carlo simulation of ion abundance ratios
    tool: Microsoft Excel
    algorithm: Monte Carlo Method (MCM) with Cholesky decomposition
    parameters: n_simulations=10000, covariance_matrix_source=experimental ion abundance data from calibrator and sample, confidence_level=0.95
    input: raw ion abundance data (characteristic ions) from GC-MS spectra
    output: statistically validated ion abundance ratio tolerance limits
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Retention time and mass spectral match evaluation
    tool: Microsoft Excel
    algorithm: joint statistical test combining retention time deviation and ion ratio deviations
    parameters: rt_tolerance_seconds=None, rt_confidence_level=0.95, ion_ratio_tolerance_method=multivariate MCM-derived confidence ellipsoid, min_ions_required=3
    input: GC-MS chromatograms (TIC or extracted ion chromatograms), peak-aligned retention times, characteristic ion abundances
    output: compound identification decision (match/no-match) with false identification rate control
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Comparison with SANTE criteria
    tool: Microsoft Excel
    algorithm: rule-based binary matching (SANTE 2021/692 guidelines)
    parameters: sanete_rt_tolerance=±0.05 min or ±0.5 % of RT, sanete_ion_ratio_tolerance=±10 % for base peak, ±20 % for other ions, sanete_min_ions=4, sanete_min_relative_intensity=10
    input: same GC-MS data used in step 2
    output: binary identification result per compound, false positive rate estimate
    confidence: high
