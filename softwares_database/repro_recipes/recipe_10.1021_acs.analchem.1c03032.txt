========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Automated Annotation of Untargeted All-Ion Fragmentation LC-MS Metabolomics Data with MetaboAnnotatoR.
source_paper: Graça G et al. (2022), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.1c03032, PMID: 35180347
overall_reproducibility_score: 88/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: AIF LC-MS data acquisition
    tool: LC-MS instrument (unspecified vendor)
    input: biological samples (human serum, other matrices)
    output: raw AIF .raw or .d files
    confidence: low
  Step 2: Data conversion to mzML
    tool: ProteoWizard (msconvert)
    parameters: 32bit=True, filter=['peakPicking', 'zeroSample', 'titleMaker']
    input: raw .raw or .d files
    output: .mzML files
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Feature detection and alignment
    tool: XCMS
    algorithm: centWave
    parameters: ppm=5, snthr=10, prefilter=[3, 100], peakwidth=[10, 60], noise=500, bw=10, mzdiff=-0.001, minfrac=0.5, max=50
    input: mzML files
    output: feature table (m/z, RT, intensity matrix)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Correlation-based parent-fragment linking
    tool: MetaboAnnotatoR (v1.0.0)
    algorithm: Pearson correlation + retention time consistency filtering
    parameters: cor_threshold=0.7, rt_window_sec=3, intensity_ratio_min=0.01, intensity_ratio_max=0.9
    input: XCMS feature table + MS1 and AIF MS/MS spectra
    output: linked parent-fragment pairs
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Molecular fragment matching and annotation
    tool: MetaboAnnotatoR (v1.0.0)
    algorithm: in-silico fragment matching against built-in fragment libraries
    parameters: mass_tol_ppm=10, library_match_score_min=0.6, min_fragment_matches=2
    input: linked parent-fragment pairs + fragment libraries
    output: annotated features (isotopologues, adducts, in-source fragments, metabolite candidates)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Ranking and prioritization of annotations
    tool: MetaboAnnotatoR (v1.0.0)
    algorithm: composite scoring (correlation strength × fragment match score × library confidence)
    parameters: top_n=5
    input: candidate annotations per feature
    output: ranked annotation list (top 1–5 per feature)
    confidence: high
