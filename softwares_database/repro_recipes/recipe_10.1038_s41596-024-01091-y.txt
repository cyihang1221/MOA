    tool: instrumental tuning and source parameter optimization

    input: prepared protein solutions
    output: sparse ion population suitable for charge detection
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Charge calibration curve generation
    tool: STORIboard
    algorithm: linear regression of m/z vs. 1/z using standard proteins with known charge states
    parameters: standards=['carbonic anhydrase', 'SILu antibody', 'pyruvate kinase', 'β-galactosidase']
    input: raw I²MS data from standard proteins
    output: charge calibration model (m/z ↔ z mapping)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Mass spectral output generation
    tool: STORIboard
    algorithm: STORI (Stochastic Trajectory Orbitrap Reconstruction Imaging) algorithm
    parameters: resolution_setting=12500, mass_domain_conversion=True
    input: raw I²MS data + charge calibration model
    output: mass distribution profile (mass domain spectrum)
    confidence: high
