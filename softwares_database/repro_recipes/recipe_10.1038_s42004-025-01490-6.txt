    algorithm: expert-driven spectral interpretation

  Step 7: Quantitative PG composition analysis
    tool: Custom calculation (formula-based)

    parameters: crosslinking_formula=0.5 * %dimers + (2/3) * %trimers, chain_length_formula=100 / (%anhydro_monimers + 0.5 * %anhydro_dimers + (1/3) * %anhydro_trimers), assumption=no di-anhydro species included
    input: relative abundances of muropeptide classes (monomers/dimers/trimers/anhydro forms)
    output: cross-linking index, average glycan chain length
    confidence: high
