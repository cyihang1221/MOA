========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: ChloroDBPFinder: Machine Learning-Guided Recognition of Chlorinated Disinfection Byproducts from Nontargeted LC-HRMS Analysis.
source_paper: Zhao T et al. (2024), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.3c05124, PMID: 38294426
overall_reproducibility_score: 88/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Molecular formula generation
    tool: XCMS
    algorithm: centWave
    parameters: ppm=5, snthr=10, prefilter=3, peakwidth=[10, 60]
    input: raw .mzML files
    output: feature table with m/z and RT
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Molecular formula prediction
    tool: SIRIUS
    algorithm: CSI:FingerID + CANOPUS
    parameters: mass_error_ppm=5, adducts=['[M+H]+', '[M+Na]+']
    input: feature table with m/z and RT
    output: candidate molecular formulas per feature
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Chlorine-containing molecular formula recognition
    tool: ChloroDBPFinder (v1.0.0)
    algorithm: hierarchical random forest
    parameters: n_trees=500, min_node_size=1, mtry=10
    input: candidate molecular formulas (MFs)
    output: Cl-MF classification (binary) and Cl-count annotation (multiclass)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Feature prioritization and filtering
    tool: ChloroDBPFinder (v1.0.0)
    algorithm: intensity-based and retention time consistency filtering
    parameters: min_intensity_ratio=3, rt_window_sec=5
    input: Cl-MF-annotated features
    output: high-confidence Cl-DBP features
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Tentative structural annotation via molecular networking
    tool: GNPS
    algorithm: Molecular Networking (Cosine score ≥ 0.7, min matched peaks ≥ 6)
    parameters: cosine_score_threshold=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, max_mass_diff_da=0.02
    input: MS/MS spectra of Cl-DBP features
    output: tentatively annotated structures via spectral matching and network propagation
    confidence: high
