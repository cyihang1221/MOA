    confidence: medium
  Step 5: MS/MS spectral interpretation for double bond regiochemistry

  Step 6: Cross-sample lipid profiling
    tool: Compound Discoverer (quantitative comparison) (v3.3 or later (inferred))

    parameters: samples_analyzed=9, strains=different hemp cultivars, normalization=internal standard not specified, filtering=presence in ≥80% of samples
    input: Feature table from Compound Discoverer
    output: Strain-comparative lipid profile (184 annotated lipids)
