========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: In silico prediction, LC-HRMS/MS analysis, and targeted/untargeted data-mining workflow for the profiling of phenylfentanyl in vitro metabolites.
source_paper: Di Trana A et al. (2021), Talanta, DOI: 10.1016/j.talanta.2021.122740, PMID: 34517608
overall_reproducibility_score: 42/100

workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: In silico metabolic prediction
    tool: GLORYx
    algorithm: rule-based biotransformation prediction
    input: chemical structure (SMILES or molfile of phenylfentanyl)
    output: predicted metabolite structures and biotransformation pathways
    confidence: high
  Step 2: In vitro metabolism incubation
    tool: human hepatocytes
    parameters: incubation_time=3 h, matrix=human hepatocyte suspension, controls=blank hepatocytes, solvent controls
    input: phenylfentanyl compound
    output: biological incubation samples (metabolite-containing supernatants)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: LC-HRMS/MS data acquisition
    tool: liquid chromatography-high-resolution tandem mass spectrometry
    parameters: instrument=Q-Exactive Orbitrap, ionization=electrospray ionization (ESI), polarity=['positive', 'negative'], resolution=70,000 FWHM (MS1), 17,500 FWHM (MS2), mass_range=m/z 50–1000, collision_energy=stepped HCD (20, 40, 60 eV)
    input: hepatocyte incubation extracts
    output: raw .raw files (Thermo format)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Targeted/untargeted data mining
    tool: data-mining software
    algorithm: custom targeted/untargeted workflow
    parameters: initial_feature_count_threshold=90000, automated_selection_threshold=115, filtering_criteria=['mass defect filtering', 'isotopic pattern matching', 'retention time consistency', 'fragmentation similarity to parent']
    input: LC-HRMS/MS raw files
    output: candidate metabolite list (115 entries) → manually verified metabolite table (13 confirmed)
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Metabolite identification and annotation
    tool: mzCloud and HighResNPS online libraries
    algorithm: spectral library matching
    parameters: match_score_threshold=≥70%, mass_tolerance_ppm=5, min_matched_peaks=3
    input: MS/MS spectra of candidate metabolites
    output: annotated metabolites with confidence levels (Level 1–2 per MSI guidelines)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Biological marker prioritization
    tool: manual expert curation
    algorithm: abundance + specificity + stability assessment
    parameters: priority_criteria=['peak intensity', 'uniqueness to phenylfentanyl pathway', 'absence in controls', 'chemical stability']
    input: confirmed metabolite list with MS/MS and RT data
    output: ranked biomarker candidates (e.g., phenylnorfentanyl as primary marker)
    confidence: low
