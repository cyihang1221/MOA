paper_title: Development of a multi-omics extraction method for ecotoxicology: investigation of the reproductive cycle of Gammarus fossarum.
source_paper: Faugere J et al. (2023), Talanta, DOI: 10.1016/j.talanta.2022.123806, PMID: 36113334
overall_reproducibility_score: 72/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Biphasic extraction
    tool: MTBE/Methanol biphasic extraction
    parameters: solvent_ratio=MTBE:methanol:water = 10:3:2.5 (v/v/v), extraction_cycles=2, centrifugation=10 min at 1000 × g, 4°C
    input: whole organism homogenate of Gammarus fossarum
    output: polar phase (proteins + metabolites) and apolar phase (lipids)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: LC-MS/MS data acquisition
    tool: Liquid chromatography tandem mass spectrometry
    parameters: instrument=Q-Exactive HF-X, column=Acquity UPLC BEH C18, gradient=not fully specified, ionization=ESI positive and negative mode, scan_range=m/z 70–1050, resolution=60,000 (MS1), 15,000 (MS2), targeted_method=True
    input: extracted polar and apolar phases
    output: raw .raw files
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Targeted data processing
    tool: Compound Discoverer (Thermo Fisher Scientific)
    algorithm: targeted workflow with library matching
    parameters: library=in-house and public MS/MS libraries, mass_tolerance_ppm=5, retention_time_window_min=0.5, min_peak_intensity=10000
    input: raw .raw files
    output: quantified compound table (proteins, metabolites, lipids)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Multivariate data integration
    tool: Multiple Factor Analysis (MFA)
    algorithm: MFA (a variant of PCA for multiple tables)
    parameters: scaling=center and scale per block, weighting=inverse of within-block inertia
    input: quantified compound tables per omics layer (proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics)
    output: integrated coordinates, component loadings, sample scores
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Supervised classification
    tool: Partial Least Squares Discriminant Analysis (PLS-DA)
    algorithm: PLS-DA
    parameters: n_components=3, cross_validation=7-fold, validation_metric=Q2
    input: quantified compound tables (per omics or integrated)
    output: discriminant models, variable importance (VIP) scores
    confidence: high
