========== sample_description&software_configuration_description entry ==========
sample_description: The samples used in the research were grab samples collected from the effluent of a wastewater treatment plant located in Warburg, Germany (Stadtwerke Warburg GmbH). These non-target screening samples are characterized by a high organic load, which induces significant matrix-associated effects in high-resolution mass spectrometry (HRMS), such as signal overlay—making them particularly suitable for robustness testing of the newly developed centroiding algorithm. Sample collection and preparation followed the detailed protocols published by Hohrenk et al. (2020) [24].
software_configuration_description: The instrumental raw data files—specifically Thermo *.raw files generated by the Q Exactive Orbitrap mass spectrometer—were converted to open-format profile mass spectra (*.mzXML) using ProteoWizard’s msConvert tool. Importantly, msConvert was used solely for format conversion and not for centroiding; no centroiding functionality or peak-picking algorithms were enabled during this step. The conversion preserved the full profile (non-centroided) nature of the spectra to retain all raw intensity and m/z point information required for subsequent regression-based centroiding. No additional parameters, filters, or preprocessing options (e.g., smoothing, deisotoping, or peak detection) were applied in msConvert, as explicitly stated in the text.
