workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: ROI extraction

    algorithm: region-of-interest detection

    output: list of ROIs (m/z, RT range, intensity profile)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Chromatographic peak profile preprocessing
    tool: AutoMS (custom)
    algorithm: linear interpolation + max-normalization
    parameters: profile_length=50, normalization_method=divide_by_max
    input: ROI peak profiles (ion abundance vs. scan index)
    output: normalized 1D vectors of length 50
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Denoising autoencoder inference
    tool: TensorFlow (v2.9.1)
    algorithm: denoising autoencoder (DAE)
    parameters: interference_std=0.2, interference_mean=0.0, encoder_conv_kernels=[8, 4], encoder_kernel_size=5, latent_vector_length=64, decoder_dense_channels=32, activation_function=ReLU (hidden), linear (output), loss_function=mean_squared_error, optimizer=Adam
    input: normalized 1D peak profile vectors
    output: reconstructed noise-deducted peak profiles
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Continuous peak quality scoring
    tool: AutoMS (custom score function)
    algorithm: reconstruction residual-based scoring
    parameters: scoring_metric=mean_absolute_error_or_mse_between_input_and_reconstruction
    input: input peak profile and reconstructed profile
    output: continuous peak quality score (0–1 or arbitrary scale)
    confidence: high
