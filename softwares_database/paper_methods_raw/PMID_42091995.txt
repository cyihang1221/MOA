
Methods and materials
This section first constructs a mechanical fault classification model
that incorporates SVM with multi-strategy improved SSA. The study
additionally suggests an mechanical FD approach based on SVM-
MSISSA with two-channel CNN to further increase the precision and
effectiveness of FD.
Fusion of SVM and multi-strategy improved SSA for
mechanical fault classification model construction
There are various types and manifestations of mechanical failures.
As an effective classification algorithm, SVM can effectively classify
sample data with different failure modes by finding the optimal
hyperplane. However, the parameters, including the kernel function
(KF) and penalty factor, affects how well SVM performs. Overfitting
of the model might result from unreasonable parameter values,
which compromises fault classification’s accuracy and
dependability19. Therefore, the study improves the SSA to construct
an method that fuses SVM and multi-strategy improved sparrow
search algorithm (SVM-MSISSA) for mechanical fault classification
to accurately classify mechanical faults. To improve the
performance of SVM in mechanical fault classification, MSISSA
adjusts the parameters of the model.

[image] denotes the parameters of Lévy distribution. Unlike Chaos-
SSA that mainly replaces random initialization with a chaotic
sequence, Lévy-SSA that only injects Lévy flight as a perturbation,
or adaptive SSA that focuses on step-size scheduling, the proposed
MSISSA explicitly binds (1) chaotic mapping to population
initialization for diversity control, (2) Lévy flight to iterative
updates for exploration enhancement, and (3) adaptive step-size to
global-local search balancing within a single optimization pipeline
dedicated to SVM hyperparameter tuning. Therefore, MSISSA
should be viewed as a stage-wise integrated and task-oriented
framework rather than a simple engineering concatenation of
common heuristics. Finally, the study identifies the mechanical
