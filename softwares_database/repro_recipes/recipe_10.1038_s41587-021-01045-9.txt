paper_title: High-confidence structural annotation of metabolites absent from spectral libraries.
source_paper: Hoffmann MA et al. (2022), Nature biotechnology, DOI: 10.1038/s41587-021-01045-9, PMID: 34650271
overall_reproducibility_score: 87/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: In silico structure database generation
    tool: SmiLib v2.0 (vv2.0)
    algorithm: combinatorial scaffold-building
    parameters: scaffolds=322, building_blocks=91, tautomer_standardization=PubChem standardization procedure, stereochemistry_handling=removed before database generation
    input: SMILES scaffolds and building blocks
    output: combinatorial structure database (e.g., 28,630 bile acid conjugates)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: MS/MS spectral preprocessing and feature extraction
    tool: SIRIUS
    algorithm: peak detection, deconvolution, formula annotation
    parameters: ion_mode=positive, instrument_types=['Orbitrap', 'QTOF']
    input: raw .mzML or vendor files (Q Exactive Plus, Sciex X500R)
    output: .ms files and MassBank records
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: In silico structure annotation via CSI:FingerID
    tool: CSI:FingerID
    algorithm: Input Output Kernel Regression (IOKR)
    parameters: training_datasets=['MassBank', 'GNPS', 'NIST 2017', 'MassHunter Forensics/Toxicology PCDL'], training_size=23965, structure_database=biomolecule DB (HMDB+ChEBI+KEGG+UNPD), PubChem, CASMI 2016 DB, SmiLib bile acid DB
    input: SIRIUS-processed MS/MS spectra (.ms files)
    output: candidate structure rankings with fingerprint similarity scores
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Confidence scoring and FDR control
    tool: COSMIC
    algorithm: kernel density P value estimation + directionally constrained SVM
    parameters: FDR_control=structure-disjoint cross-validation, novelty_definition=no identical 2D structure in training MS/MS data, stereochemistry_handling=ignored (2D only)
    input: CSI:FingerID candidate rankings + metadata
    output: high-confidence structural annotations with FDR estimates
    confidence: high
