paper_title: CAMERA: An Integrated Strategy for Compound Spectra Extraction and Annotation of Liquid Chromatography/Mass Spectrometry Data Sets
source_paper: Kuhl C et al. (2012), Analytical Chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/ac202450g, PMID: 22111785
overall_reproducibility_score: 72/100

workflow_steps: 7
  Step 1: Feature detection
    tool: XCMS
    algorithm: centWave
    parameters: snthresh=6, ppm=30, peakwidth=[5, 12], prefilter=[2, 200]
    input: raw .d/.raw files (Bruker MicrOTOF-Q-I)
    output: feature table (m/z, RT, intensity)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Feature alignment and retention time correction
    tool: XCMS
    algorithm: group.density
    parameters: bw=3, mzwid=0.015
    input: feature tables from multiple samples
    output: aligned feature table with matched features across samples
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Compound spectrum construction (retention time grouping)
    tool: CAMERA (vBioconductor release 2.4 (2009+), used in 2012 study)
    algorithm: RT window-based grouping (60% of FWHM around centroid)
    parameters: rt_window_factor=0.6
    input: XCMS-aligned feature table
    output: initial compound spectra (groups of co-eluting features)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Isotope peak detection
    tool: CAMERA
    algorithm: pairwise m/z distance + intensity ratio check
    parameters: mass_difference_ppm=1000, isotope_mass_tolerance_ppm=10, intensity_ratio_check=described in Supplementary Information S1
    input: compound spectra
    output: isotope-annotated features with charge state inference
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Graph-based compound spectrum refinement
    tool: CAMERA
    algorithm: Highly-connected-subgraphs (HCS) or label propagation community (LPC)
    parameters: scoring_function=Score(x,y) = CAS_xy + ISO_xy + (1/N) * sum_i(CPS_i_xy), correlation_method=Pearson, peak_shape_correlation_scope=EIC from sample with most intense feature (default) OR all samples, clustering_algorithm=['HCS', 'LPC']
    input: compound spectra with isotope annotations
    output: refined compound spectra (deconvolved co-eluting compounds)
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Adduct and fragment ion annotation
    tool: CAMERA
    algorithm: dynamic rule matching against ion species database
    parameters: mass_tolerance_relative_error=given relative error threshold (unspecified numerically), rule_sources=predefined lists of observable ions (e.g., [M+H]+, [M+Na]+, [M-H]−, [M+Cl]−, neutral losses)
    input: refined compound spectra
    output: annotated ion species (adducts, fragments) and molecular mass hypotheses
    confidence: low
  Step 7: Cross-polarity annotation verification
    tool: CAMERA
    algorithm: bipolar m/z difference matching against cross-polarity rule table
    parameters: retention_time_window=unspecified (but implied narrow window for co-elution), cross_polarity_rules=trusted combinations only (e.g., [M+H]+ ↔ [M-H]−)
    input: compound spectra from positive and negative ion mode datasets
    output: verified/updated molecular mass hypotheses and ion annotations
    confidence: low
