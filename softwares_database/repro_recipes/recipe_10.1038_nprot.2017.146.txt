========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Structural prediction of protein models using distance restraints derived from cross-linking mass spectrometry data.
source_paper: Orbán-Németh Z et al. (2018), Nature protocols, DOI: 10.1038/nprot.2017.146, PMID: 29419816
overall_reproducibility_score: 72/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Cross-link identification and validation
    tool: XlinkX or pLink or XiSearch
    algorithm: mass tolerance-based cross-link spectrum matching
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=10, fragment_tolerance_da=0.5
    input: raw .raw or .mzML files from XL-MS experiments
    output: validated cross-link list (residue–residue distance restraints)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Homology modeling with distance restraints
    tool: I-TASSER
    algorithm: iterative threading assembly simulation with restraint-guided refinement
    parameters: restraint_file_format=custom distance restraint file, num_models=5, use_restraints=True
    input: protein sequence + validated cross-link restraints
    output: ensemble of comparative structural models (PDB)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Model selection and scoring against XL data
    tool: Xwalk
    algorithm: distance-based model evaluation using cross-link violation analysis
    parameters: max_violation_distance_angstrom=25, violation_weight=1.0
    input: PDB models + cross-link restraints
    output: ranked model list with violation scores
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Protein–protein docking with cross-link restraints
    tool: HADDOCK
    algorithm: data-driven docking with ambiguous interaction restraints (AIRs) derived from cross-links
    parameters: air_lower_bound=0, air_upper_bound=35, restraint_type=distance, num_structures=1000, cluster_cutoff_angstrom=7.5
    input: subunit PDB structures + inter-subunit cross-link restraints
    output: docked complex models (PDB ensemble)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Docked model scoring and validation
    tool: HADDOCK scoring function
    algorithm: weighted sum of van der Waals energy, electrostatic energy, desolvation energy, and AIR violation penalty
    parameters: score_weight_vdw=1.0, score_weight_elec=0.2, score_weight_desolv=1.0, score_weight_air=1.0
    input: HADDOCK-generated docked models + AIR restraints
    output: ranked docked complexes with HADDOCK score and Z-score
    confidence: high
