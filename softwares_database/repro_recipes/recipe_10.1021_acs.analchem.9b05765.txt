    output: trained RT prediction model
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Retention time prediction model training
    tool: Retip R package
    algorithm: Keras (deep neural network)
    parameters: mean_absolute_error=0.57, chromatography_mode=RPLC
    input: molecular descriptors + experimentally measured retention times
    output: trained RT prediction model
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Retention time prediction for candidate compounds
    tool: Retip R package

    output: predicted retention times
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Integration with compound identification workflow
    tool: MS-DIAL and MS-FINDER

    output: ranked candidate annotations with RT support
    confidence: low
