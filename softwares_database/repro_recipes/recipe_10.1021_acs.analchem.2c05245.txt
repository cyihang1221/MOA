========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Quantifying Cell Heterogeneity and Subpopulations Using Single Cell Metabolomics.
source_paper: Liu R et al. (2023), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.2c05245, PMID: 37115510
overall_reproducibility_score: 30/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Single-cell metabolite acquisition
    tool: Single-probe single cell MS (SCMS)
    input: intact single cells
    output: raw mass spectra per cell
    confidence: low
  Step 2: Cell heterogeneity analysis and subpopulation identification
    tool: SinCHet-MS
    algorithm: unsupervised clustering + statistical prioritization
    parameters: min_cluster_size=5, distance_metric=cosine, clustering_method=HDBSCAN
    input: raw mass spectra per cell
    output: cell subpopulations with cluster assignments
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Metabolite biomarker prioritization per subpopulation
    tool: SinCHet-MS
    algorithm: differential abundance scoring (DAS) + stability filtering
    parameters: p_value_threshold=0.05, fold_change_threshold=1.5, stability_cutoff=0.8
    input: clustered spectral features (peak intensities per cell)
    output: ranked list of subpopulation-specific metabolite features
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Biological interpretation and marker validation
    tool: manual curation + literature matching
    algorithm: annotation by m/z and retention time (if available), MS/MS matching (not performed)
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=10, database=HMDB, METLIN
    input: prioritized metabolite features (m/z, intensity)
    output: putatively annotated metabolite biomarkers
    confidence: low
