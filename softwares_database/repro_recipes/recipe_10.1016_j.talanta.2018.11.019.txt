  Step 4: Stratified subsampling for batch effect removal
    tool: Custom stratified subsampling
    algorithm: stratified random subsampling by batch
    parameters: n_subsamples_per_batch=1, strata=['chemical', 'batch']
    input: batch-affected feature matrix
    output: batch-corrected feature matrix
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Extended steroidomic profiling and subgroup identification
    tool: Unsupervised clustering / similarity analysis
    algorithm: hierarchical clustering or PCA-based grouping
    parameters: distance_metric=Euclidean, clustering_method=average linkage
    input: batch-corrected steroid metabolite abundances
    output: chemical subgroups with similar steroid patterns
