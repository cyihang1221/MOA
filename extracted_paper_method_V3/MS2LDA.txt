sample_description: The sample used in the research was the marine sponge S. aurea, collected by hand at a depth of 4–7 m at the Wonderland Reef in the Florida Keys. Specimens were identified by visual inspection based on characteristic large oscula and a fleshy yellow interior, then frozen at −80 °C pending chemical extraction. Total DNA was isolated from RNAlater-preserved tissue for 18S rDNA amplification and Sanger sequencing to confirm taxonomic identity. Lyophilized sponge material was extracted with methanol (MeOH) at room temperature, yielding a bright green extract analyzed directly by LC-MS/MS without derivatization or prior fractionation. For microscopy and MALDI imaging, sponge tissue was embedded in 20% gelatin, sectioned at low temperature (−15 °C) into 8–10 μm thick cryosections using a cryomicrotome, and thaw-mounted onto prechilled conductive ITO slides. Sections were vacuum-desiccated for 60 min prior to fluorescence imaging or MALDI matrix application.
software_configuration_description: MS2LDA was employed as a web-based online tool to identify conserved structural motifs across MS/MS spectra in the S. aurea metabolome. The method involved tabulating MS2LDA motifs for all nodes within the smenamide cluster (e.g., motif 528, motif 480), followed by manual curation of motif conservation across the entire S. aurea molecular network. Motifs were defined as sets of common fragment ions and/or neutral losses corresponding to shared substructures among analogs. The analysis enabled detection of structural conservation even when spectral cosine similarity fell below the clustering threshold—thus connecting otherwise separate clusters (e.g., smenamide cluster and a second cluster containing nodes at m/z 532.276, 518.260, 504.245, and 461.202). Specific motif IDs (e.g., motif 528 in Supplementary Fig. S2a; motif 480 in Supplementary Fig. S4) were referenced, and motif sharing was validated via manual inspection of MS/MS spectra. No explicit software version, server URL, or computational parameters (e.g., minimum motif size, ion tolerance, or significance thresholds) were stated in the text beyond its use as an online tool integrated with GNPS infrastructure.

sample_description: The study used three types of biological samples: (i) 22 human urine samples, (ii) 19 beer samples, and (iii) fecal (stool) samples. For urine, 5 μL was extracted with 200 μL of chloroform/methanol/water (1:3:1 v/v/v) at 4 °C, vortexed for 5 min at 4 °C, centrifuged at 13,000 g for 3 min at 4 °C, and the supernatant stored at −80 °C. A pooled aliquot of the 22 urine samples was prepared prior to LC–MS analysis. For beer, the same metabolome extraction procedure was applied. For fecal samples, stool material was freeze-dried, and 5 mg of lyophilized material was extracted in 200 μL of chloroform/methanol/water (1:3:1) at 4 °C, homogenized using a FastPrep-24 homogenizer for 60 s at stroke setting 5, then vortexed, centrifuged, and processed identically to urine. All samples were analyzed via LC–MS/MS using HILIC chromatography and Orbitrap-based detection.
software_configuration_description: The MS2LDA+ model is an extension of standard Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) designed for mass spectrometry fragmentation data, where a single shared set of Mass2Motifs (topics) is inferred across multiple samples. It employs a Variational Bayes inference scheme in which each sample is modeled with standard MS2LDA, but motif updates are pooled across all samples. The model outputs Mass2Motifs (collections of co-occurring fragment ions and neutral losses) and per-sample prevalence vectors α_f, where each element α_k^f represents the proportion of total feature intensity in sample f explained by Mass2Motif k. The implementation includes two complementary linking scores: (1) Mass2Motif–molecule probability (proportion of a molecule’s spectrum explained by the Mass2Motif), and (2) overlap score (proportion of the Mass2Motif present in the molecule’s spectrum, i.e., how many Mass2Motif fragments/losses are observed). The model enforces that every MS2 spectrum is assigned to at least one Mass2Motif. The software implementation is available on GitHub (https://github.com/sdrogers/lda); no specific version or command-line parameters are stated, but inference details are described in Supporting Information section S4. Input data consisted of .mzXML (full-scan) and .mzML (fragmentation) files preprocessed with XCMS, MzMatch, and RMassBank for peak detection, MS1–MS2 pairing, and alignment.
