    parameters: context_variables=['polarity', 'ionization_source', 'mass_analyzer', 'mz_class', 'sample_type', 'species'], kingdom_split=['Animal', 'Plant'], min_datasets_per_context=45, test_set_size_per_context=30, training_set_sizes_explored=[10, 20, 30, 40, 50], bottom_species_frequency_grouping_percentile=10, sample_type_whitelist=['Tissue', 'Whole Organism', 'Cells', 'Uncurated']

  Step 5: Reprocessing of training datasets with rule-based METASPACE
    tool: METASPACE (rule-based) (vv2020+)
    algorithm: MSM scoring (spatial isotope rho_spatial, spectral isotope rho_spectral, spatial chaos rho_chaos)
    parameters: annotation_database=CoreMetabolome, configuration_modifications=see Supplementary Note S1
    input: raw .imzML files (centroided)
    output: rule-based annotation results (MSM scores, FDR estimates)
    confidence: medium

    confidence: high
  Step 7: Decoy adduct sampling and robust FDR estimation
