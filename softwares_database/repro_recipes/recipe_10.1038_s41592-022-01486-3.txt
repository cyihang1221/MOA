========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: MSNovelist: de novo structure generation from mass spectra.
source_paper: Stravs MA et al. (2022), Nature methods, DOI: 10.1038/s41592-022-01486-3, PMID: 35637304
overall_reproducibility_score: 87/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Molecular formula and structural fingerprint prediction
    tool: SIRIUS + CSI:FingerID
    algorithm: CSI:FingerID fingerprint prediction; SIRIUS molecular formula assignment
    parameters: fingerprint_dimensionality=3609, mass_tolerance_ppm=20
    input: tandem mass spectrum (.mgf or .mzML)
    output: molecular formula (x_M) and structural fingerprint vector (x_F)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: De novo SMILES generation via encoder-decoder RNN
    tool: Custom Keras/TensorFlow model
    algorithm: LSTM-based encoder-decoder with auxiliary atom-counting and bracket-balancing constraints
    parameters: encoder_layers=3, decoder_layers=3, lstm_units_per_layer=512, beam_width=128, smiles_character_vocabulary_size=37, fingerprint_input_dim=3609, hydrogen_prediction_lstm_layers=2, use_augmented_feature_vector=True, termination_condition=atom_count_vector_norm_zero
    input: molecular fingerprint (x_F) and molecular formula (x_M)
    output: top-k valid SMILES strings (k ≤ 128)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: SMILES validation and dereplication
    tool: RDKit
    algorithm: SMILES parsing, canonicalization, and duplicate removal
    parameters: remove_stereochemistry=False, validate_valence=True
    input: raw SMILES strings from RNN decoder
    output: unique, chemically valid SMILES strings
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Re-ranking by modified Platt score
    tool: Custom implementation (based on CSI:FingerID)
    algorithm: Platt-calibrated cosine similarity between predicted fingerprint (x_F) and deterministic fingerprint of candidate structure
    parameters: fingerprint_type=ECFP4, radius=2, bit_length=2048
    input: valid SMILES strings and query fingerprint (x_F)
    output: re-ranked list of candidate structures with modified Platt scores
    confidence: medium
