  Step 2: Cl/Br-specific feature screening
    tool: custom Python/R scripting

    input: raw mass spectra

    confidence: low
  Step 3: Isotopologue distribution similarity scoring
    tool: custom scripting
    algorithm: cosine similarity analysis
    parameters: similarity_metric=cosine, reference_distribution=theoretically simulated Cl/Br isotopologue patterns
    input: candidate HOC features with measured isotopologue intensities
    output: similarity scores and filtered HOC formulae
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Elemental formula assignment
    tool: custom scripting + elemental composition databases
    algorithm: mass defect filtering + heteroatom constraint (Cl/Br/O/N/S/P)
    parameters: max_elements={'Cl': 10, 'Br': 10, 'O': 20, 'N': 5, 'S': 3, 'P': 2}, mass_error_ppm=1.0, elemental_constraints=['Cl', 'Br', 'O', 'N', 'S', 'P']
    input: filtered m/z features with high cosine similarity
    output: assigned molecular formulae (824 HOCs)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Tentative structural elucidation
    tool: literature-based interpretation + MS/MS (if available) + functional group knowledge
    algorithm: pattern recognition of halogenated compound classes
    parameters: compound_classes_targeted=['halogenated phenols', 'carboxylic acids', 'benzenesulfonic acids']
    input: assigned formulae + retention behavior (not specified) + literature databases
    output: tentatively annotated structures (dozens), including 3 novel OClBrs
    confidence: low
