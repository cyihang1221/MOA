workflow_steps: 3
  Step 1: Adduct and fragment ion annotation using covariant ion analysis
    tool: COVINA
    algorithm: covariance-based ion grouping
    parameters: min_correlation_coefficient=0.95, ion_intensity_threshold=top 1000 ions, metabolite_list=['10 reference metabolites']
    input: LC-MS raw data (HILIC separation, high-salt mobile phase)
    output: annotated adduct/fragment ion table with covariance scores
    confidence: low
  Step 2: In-source CID ramping (InCIDR) acquisition
    tool: LC-MS instrument (Q-TOF or similar)
    algorithm: ramped in-source collision energy
    parameters: collision_energy_range=10–100 eV, step_increment=5 eV, dwell_time_per_step=100 ms
    input: HILIC-LC-MS raw data
    output: energy-resolved intensity profiles for adduct/fragment ions
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Ion form discrimination (protonated vs. deprotonated vs. adduct)
    tool: custom analysis
    algorithm: intensity profile shape comparison across InCIDR ramp
    parameters: profile_similarity_threshold=Pearson r > 0.9, reference_ion_types=['[M+H]+', '[M-H]-', '[M+Na]+', '[M+K]+', '[M+NH4]+', 'cluster_adducts']
    input: InCIDR intensity profiles
    output: classified ion forms per metabolite
    confidence: low
