    output: raw MS1 and MS2 DIA data matrices
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Regions of Interest Multivariate Curve Resolution (ROIMCR)
    tool: ROIMCR
    algorithm: multivariate curve resolution (MCR) applied to user-defined regions of interest (ROIs)
    parameters: constraint=non-negativity, bilinear_structure_assumption=True, no_peak_matching_alignment_modeling=True
    input: raw MS1 and MS2 DIA data matrices
    output: resolved elution profiles and spectral profiles (MS1 & MS2) for all detectable components
    confidence: low
  Step 3: Compound annotation and identification
    tool: spectral library matching
    algorithm: direct comparison of ROIMCR-resolved MS1/MS2 spectra
    parameters: reference_sources=['standards', 'mass spectral libraries']
    input: ROIMCR-resolved MS1 and MS2 spectra
    output: annotated/identified compounds
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Quantification via calibration curves
    tool: calibration modeling
    algorithm: linear or nonlinear regression using ROIMCR elution profiles
    parameters: response_measure=integrated elution profile area/amplitude, calibration_samples=standard mixtures
    input: ROIMCR-resolved elution profiles + standard concentration data
    output: predicted concentrations in unknown samples
    confidence: medium
