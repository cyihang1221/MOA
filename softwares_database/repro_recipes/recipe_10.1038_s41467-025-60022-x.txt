paper_title: Expanding the landscape of aging via orbitrap astral mass spectrometry and tandem mass tag integration.
source_paper: Keele GR et al. (2025), Nature communications, DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-60022-x, PMID: 40404760
overall_reproducibility_score: 92/100

workflow_steps: 9
  Step 1: Raw data conversion
    tool: ReAdW.exe
    algorithm: modified version
    input: raw .raw files from Orbitrap Astral
    output: .mzXML files
    confidence: low
  Step 2: Database search and PSM identification
    tool: COMET
    algorithm: linear discriminant analysis (LDA) for FDR control
    parameters: precursor_mass_tolerance_ppm=50, product_ion_tolerance_da=0.03, fdr_threshold=0.01, static_modifications=['TMTpro on lysine and N-terminus (+304.2071 Da)', 'carbamidomethylation of cysteine (+57.021 Da)'], variable_modifications=['oxidation of methionine (+15.995 Da)']
    input: .mzXML files
    output: peptide-spectrum matches (PSMs) with LDA-scoring
    confidence: high
  Step 3: PSM filtering based on resolution and S/N
    tool: custom filtering strategy
    algorithm: peptide-spectrum match-based filtering
    parameters: min_resolution_ms2=45000, min_total_signal_to_noise_across_channels=1440, reporter_ion_match_tolerance_da=0.001
    input: PSMs from COMET
    output: filtered PSMs
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Protein inference and quantification
    tool: protein parsimony rules
    algorithm: protein-level summarization by summing reporter ion intensities
    parameters: quantification_method=sum of TMT reporter ion counts across retained PSMs, normalization_method=channel-wise S/N-sum normalization to equalize total signal per channel
    input: filtered PSMs
    output: protein abundance matrix (log2-transformed)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Statistical modeling for age/sex effects (per tissue)
    tool: R (base stats)
    algorithm: log-linear regression with F-tests
    parameters: model_age_continuous=log2(protein) ~ intercept + age_months + sex, model_age_categorical=log2(protein) ~ intercept + factor(age_group) + sex, model_interaction=log2(protein) ~ intercept + age + sex + age:sex, fdr_method=Benjamini-Hochberg, p_value_threshold_fdr=0.1, model_selection_criterion=BIC
    input: protein abundance matrix
    output: differentially abundant proteins (age-, sex-, interaction-significant)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Cross-tissue joint modeling
    tool: R (base stats)
    algorithm: multi-tissue log-linear regression with interaction terms
    parameters: model_full=log2(protein) ~ intercept + age + sex + tissue + age:tissue + sex:tissue, fdr_method=Benjamini-Hochberg, p_value_threshold_fdr=0.1
    input: protein abundance matrix (5778 proteins × 70 samples)
    output: tissue-shared and tissue-specific age/sex effect estimates
    confidence: high
  Step 7: Principal component analysis
    tool: pcaMethods R package
    algorithm: PCA
    parameters: n_components=10, preprocessing=log2 transformation, no centering/scaling specified (implied by package defaults)
    input: protein abundance matrix (5778 × 70)
    output: PCA scores and loadings
    confidence: medium
  Step 8: Gene set enrichment analysis
    tool: clusterProfiler R package
    algorithm: GSEA (competitive enrichment)
    parameters: gene_set_definition=['age_coefficient > 0 & padj < 0.1', 'categorical age trends (e.g., Down-Flat)'], fdr_threshold_gsea=0.1, fdr_threshold_trends=0.01, identifier_mapping={'GO': 'ENSEMBL', 'KEGG': 'UniProt'}, background_gene_set=all analyzed proteins per tissue
    input: differentially abundant protein lists + metadata
    output: enriched GO terms and KEGG pathways
    confidence: high
  Step 9: Re-analysis of external datasets
    tool: R (custom scripts)
    algorithm: consistent statistical modeling pipeline applied to external DDA (Takasugi et al.) and DIA (Wang et al.) data
    parameters: takasugi_processing=TMT 16-plex, Lumos, 4 age groups, male-only, non-continuous age modeling, wang_processing=DIA, 3 early-life age groups, both sexes, missingness filter (>80% missing → removed)
    input: reprocessed external proteomic matrices
    output: harmonized differential protein lists for cross-study comparison
    confidence: medium
