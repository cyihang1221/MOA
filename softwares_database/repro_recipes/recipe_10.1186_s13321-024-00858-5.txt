========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: TransExION: a transformer based explainable similarity metric for comparing IONS in tandem mass spectrometry.
source_paper: Bui-Thi D et al. (2024), Journal of cheminformatics, DOI: 10.1186/s13321-024-00858-5, PMID: 38807166
overall_reproducibility_score: 87/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: MS/MS spectral preprocessing and pair generation
    tool: GNPS
    algorithm: spectral clustering and filtering
    parameters: min_peaks=6, min_matched_peaks=3, precursor_mz_tolerance=0.02, fragment_mz_tolerance=0.02
    input: raw .mzML or .mgf MS/MS spectra
    output: filtered spectral pairs with structural relationships (e.g., same compound, analogues, scaffolds)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Fragment relation detection via mass difference
    tool: TransExION (custom Transformer model) (v1.0)
    algorithm: Transformer encoder with learned fragment difference embeddings
    parameters: max_sequence_length=128, embedding_dim=256, num_heads=8, num_layers=4, dropout=0.1, mass_diff_tolerance_da=0.02
    input: paired MS/MS spectra (peak lists with m/z and intensity)
    output: similarity score + attention-based fragment correspondence map
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Post-hoc explanation generation
    tool: TransExION (integrated attention visualization) (v1.0)
    algorithm: attention-weighted fragment alignment explanation
    parameters: top_k_explained_pairs=5, min_attention_weight=0.05
    input: Transformer attention weights + input fragment m/z values
    output: human-readable fragment relation explanations (e.g., 'loss of H2O', 'shared indole substructure')
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Evaluation against structural similarity benchmarks
    tool: Tanimoto similarity (RDKit)
    algorithm: ECFP4 fingerprint comparison
    parameters: radius=2, nBits=2048
    input: SMILES strings of annotated compounds
    output: Tanimoto similarity matrix
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Molecular networking integration
    tool: GNPS Molecular Networking
    algorithm: cosine similarity (baseline) vs TransExION similarity
    parameters: edge_threshold=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, max_mz_tolerance=0.02
    input: MS/MS spectral similarity matrix
    output: network graph (nodes = spectra, edges = similarity > threshold)
    confidence: medium
