workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Isotopic peak detection and monoisotopic peak selection
    tool: rMSIannotation
    algorithm: Isotopic Likelihood Score (ILS)
    parameters: ils_components=['image_morphology_correlation', 'isotopic_intensity_ratio_validation', 'peak_centroid_mass_deviation'], carbon_isotope_ratio_modeling=based_on_HMDB_compounds
    input: raw MSI spectra (e.g., imzML or .ibd/.imzML files)
    output: monoisotopic peak assignments with ILS scores
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Adduct pair proposal
    tool: rMSIannotation
    algorithm: Adduct scoring based on three criteria
    parameters: adduct_scoring_components=['isotopic_pattern_coherence', 'image_correlation', 'mass_error']
    input: detected peaks (m/z, intensity, spatial coordinates)
    output: candidate adduct pairs with composite scores
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Validation against expert manual annotation
    tool: custom evaluation script
    algorithm: precision/recall comparison
    parameters: validation_metric=overlap_with_expert_annotations, threshold=90% replication rate reported
    input: rMSIannotation output vs. manually curated annotations
    output: validation metrics (e.g., % replicated manual annotations)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Comparison with METASPACE
    tool: METASPACE
    algorithm: library-based annotation (HMDB, LipidMaps)
    input: same raw MSI datasets
    output: library-matched compound annotations
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Data reduction via isotopic peak filtering
    tool: rMSIannotation + custom processing
    algorithm: removal of non-monoisotopic peaks
    parameters: filtering_criterion=retention of only monoisotopic peaks selected by ILS
    input: full peak list from MSI dataset
    output: reduced peak table (monoisotopic-only)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Downstream multivariate analysis (PCA and k-means)
    tool: standard multivariate tools (e.g., R stats/prcomp, cluster)
    algorithm: PCA, k-means segmentation
    parameters: n_components_pca=None, k_means_k=None, comparison_basis=biological variance retention before/after isotopic filtering
    input: intensity matrix (spatial × m/z) — full vs. monoisotopic-only
    output: PCA scores, k-means segmentation maps
    confidence: low
