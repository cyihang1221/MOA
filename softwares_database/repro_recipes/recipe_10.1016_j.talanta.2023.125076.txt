    input: HPLC-collected fractions
    output: purified monacolin K/lovastatin isolates

    tool: EA-IRMS

    output: δ¹³C values (‰)
    confidence: high

    tool: threshold-based decision rule

    input: δ¹³C values and quantified monacolin K/lovastatin concentrations
    output: adulteration classification (likely adulterated / likely natural)
    confidence: high
