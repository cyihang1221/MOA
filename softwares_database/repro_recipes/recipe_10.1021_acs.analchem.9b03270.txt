========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Quantitative Fragmentation Model for Bottom-Up Shotgun Lipidomics.
source_paper: Schuhmann K et al. (2019), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.9b03270, PMID: 31441640
overall_reproducibility_score: 78/100

workflow_steps: 3
  Step 1: Tandem mass spectrometry acquisition
    tool: mass spectrometer
    parameters: ionization_mode=negative, fragmentation_method=higher-energy collisional dissociation (HCD), analyte_form=molecular anions
    input: lipid extracts in solvent
    output: raw .raw or .wiff files (vendor format)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Carboxylate fragment detection and quantification
    tool: LipidXte
    algorithm: quantitative fragmentation model
    parameters: sn_position_correction=True, chain_length_dependence=True, double_bond_number_and_position_dependence=True
    input: tandem MS data (MS/MS spectra)
    output: adjusted carboxylate fragment abundances
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Absolute molar quantification of glycerophospholipids
    tool: LipidXte
    algorithm: postacquisition abundance harmonization
    parameters: standard_type=internal standards with structurally similar but non-identical fatty acyl chains, correction_target=unbiased absolute (molar) quantification
    input: adjusted carboxylate fragment abundances + internal standard responses
    output: absolute molar concentrations of glycerophospholipid molecular species
    confidence: high
