========== paper_workflow entry ==========
related_tool: ThermoRawFileParser
sample_description: The study analyzed seven compound libraries totaling 30,008 unique small molecules: NIHNP (3,988 natural products), OTAVAPEP (1,298 peptidomimetics), ENAMDISC (10,240 diverse compounds), ENAMMOL (4,378 compounds including a 4,000-carboxylic acid fragment library), MCEBIO (10,315 bioactive compounds), MCESCAF (5,000 scaffold compounds), and MCEDRUG (2,610 FDA-approved drugs). Compounds were pooled in multi-well plates (96- or 384-well) using automated liquid handlers (Opentrons OT-2, Beckman Coulter Echo 650, CERTUS FLEX), diluted in methanol/water (1:1) or methanol/acetonitrile/water (4:4:2), and injected via flow injection–Orbitrap MSⁿ analysis. Sample concentrations ranged from ~5 µM (NIHNP, concentration uncertain due to evaporation) to 20 µM (MCEBIO), with injection volumes of 2–3 µL.
complete_workflow_description: The end-to-end workflow consists of: (1) metadata clean-up using a custom Python script (metadata_cleanup_prefect.py) for structure standardization (via ChEMBL pipeline), salt removal, SMILES/InChI/InChIKey/logP/monoisotopic mass calculation, and optional database annotation (PubChem, ChEMBL); (2) sequence generation using sequence_creation.py to produce Xcalibur-compatible acquisition sequences with polarity-aware plate ordering and filename conventions embedding unique sample identifiers; (3) automated sample preparation via liquid handlers (OT-2, Echo 650, CERTUS FLEX) for pooling and dilution into multi-well plates; (4) flow injection–MSⁿ data acquisition on a Vanquish Horizon UHPLC coupled to an Orbitrap ID-X, using dual-pump gradient-free flow modulation (delivery and make-up pumps), H-ESI ionization, and data-dependent MSⁿ tree acquisition (MS¹ at 30,000 resolution, m/z 115–2000; top-3 precursors isolated with m/z 1.2 window, AGC targets and maxIT optimized per polarity); and (5) automatic MSⁿ tree library creation and data evaluation within MZmine, where cleaned metadata is linked to raw files via substring matching of unique sample identifiers embedded in filenames, enabling structural annotation and spectral tree curation. All steps are integrated into a reproducible, open-source pipeline designed for high-throughput generation of >2 million open MSⁿ spectra across 30,008 compounds.
source_paper: C et al. (2025), Nature methods, DOI: 10.1038/s41592-025-02813-0, PMID: 40954295
