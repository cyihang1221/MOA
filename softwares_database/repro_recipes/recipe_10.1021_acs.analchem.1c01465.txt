workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Fragmentation graph generation
    tool: CFM-ID 4.0 (v4.0)
    algorithm: Stochastic homogeneous Markov process with sequential ring cleavage modeling
    parameters: ring_cleavage_model=sequential_two_step, persistence_probability_intermediate_fragment=0.0, bond_break_feature_representation=single-bond-centric, fragmentation_graph_structure=directed_graph_with_self_loops
    input: molecular structure (SMILES or InChI)
    output: fragmentation graph (nodes = ion fragments, edges = transitions)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Transition probability estimation
    tool: CFM-ID 4.0 (v4.0)
    algorithm: Machine-learned parameter model trained on QTOF ESI-MS/MS spectra
    parameters: training_data_source=quadrupole time-of-flight (QTOF) ESI-MS/MS spectra, feature_basis=molecular topology-derived features, model_type=learned parameters from bond topology descriptors
    input: fragmentation graph + molecular topology features
    output: weighted fragmentation graph (edge weights = transition probabilities)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: In silico ESI-MS/MS spectrum prediction
    tool: CFM-ID 4.0 (v4.0)
    algorithm: Spectrum construction from weighted fragmentation graph
    parameters: ionization_mode=ESI+, precursor_ion=[M+H]+, spectral_resolution=unit mass resolution (implied), peak_intensity_calculation=path-probability aggregation over fragmentation pathways
    input: weighted fragmentation graph
    output: predicted ESI-MS/MS spectrum (m/z, intensity pairs)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Compound identification via spectral matching
    tool: CFM-ID 4.0 (v4.0)
    algorithm: Spectral similarity search against in silico reference spectral library
    parameters: matching_algorithm=standard spectral matching (e.g., dot product, cosine similarity), reference_library=CFM-ID 4.0–generated in silico spectral library, chemical_classes_covered=['acylcarnitines', 'acylcholines', 'flavonols', 'flavones', 'flavanones', 'flavonoid glycosides']
    input: experimental ESI-MS/MS spectrum
    output: ranked list of candidate compounds with spectral similarity scores
    confidence: medium
