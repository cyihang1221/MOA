workflow_steps: 6
  Step 1: Protein purification
    tool: Immunoprecipitation
    parameters: antibody=anti-A1AT antibody conjugated to agarose beads
    input: human serum samples
    output: purified α-1-antitrypsin (A1AT) protein
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Proteolytic digestion
    tool: Trypsin digestion
    input: purified A1AT protein
    output: glycopeptide mixture
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Nontargeted glycopeptide structural analysis
    tool: pGlyco2.0 (v2.0)
    algorithm: stepped HCD (higher-energy collisional dissociation)
    parameters: fragmentation_mode=stepped HCD, ms2_acquisition=nontargeted
    input: LC-HCD-MS/MS raw data (.raw or .mzML)
    output: identified glycopeptides (structures, sites, glycan compositions)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Targeted glycoform quantification
    tool: Skyline
    algorithm: Parallel Reaction Monitoring (PRM)
    parameters: transition_type=Y1 ion (peptide+GlcNAc), quantification_ion=Y1, target_glycopeptides=7
    input: LC-HCD-PRM-MS raw data
    output: relative/absolute quantification of site-specific glycoforms
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Statistical discrimination and biomarker evaluation
    tool: ROC analysis
    algorithm: Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) curve analysis
    parameters: metric=charge state ratio (2+/3+), classifier=early-stage HCC vs cirrhosis, auc_threshold=0.9
    input: quantified glycoform data (e.g., charge state ratios)
    output: AUC value, diagnostic performance metrics
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Glycan linkage inference
    tool: Bioinformatic interpretation
    algorithm: Sialic acid/galactose linkage hypothesis generation
    parameters: linkage_inference_basis=differential glycopeptide isomer behavior
    input: Structural glycopeptide identifications (pGlyco2.0 output) + quantification patterns
    output: Hypothesis on sialyl-T/Galectin-3–related linkage differences
    confidence: low
