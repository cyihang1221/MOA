========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: <i>De Novo</i> Cleaning of Chimeric MS/MS Spectra for LC-MS/MS-Based Metabolomics.
source_paper: Zhao T et al. (2023), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.3c00736, PMID: 37603462
overall_reproducibility_score: 87/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Chimeric MS/MS spectrum simulation and impact assessment
    tool: Custom simulation framework
    algorithm: In silico chimeric spectrum generation
    parameters: include_contamination_models=True, vary_isolation_window=[1.0, 2.0, 4.0]
    input: Simulated LC-MS/MS datasets with controlled chimerism
    output: Assessment of spectral similarity degradation and molecular networking distortion
    confidence: low
  Step 2: Feature engineering from chemical standards MS/MS
    tool: Custom R/Python scripts
    algorithm: Fragment peak attribute extraction
    parameters: intensity_ratio_fluctuation_threshold=0.3, appearance_rate_threshold=0.2, relative_intensity_threshold=0.05
    input: MS/MS spectra of chemical standards
    output: Labeled fragment dataset (true/false)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Machine learning model training and selection
    tool: XGBoost
    algorithm: Gradient boosting decision tree
    parameters: n_estimators=100, max_depth=6, learning_rate=0.1, eval_metric=pr-auc
    input: Labeled fragment dataset with three attributes
    output: Trained binary classifier (true vs false fragment)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Chimeric MS/MS spectrum cleaning
    tool: DNMS2Purifier
    algorithm: XGBoost-based fragment filtering
    parameters: min_precursor_correlation=0.6, score_threshold=0.7
    input: Raw or processed MS/MS spectra (e.g., .mzML, .mgf)
    output: Purified MS/MS spectra (cleaned .mgf or feature table with cleaned spectra)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Downstream metabolite annotation and molecular networking
    tool: GNPS (Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking)
    algorithm: Molecular networking with cosine score
    parameters: cosine_score_threshold=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, max_shift_mz=0.02
    input: Purified MS/MS spectra (.mgf)
    output: Molecular network clusters, spectral match reports
    confidence: high
