paper_title: Annotation of natural product compound families using molecular networking topology and structural similarity fingerprinting.
source_paper: Morehouse NJ et al. (2023), Nature communications, DOI: 10.1038/s41467-022-35734-z, PMID: 36658161
overall_reproducibility_score: 78/100

workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Molecular network construction
    tool: GNPS
    algorithm: cosine similarity scoring
    parameters: precursor_mass_tolerance_da=2.0, fragment_mass_tolerance_da=0.5, cosine_score_threshold=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, top_n_similar_nodes=10, max_molecular_family_size=100, msms_filter_window_da=50, top_n_fragment_ions=6, msms_precursor_exclusion_window_da=17
    input: raw .mzML or .mgf files
    output: GraphML molecular network
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Structural similarity network generation for fingerprint selection
    tool: RDKit
    algorithm: Morgan (ECFP4), MACCS, or atom-pair fingerprints with Sørensen–Dice or Tanimoto coefficients
    parameters: fingerprint_radius=2, similarity_coefficient=Dice, similarity_cutoff_range_min=0.01, similarity_cutoff_range_max=1.0, edge_retention_threshold_excluded=0.995
    input: SMILES strings from NPAtlas/NIH Natural Products Library
    output: structural similarity network (GraphML or adjacency matrix)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Compound family annotation via SNAP-MS
    tool: SNAP-MS
    algorithm: Morgan fingerprinting (radius=2) + Dice similarity clustering
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=None, min_cluster_size=None, max_node_count=2000, max_edge_count=10000, dice_similarity_cutoff=0.71, taxonomic_filtering_enabled=True, adducts_included=True, fragments_included=True
    input: GNPS GraphML network file or CSV mass list
    output: annotated GraphML network or Cytoscape session (.cys)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Validation of compound family predictions
    tool: NMR spectroscopy, co-injection with standards, LC-MS retention time matching
    algorithm: experimental orthogonal validation
    input: annotated subnetworks and prefractions
    output: validated compound family assignments
    confidence: high
