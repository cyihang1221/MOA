  Step 1: NMR data acquisition

    parameters: field_strength=600, unit=MHz, pulse_sequence=1D NOESYPR1D, temperature=298, unit_temperature=K, relaxation_delay=4, unit_relaxation_delay=s, acquisition_time=2.73, unit_acquisition_time=s, scans=64, spectral_width=20.02, unit_spectral_width=ppm
    input: liquid-state 1H NMR raw FID files (.fid)
    output: processed 1D NMR spectra (.jdx or .csv)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Spectral preprocessing
    tool: TopSpin 3.6.3 (v3.6.3)
    algorithm: Fourier transform, phase correction, baseline correction, chemical shift referencing (TSP at 0 ppm)
    parameters: zero_filling=64, apodization=exponential multiplication (LB = 0.3 Hz), phasing=manual or automatic, referencing=TSP internal standard
    input: raw FID files
    output: preprocessed 1H NMR spectra (intensity vs. ppm)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Data reduction and bucketing
    tool: MATLAB R2021a (vR2021a)
    algorithm: Binning (bucketing) with adaptive bin width
    parameters: bin_width_ppm=0.004, region_ppm=[0.5, 10.0], exclusion_regions_ppm=[[4.7, 4.9], [2.15, 2.25]], normalization=Probabilistic Quotient Normalization (PQN)
    input: preprocessed NMR spectra
    output: binned intensity matrix (samples × bins)
    confidence: medium
  Step 4: Multivariate Curve Resolution–Alternating Least Squares (MCR-ALS)
    tool: Custom MATLAB toolbox (developed in-house)
    algorithm: MCR-ALS with selectivity constraints
    parameters: number_of_components=10, closure_constraint=True, nonnegativity_constraint=True, selectivity_constraints=reconstructed pure profiles used as initial estimates and targets, convergence_threshold=1e-06, max_iterations=500
    input: binned intensity matrix or full spectra
    output: resolved pure component profiles (spectral) and concentrations (sample-wise)
    confidence: low
  Step 3.1: Alternative MCR-ALS on manually-defined spectral intervals
    tool: Custom MATLAB toolbox (developed in-house)
    algorithm: MCR-ALS applied per interval
    parameters: intervals_ppm=[[0.5, 1.5], [1.5, 2.0], [2.0, 2.5], [2.5, 3.0], [3.0, 3.5], [3.5, 4.0], [4.0, 4.7], [4.9, 5.5], [5.5, 6.5], [6.5, 10.0]], number_of_components_per_interval=2
    input: preprocessed NMR spectra
    output: interval-specific resolved profiles and concentrations
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Metabolite identification and annotation
    tool: Chenomx NMR Suite 9.0 (v9.0)
    algorithm: Spectral fitting and database matching
    parameters: library=Chenomx Human Metabolome Database (HMDB)-based library for dairy, fitting_range_ppm=[0.5, 10.0], tolerance_ppm=0.02, quantification_method=relative concentration (arbitrary units)
    input: resolved pure profiles (from MCR-ALS) and/or binned spectra
    output: putatively annotated metabolite features with relative concentrations
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Multivariate statistical analysis
    tool: MATLAB R2021a + custom scripts (vR2021a)
    algorithm: PCA, PLS-DA, OPLS-DA
    parameters: cross_validation=7-fold, permutation_test=200, model_validation=['R2X', 'R2Y', 'Q2Y'], scaling=Pareto scaling
    input: metabolite feature table (concentrations or binned intensities)
    output: discriminatory models, loadings, VIP scores, classification results
    confidence: medium
