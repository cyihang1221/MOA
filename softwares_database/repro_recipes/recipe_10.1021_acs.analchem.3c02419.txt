========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Identification of Xenobiotic Biotransformation Products Using Mass Spectrometry-Based Metabolomics Integrated with a Structural Elucidation Strategy by Assembling Fragment Signatures.
source_paper: Chen YC et al. (2023), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.3c02419, PMID: 37713273
overall_reproducibility_score: 87/100

workflow_steps: 7
  Step 1: UHPLC-HRMS data acquisition
    tool: UHPLC-HRMS
    parameters: instrumentation=Q-Exactive Orbitrap, ionization_mode=positive and negative ESI, mass_resolution=70000, mass_accuracy=5 ppm, scan_range_mz=[100, 1200], collision_energy=stepped NCE: 10, 20, 40 eV
    input: biological samples (urine, liver microsomes)
    output: raw .raw files
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Data conversion and preprocessing
    tool: ProteoWizard
    algorithm: msconvert
    parameters: output_format=mzML, 32bit_precision=True
    input: raw .raw files
    output: converted .mzML files
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Feature detection and alignment
    tool: XCMS (v3.18.0)
    algorithm: centWave
    parameters: peakwidth=[10, 60], ppm=5, snthresh=10, prefilter=[3, 100], noise=1000, bw=30, mzdiff=0.001, minfrac=0.5, maxfrac=1, intensity=5000
    input: .mzML files
    output: feature table (m/z, RT, intensity)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Mass defect filtering (MDF)
    tool: Custom R script
    algorithm: mass defect-based biotransformation filtering
    parameters: mass_defect_tolerance_da=0.005, elemental_composition_constraints=['C', 'H', 'O', 'N', 'S', 'P', 'Cl', 'F', 'Br', 'I']
    input: feature table with m/z and adduct annotation
    output: MDF-filtered candidate features
    confidence: low
  Step 5: Multiple-group comparison for differential feature selection
    tool: MetaboAnalyst (v5.0)
    algorithm: t-test + FDR correction
    parameters: p_value_threshold=0.05, fdr_method=Benjamini-Hochberg, fold_change_threshold=2.0, group_comparison=control vs. treated
    input: normalized feature table
    output: differentially abundant features
    confidence: high
  Step 6: MS/MS spectral processing and fragment signature assembly
    tool: FragAssembler (v1.0)
    algorithm: fragment signature assembly from MS/MS
    parameters: min_fragment_intensity_ratio=0.01, max_fragment_count_per_signature=10, moiety_mass_tolerance_ppm=5, allowed_biotransformation_reactions=['hydroxylation', 'dehydrogenation', 'glucuronidation', 'sulfation', 'N-acetylation', 'oxidation', 'demethylation']
    input: MS/MS spectra of candidate features
    output: assembled fragment signatures + modified moiety assignments
    confidence: high
  Step 7: Candidate structure filtering and ranking
    tool: FragAssembler (v1.0)
    algorithm: reaction-aware structural filtering
    parameters: exclude_nonbiological_transformations=True, isomer_discrimination_enabled=True, ranking_metric=signature_match_score
    input: BioTransformer 3.0 candidate structures + FragAssembler signatures
    output: ranked, high-confidence biotransformation product structures
    confidence: high
