========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Direct glycosylation analysis of intact monoclonal antibodies combining ESI MS of glycoforms and MALDI-in source decay MS of glycan fragments.
source_paper: Senini I et al. (2024), Communications chemistry, DOI: 10.1038/s42004-024-01297-x, PMID: 39261598
overall_reproducibility_score: 42/100

workflow_steps: 10
  Step 1: Sample preparation for ESI-MS
    tool: Amicon® Ultra centrifugal filters
    algorithm: buffer exchange
    parameters: molecular_weight_cutoff=30000, diluent=5:94.9:0.1 v/v ACN/H2O/TFA, centrifugation_speed_rpm=8000, centrifugation_time_min=12, number_of_washes=3, final_diluent=50:49.9:0.1 ACN/H2O/TFA
    input: purified mAb in PBS or storage buffer
    output: buffer-exchanged mAb solution ready for direct infusion ESI-MS
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Sample purification for MALDI-ISD
    tool: Capture Select™ FcXL affinity matrix
    algorithm: affinity capture and elution
    parameters: bead_volume=10 µL, binding_buffer=50 mM ammonium bicarbonate, incubation_time_min=30, incubation_speed_rpm=900, wash_buffer_volume_per_wash=100 µL, number_of_washes=3, elution_buffer=100 mM formic acid, elution_time_min=5, elution_speed_rpm=900, final_concentration=1 µg/µL
    input: buffer-exchanged mAb solution
    output: purified, salt-free mAb solution in 100 mM FA
    confidence: high
  Step 3: MALDI matrix spotting and sample preparation
    tool: AnchorChip MALDI target plate
    algorithm: co-crystallization with sodium-doped matrix
    parameters: matrix_composition=1,5-DAN/DHB/NaHCO3 at 2:10:5 mM, matrix_preparation_solvent=1:1 ACN/H2O, spot_volume_mAb=1 µL, spot_volume_matrix=1 µL, washing_step=cold water wash to remove excess salt
    input: purified mAb solution (1 µg/µL)
    output: dried MALDI target spot with co-crystallized mAb and sodium-doped matrix
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Direct infusion ESI-FT-ICR MS of intact glycoforms
    tool: Bruker solariX 2xR FT-ICR MS
    algorithm: intact mass deconvolution
    parameters: instrument_field_strength_T=15, infusion_rate_μL_h=120, mz_range=[398.18, 5000], data_points=131072, skimmer_voltage_V=130, scans_per_sample=1000–1500, deconvolution_tool=Maximum Entropy, instrument_resolving_power=5000, resolution_setting=high
    input: buffer-exchanged mAb solution
    output: deconvoluted intact mass spectrum (glycoform distribution)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: MALDI-ISD FT-ICR MS for glycan fragment profiling
    tool: Bruker solariX 2xR FT-ICR MS
    algorithm: in-source decay (ISD) fragmentation
    parameters: laser_system=Smartbeam-II, laser_frequency_Hz=500, laser_shots_per_scan=200, mz_range=[918, 5000], data_points=1048576, scans_summed=40, fragment_ion_selection=<sup>0,2</sup>A ions, intensity_calculation=sum of first three isotopic peaks
    input: MALDI-spotted mAb
    output: glycan profile (relative abundance of <sup>0,2</sup>A fragment ions)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: MALDI-ISD-CID tandem MS for structural confirmation
    tool: Bruker solariX 2xR FT-ICR MS
    algorithm: collision-induced dissociation
    parameters: mz_ranges=[[300, 8000], [90, 8000]], data_points=[1048576, 2097152], collision_energy_V=55–90 (optimized per precursor)
    input: selected <sup>0,2</sup>A fragment ions from ISD spectra
    output: tandem MS spectra for glycan structural assignment
    confidence: medium
  Step 7: Bottom-up LC-MS/MS glycopeptide analysis
    tool: Thermo Exploris480 + Ultimate3000nano
    algorithm: in-gel tryptic digestion + nanoLC-MS/MS
    parameters: gel_electrophoresis=4–12% Bis-Tris PAGE, reduction_agent=10 mM DTT, alkylation_agent=50 mM iodoacetamide, digestion_enzyme=trypsin (12.5 ng/µL), digestion_time_h=16, digestion_temperature_C=37, extraction_solvent=50/50/0.1 v/v/v water/acetonitrile/formic acid, LC_gradient=2–40% solvent B over 30 min, solvent_B_composition=20/80/0.1 v/v/v water/acetonitrile/formic acid, MS1_resolution=120000, MS1_scan_range_mz=[400, 3500], AGC_target=standard, max_fill_time_ms=50, lock_mass_mz=445.12
    input: intact mAb
    output: glycopeptide identifications and site-specific glycoform abundances
    confidence: high
  Step 8: LC-fluorescence detection (LC-FD) of released glycans
    tool: Acquity UPLC Glycan BEH Amide Column
    algorithm: PNGase F release + 2-AB labeling + HILIC separation
    parameters: enzyme=PNGase F (500,000 U/mL), deglycosylation_time_h=1, deglycosylation_temperature_C=45, labeling_reagent=2-AB, labeling_time_h=2, labeling_temperature_C=65, column=Waters Acquity UPLC Glycan BEH Amide, 1.7 μm, 2.1 × 150 mm, gradient_duration_min=47.5, eluent_A=100 mM ammonium formate, pH 4.5, eluent_B=acetonitrile, detection_mode=fluorescence, excitation_wavelength_nm=330, emission_wavelength_nm=420
    input: standard and glycoengineered trastuzumab
    output: quantitative glycan profile (HILIC-FD chromatogram)
    confidence: high
  Step 9: Data processing and annotation
    tool: DataAnalysis 5.0 SR1 (v5.0 SR1)
    algorithm: manual curation + mass list generation
    parameters: background_evaluation=visual, fragment_annotation_source=GlycoWorkbench 2.1 (theoretical N-glycan fragments), protein_fragment_annotation_source=ProteinProspector online tool
    input: raw MALDI-ISD FT-ICR spectra
    output: annotated glycan fragment table
    confidence: high
  Step 10: Glycopeptide quantification
    tool: Skyline (v23.1.0.268)
    algorithm: peak area integration
    parameters: software=Skyline (64 bit), quantification_method=signal intensity of glycopeptides
    input: LC-MS/MS raw files
    output: relative glycopeptide abundances
    confidence: high

reproducibility_gaps:

  - No code or scripts shared for data processing (e.g., Excel-based workflows, custom curation logic)
  - Exact gradient profiles for LC-MS/MS and LC-FD are incompletely specified (e.g., flow rate, column temperature, injection volume)
  - Collision energy optimization procedure for CID is described only as 'optimized for each precursor' — no calibration or criteria provided
  - No information on statistical repeatability metrics (e.g., CV%, %RSD) beyond '12–14 replicates'
  - Commercial software dependencies (DataAnalysis, Xcalibur, Skyline, Bruker ftmsControl) require licenses; Skyline is open but version-specific workflows not shared
