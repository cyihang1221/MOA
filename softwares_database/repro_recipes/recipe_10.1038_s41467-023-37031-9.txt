    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Sample preparation and extraction

    parameters: solvent_ratio=chloroform:methanol:water, temperature=ice bath, resuspension_solvent=3:2 acetonitrile:water, resuspension_volume=300 µL
    input: Frozen microbial cell pellets
    output: Dried polar/non-polar extract resuspended for LC-MS
    confidence: high
  Step 3: LC-IM-MS data acquisition
    tool: Agilent 6560 DTIMS-QTOF MS + Agilent 1290 UHPLC (vMassHunter v.B.09.00 (B9044.0))
    algorithm: Data-independent acquisition (DIA)
    parameters: column=Waters XBridge BEH Amide XP, 2.1 × 50 mm, 2.5 µm, column_temperature=50, injection_volume=10, autosampler_temperature=4, mobile_phase_A=10 mM ammonium acetate, 10 µM deactivator, pH 9.2, 90% water/10% ACN, mobile_phase_B=10 mM ammonium acetate, pH 9.2, 90% ACN, gradient_length_min=8.7, gradient_profile=[[0.0, 0.35, 90], [1.0, 0.35, 90], [1.1, 1.0, 85], [4.0, 0.75, 80], [5.0, 0.75, 40], [6.5, 0.75, 40], [6.8, 0.75, 20], [7.0, 0.75, 20], [7.5, 0.75, 90]], equilibration_time_min=3.0, ion_mode=negative, mass_range_mz=[65, 1400], drift_gas=high-purity nitrogen, gas_temperature_C=325, drying_gas_flow_L_per_min=5, nebulizer_pressure_psi=30, calibration=Agilent ESI Tune solution before each batch
    input: Resuspended extracts
    output: Raw .d files (Agilent MassHunter format)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: SRM method development and quantification
    tool: Agilent 6490 QQQ + Skyline (vSkyline v.64.21.1.0.146)
    algorithm: Selected reaction monitoring
    parameters: ion_mode=negative, ion_spray_voltage_V=3000, capillary_inlet_temperature_C=225, gas_flow_ml_per_min=15, nebulizer_pressure_psi=20, sheath_gas_temperature_C=250, sheath_gas_flow_ml_per_min=11, mass_range_mz=[65, 1400], biological_replicates={'P_putida': {'mucK_PP2224': 2, 'others': 3}}
    input: Standard mixes and microbial samples
    output: Calibration curves and peak area integrations for targeted metabolites
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Metabolite annotation and profiling
    tool: PeakDecoder
    algorithm: Machine learning-based co-elution/co-mobility discrimination + identification error rate estimation
    parameters: input_data=LC-IM-MS DIA raw data (.d files), reference_library=64 standards, features_used=['retention time', 'collision cross-section (CCS)', 'm/z', 'intensity', 'peak shape', 'mobility drift time'], error_rate_estimation=built-in probabilistic model
    input: Raw LC-IM-MS DIA data
    output: Annotated feature table with confidence scores and error rates
    confidence: low
  Step 6: Validation against orthogonal platforms
    tool: GC-MS + SRM
    algorithm: Cross-platform quantitative validation
    parameters: GC-MS_method=as described previously (ref 52), SRM_validation_targets=subset of 2683 annotated features, validation_metrics=['correlation', 'recovery', 'LOD/LOQ']
    input: Same biological samples analyzed by GC-MS and SRM
    output: Manually validated metabolite identifications and quantification concordance
    confidence: medium
