========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Molecular Networking and On-Tissue Chemical Derivatization for Enhanced Identification and Visualization of Steroid Glycosides by MALDI Mass Spectrometry Imaging.
source_paper: Dreisbach D et al. (2022), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.2c02694, PMID: 36347515
overall_reproducibility_score: 58/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: LC-MS-based molecular networking
    tool: GNPS
    algorithm: Molecular Networking (cosine score)
    parameters: min_cosine_score=0.7, min_matched_peaks=6, max_mass_diff=50
    input: LC-MS/MS spectra (.mzML)
    output: molecular families and putative compound classes
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: AI-driven chemical characterization
    tool: SIRIUS + CSI:FingerID
    algorithm: Structure elucidation via fragmentation tree analysis and fingerprint prediction
    parameters: mass_accuracy_ppm=5, fragmentation_model=MS2
    input: LC-MS/MS spectra with high mass accuracy
    output: structural annotations for steroidal glycosides
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: On-tissue chemical derivatization (OTCD) for MALDI MSI
    tool: custom protocol
    algorithm: chemical derivatization with Girard's reagent T
    parameters: derivatization_time_min=30, temperature_C=37, reagent_concentration_mM=100, matrix=DHB, laser_frequency_Hz=1000, pixel_size_um=[2, 5]
    input: fresh-frozen tissue sections
    output: derivatized MALDI MSI data (.imzML)
    confidence: low
  Step 4: MALDI MSI data acquisition and preprocessing
    tool: SCiLS Lab
    algorithm: peak picking, noise filtering, spatial smoothing
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=10, snr_threshold=3, smoothing_kernel=Gaussian_3x3
    input: raw .imzML files
    output: ion images and peak list
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Metabolite annotation and spatial visualization
    tool: MSiReader
    algorithm: m/z-based ion image generation and overlay
    parameters: mz_tolerance_ppm=10, normalization=TIC
    input: preprocessed .imzML and annotated m/z list
    output: spatial ion images of steroidal glycosides
    confidence: high
