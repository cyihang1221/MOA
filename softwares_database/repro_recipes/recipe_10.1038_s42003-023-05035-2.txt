workflow_steps: 8
  Step 1: Data acquisition via intraoperative microdialysis
    tool: M Dialysis 71 High Cut-Off Brain Microdialysis Catheters

    output: raw microdialysate samples
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Targeted D/L-2-HG quantification
    tool: Mayo Clinic Metabolomic Core LC/MS
    algorithm: derivatisation-based LC/MS quantification
    parameters: isomer_resolution=D and L enantiomers separately quantified
    input: microdialysate samples
    output: quantified D-2-HG and L-2-HG concentrations
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Untargeted metabolomic profiling
    tool: Metabolon, Inc. UPLC-MS/MS platform
    algorithm: ultra-performance liquid chromatography tandem mass spectrometry
    input: microdialysate samples
    output: untargeted peak area matrix (162 named metabolites)
    confidence: low
  Step 4: Fold-change ranking (tumour vs brain)
    tool: custom R/Python script (not specified)
    algorithm: fold-change calculation (X/Z or Y/Z)
    parameters: minimum_sample_presence=present in >90% of microdialysate samples, exclusion_rule=Oligo 2 excluded due to no brain catheter
    input: normalised peak area matrix
    output: ranked metabolite lists (enhancing tumour vs brain, non-enhancing vs brain, enhancing vs non-enhancing)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Bloodiness-associated metabolite ranking (CSF-based proxy)
    tool: custom analysis
    algorithm: fold-change ranking (bloody vs clean CSF) + quartile pooling
    parameters: CSF_bloody_quartile_n=7, CSF_clean_quartile_n=7, metabolite_presence_threshold_CSF=present in >85% of CSF samples, metabolite_presence_threshold_microdialysate=present in >90% of microdialysate samples, paired_CSF_sample_timing=within ~1 minute
    input: CSF metabolomics data (n=28 total, including ependymoma pair)
    output: bloodiness-ranked metabolite list
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA) for metabolites
    tool: GSEA 4.1.0 (v4.1.0)
    algorithm: rank-based enrichment scoring with custom .gmx metabolite sets
    parameters: fdr_threshold=0.05, enrichment_score_type=normalized enrichment score (NES), multiple_testing_correction=FDR, top_bottom_set_size=35
    input: ranked metabolite list + custom .gmx metabolite sets
    output: enrichment results (NES, p-value, FDR)
    confidence: high
  Step 7: Statistical testing and visualisation
    tool: MetaboAnalyst 5.0 (v5.0)
    algorithm: Spearman correlation, PCA, heatmap, volcano plot generation
    parameters: minimum_metabolite_presence=present in ≥40/44 catheters, normalisation_method=median = 1 per metabolite per batch, correlation_method=Spearman, volcano_plot_fc_threshold=2, volcano_plot_p_threshold=0.05
    input: normalised peak area matrix
    output: correlation maps, PCA plots, heatmaps, volcano plots
    confidence: high
  Step 8: Non-parametric statistical testing
    tool: GraphPad Prism 9.1 (v9.1)
    algorithm: Wilcoxon signed-rank test
    parameters: distribution_test=D'Agostino-Pearson normality test, paired_comparison=enhancing tumour vs brain (paired), significance_threshold=0.05
    input: normalised peak areas (paired samples)
    output: p-values, significance annotations
    confidence: high
