sample_description: The study utilized phenotypic and genotypic data from 27,481 Duroc pigs collected by Smithfield Premium Genetics between 2015 and 2021. Six complex traits were analyzed: three performance traits (Off-test Back fat thickness [BF, n=26,731, h²=0.371], Off-test Loin muscle depth [MS, n=26,731, h²=0.205], Off-test body weight [WT, n=27,360, h²=0.103]) and three reproduction traits (Number of piglets born alive [NBA, n=3,290, h²=0.096], Number of piglets born dead [NBD, n=3,290, h²=0.051], Number of piglets weaned [NW, n=3,290, h²=0.062]). All phenotypes were pre-adjusted for systematic environmental effects (e.g., farm, physiological sources) and standardized prior to analysis. Genotyping was performed using the PorcineSNP60 BeadChip (Illumina), followed by quality control in PLINK1.90 with filters: Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium P-value > 1E-8 and minor allele frequency > 0.01, resulting in 30,981 SNPs. Genotypes were coded as 0 (homozygous major allele), 1 (heterozygous), or 2 (homozygous minor allele), then normalized.
software_configuration_description: Scikit-learn's ridge regression was implemented using the 'Ridge' class from sklearn.linear_model. The regularization strength (lambda, λ) was determined via grid search over values corresponding to heritability estimates ranging from 0.3 to 0.6 in increments of 0.01, based on prior literature for back fat thickness; for other traits, λ was similarly inferred from their reported or estimated heritabilities. For each λ, five-fold cross-validation was performed to minimize mean squared error. The model used 20 CPU threads for parallel computation. Ridge regression was mathematically linked to GBLUP via the relationship λ = σₑ² / σ_g² × m, where σₑ² and σ_g² are residual and genetic variances, and m is the number of markers (30,981). No additional preprocessing beyond phenotype standardization and genotype normalization was specified for scikit-learn; default solver behavior was assumed unless overridden by cross-validation selection.

sample_description: The study included 60 human participants divided into four subgroups of 15 individuals each: (1) Co-no (healthy controls without uterine myoma), (2) Co-um (healthy controls with uterine myoma ≥2 cm confirmed by ultrasound showing a vortex-like structure), (3) IVL-no (intravenous leiomyomatosis patients without postoperative recurrence), and (4) IVL-re (IVL patients with postoperative recurrence defined as ≥1 cm space-occupying lesion in pelvic/abdominal veins or residual lesion enlargement on two consecutive ultrasounds). Inclusion criteria for IVL patients: (1) preoperative or intraoperative imaging/surgical confirmation of vascular-occupying lesions in abdominal/pelvic veins (parauterine reproductive vein, iliac vein, inferior vena cava) or right atrium; (2) surgical treatment at Peking Union Medical College Hospital; (3) postoperative pathological diagnosis of IVL with vascular invasion; (4) age ≥18 years. Exclusion criteria: (1) incomplete clinical data; (2) pregnancy or lactation; (3) intellectual disability or inability to self-care; (4) unwillingness to participate; (5) comorbid malignant tumors. Healthy control inclusion criteria: (1) age ≥18; (2) no history of malignant tumors; (3) non-pregnant and non-lactating; (4) no prior hysteromyomectomy or hysterectomy. All participants provided written informed consent; the study was approved by the Ethics Committee of Peking Union Medical College Hospital (JS-2654). Fasting venous blood (150 μL serum aliquots) was collected from the cubital vein after ultrasound examination and stored at −80°C until metabolomic analysis.
