========== reproducibility_recipe entry ==========
paper_title: Combination of Structure Databases, In Silico Fragmentation, and MS/MS Libraries for Untargeted Screening of Non-Volatile Migrants from Recycled High-Density Polyethylene Milk Bottles.
source_paper: Su QZ et al. (2023), Analytical chemistry, DOI: 10.1021/acs.analchem.2c05389, PMID: 37262310
overall_reproducibility_score: 82/100

workflow_steps: 7
  Step 1: MS/MS library construction
    tool: mspcompiler
    algorithm: library compilation and standardization
    input: in-house MS/MS spectra, public MS/MS libraries (e.g., MassBank, GNPS)
    output: compiled, standardized MS/MS library (.msp format)
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Data acquisition
    tool: liquid chromatography high-resolution mass spectrometry
    parameters: ionization_mode=ESI, polarity=['positive', 'negative'], resolution=high_resolution, mass_accuracy=sub-ppm
    input: extracts from rHDPE milk bottles
    output: raw .raw or .d files (vendor format)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Feature detection and alignment
    tool: XCMS
    algorithm: centWave
    parameters: peakwidth=[10, 60], ppm=5, snthr=10
    input: raw .raw/.d files
    output: feature table (m/z, RT, intensity)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: MS/MS spectral matching
    tool: mspcompiler + SpectraMatch
    algorithm: spectral similarity scoring (dot product, reverse dot product)
    parameters: min_match_peaks=3, min_similarity_score=0.7
    input: feature table with MS/MS spectra
    output: annotated compounds list (level 1–2 identification)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: In silico fragmentation and annotation of unknowns
    tool: CFM-ID or SIRIUS/CSI:FingerID
    algorithm: in silico fragmentation prediction
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=5, fragmentation_method=CFM, candidate_generation=structure database search
    input: unidentified features with accurate mass
    output: putative structures (level 3 identification)
    confidence: low
  Step 6: Integration with plastic-related chemical lists
    tool: custom R script
    algorithm: exact mass + retention time + spectral match filtering
    parameters: rt_window_min=0.1, mass_tol_ppm=5
    input: putative structures, in-house plastic chemical list
    output: plastic-relevant compound annotations
    confidence: low
  Step 7: Pseudo-MRM screening and quantification
    tool: TargetLynx (Waters) or custom Skyline method
    algorithm: transition-based extraction
    parameters: transition_window=±0.1 Da, retention_time_window=±0.2 min, integration_method=peak area
    input: raw LC-HRMS data
    output: quantified concentrations (ng/g)
    confidence: low
