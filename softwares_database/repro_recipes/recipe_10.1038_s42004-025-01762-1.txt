  Step 1: Data conversion

    algorithm: ProteoWizard mzML conversion

    input: raw .raw files (Thermo Orbitrap Exploris 480)
    output: mzML files
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Peptide identification
    tool: Mascot (v2.5)
    algorithm: Database search
    parameters: taxonomy=Mus musculus, precursor_mass_tolerance_ppm=15, fragment_mass_tolerance_ppm=40, fixed_modifications=['carbamidomethyl (C)'], variable_modifications=['oxidation (M)', 'dimethyl (K)', 'dimethyl (N-term)'], max_missed_cleavages=3, enzyme=trypsin, fdr_target=0.01, isotope_error=2
    input: mzML files
    output: peptide identifications (PSMs, peptides, proteins)
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Monosiotopic relative abundance (RA) extraction
    tool: Custom analysis (not specified)
    algorithm: Isotope profile deconvolution and monoisotopic peak integration
    parameters: monoisotopic_peak_selection=highest intensity within expected m/z window
    input: mzML files or Mascot results with raw intensities
    output: time-series monoisotopic RA values per peptide
    confidence: low
  Step 4: Body water deuterium enrichment quantification
    tool: Los Gatos Research Liquid Water Isotope Analyzer
    algorithm: Calibration curve interpolation
    parameters: standard_range_percent_D2O=[0, 12], dilution_factor=300
    input: plasma condensate
    output: p_W (deuterium enrichment in body water, excess of natural abundance)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: Turnover rate modeling (non-duplexed)
    tool: Custom non-linear regression (not specified)
    algorithm: Non-linear least squares fitting of exponential decay model
    parameters: model_equation=I0(t) = I0_asymp + (I0(0) - I0_asymp) * exp(-k*t), I0_asymp_calculation=I0(0) * (1 - p_W / (1 - p_H))^N_EH, p_H=0.000115, fitting_method=non-linear least squares
    input: Time-series monoisotopic RA (I0(t)), p_W, N_EH (hydrogen exchangeable sites)
    output: protein/peptide turnover rate k (day⁻¹)
    confidence: medium
  Step 6: Turnover rate modeling (duplexed dimethyl)
    tool: Custom non-linear regression (not specified)
    algorithm: Non-linear least squares fitting of ratio-based exponential model
    parameters: model_equation=I0(t)/I0(0) = I0_asymp/I0(0) + (1 - I0_asymp/I0(0)) * exp(-k*t), I0_asymp_over_I0_0=(1 - p_W / (1 - p_H))^N_EH, fitting_method=non-linear least squares
    input: Time-series monoisotopic RA ratios (heavy/light dimethyl channels), p_W, N_EH
    output: protein/peptide turnover rate k (day⁻¹)
    confidence: medium
