workflow_steps: 4
  Step 1: Data acquisition
    tool: Thermo Scientific Q Exactive HF hybrid quadrupole-Orbitrap mass spectrometer
    parameters: ionization_mode=negative, resolution=120000, mass_range_mz=[100, 1000], automatic_gain_control_target=1000000.0, maximum_injection_time=100, collision_energy=stepped (10, 20, 40 eV)
    input: cannabis extract samples in methanol/water (50:50 v/v)
    output: raw .raw files
    confidence: high
  Step 2: Data processing and feature detection
    tool: Compound Discoverer 3.1 (v3.1)
    algorithm: peak detection, retention time alignment, compound classification
    parameters: mass_tolerance_ppm=5, retention_time_tolerance_min=0.2, minimum_intensity=10000, grouping_algorithm=nearest neighbor
    input: raw .raw files
    output: feature table (m/z, RT, intensity)
    confidence: medium
  Step 3: Phytocannabinoid-specific spectral matching and annotation
    tool: Compound Discoverer 3.1 with custom phytocannabinoid database (v3.1)
    algorithm: structure-based MS/MS spectral matching
    parameters: database_search_mode=library search, precursor_mass_tolerance_ppm=5, fragment_mass_tolerance_ppm=10, minimum_match_factor=70, retention_time_window_min=0.3
    input: feature table + MS/MS spectra
    output: tentatively identified phytocannabinoids (with structure class, diagnostic fragments, confidence level)
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Manual validation and expert curation
    tool: Compound Discoverer 3.1 + literature review
    algorithm: expert-driven interpretation of MS/MS spectra and retention behavior
    input: tentative identifications + reference standards (where available)
    output: curated list of 121 phytocannabinoids (known and novel)
    confidence: low
