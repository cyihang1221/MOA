    tool: various LC-MS platforms across 12 sites
    algorithm: data-dependent acquisition (DDA) or data-independent acquisition (DIA)

  Step 3: Raw data conversion (ZenoTOF only)
    tool: MSConvert (v3.0.20280)

    output: mzML files

  Step 4: DDA data processing and label-free quantification
    tool: MaxQuant (v2.3.1.0)
    algorithm: LFQ with match-between-runs (MBR)
    parameters: database=custom SwissProt human/yeast/E. coli + contaminants (UniProtKB 2020_03, 31,039 entries), lfq_minimum_ratio_count=2, enzyme=trypsin, missed_cleavages=2, fixed_modifications=['carbamidomethylation of cysteine'], variable_modifications=['methionine oxidation'], fdr_psm_level=0.01, fdr_protein_level=0.01, match_between_runs_enabled=True
    input: raw MS files (DDA mode)
    output: proteinGroups.txt, peptides.txt, evidence.txt (MaxQuant output)
    confidence: high
  Step 5: DIA data processing (library-free)
    tool: DIA-NN (v1.8.1)
    algorithm: library-free DIA analysis
    parameters: database=same as MaxQuant, protease=trypsin, missed_cleavages=1, fixed_modifications=['carbamidomethylation'], variable_modifications_max_count=0, peptide_length_min=7, peptide_length_max=30, precursor_mz_range=[300, 1800], product_mz_range=[200, 1800], quantification_strategy=robust LC (high precision), cross_run_normalization=RT-dependent median-based, ms1_ms2_mass_accuracy_optimization=auto, scan_window_size_optimization=auto, precursor_fdr=0.01
    input: raw MS files (DIA mode)
    output: DIA-NN report (protein, peptide, precursor tables)
    confidence: high
  Step 6: Alternative DIA/DDA processing via FragPipe
    tool: FragPipe (v23.0)
    algorithm: LFQ-MBR (DDA) / DIA_SpecLib_Quant (DIA) / diaPASEF-specific workflow
    parameters: database=same as MaxQuant/DIA-NN (with decoys), precursor_mass_tolerance_ppm=20, fragment_mass_tolerance_ppm=20, missed_cleavages=1, variable_modifications=['methionine oxidation'], fdr_filtering_flag=--prot 0.01, picked_flag=--picked, cross_run_normalization=['disabled for DDA', 'disabled for DIA via --no-norm'], dia_quantification_tool=DIA-NN v1.8.2 beta 8, spectrum_deconvolution=diaTracer (for diaPASEF)
    input: mzML files (DDA/DIA)
    output: FragPipe reports (protein, peptide, PSM tables)
    confidence: high
  Step 7: Downstream quantitative analysis
    tool: custom R/Python scripts (not named)
