paper_title: Plasma metabolomic signature of breastfeeding and risk of cardiometabolic diseases.
source_paper: Birukov A et al. (2025), Nature communications, DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-65977-5, PMID: 41360798
overall_reproducibility_score: 42/100

workflow_steps: 5
  Step 1: Plasma metabolite profiling
    tool: LC-MS
    parameters: platforms=['HILIC-positive', 'HILIC-negative', 'C8-positive'], sample_type=plasma, anticoagulant=EDTA, storage_temperature=['−70 °C', '−80 °C'], processing_delay_limit=2 h (PREDIMED), immediate (WHI, NHS/NHSII), exclusion_criterion=metabolites unstable due to delayed processing
    input: raw plasma samples
    output: untargeted metabolite intensities (181 named metabolites)
    confidence: medium
  Step 2: Missing value imputation
    tool: custom preprocessing
    algorithm: half-minimum imputation
    parameters: imputation_method=half the minimal non-missing value per metabolite, scope=within each case-control study separately
    input: raw metabolite intensity matrix with missing values
    output: imputed metabolite intensity matrix
    confidence: high
  Step 3: Batch effect correction and scaling
    tool: inverse-normal transformation
    algorithm: rank-based inverse normal transformation
    parameters: transformation=inverse-normal, scope=within each case-control study, purpose=['batch correction', 'metabolite range standardization']
    input: imputed metabolite intensity matrix
    output: normalized and scaled metabolite matrix
    confidence: high
  Step 4: Derivation of metabolite-based breastfeeding score
    tool: Elastic net regularized regression
    algorithm: elastic net (L1 + L2 regularization)
    parameters: response_variable=lifetime total duration of breastfeeding (categorical, ordinal-coded), predictors=181 plasma metabolites, cross_validation=used for hyperparameter tuning (alpha, lambda), penalty_mix=elastic net (not specified alpha value), feature_selection=yes (5 metabolites selected)
    input: normalized metabolite matrix + breastfeeding duration categories
    output: weighted metabolite score (5-metabolite linear combination)
    confidence: medium
  Step 5: Association analysis with disease incidence
    tool: Cox proportional hazards regression
    algorithm: multivariable Cox regression
    parameters: outcome=['T2D incidence', 'CVD incidence'], time_scale=person-time from blood draw (baseline) to event or censoring, covariates=['age', 'BMI at age 18', 'parity', 'physical activity (MET-hr/week)', 'AHEI', 'smoking', 'family history of T2D (where available)'], adjustment=for T2D and CVD risk factors; models for CVD additionally adjusted for T2D status, replication=external cohorts (WHI, PREDIMED)
    input: metabolite score + clinical/demographic covariates + time-to-event data
    output: hazard ratios (HR) with 95% confidence intervals
    confidence: high
